## Description

GCCISD is accepting proposals for Cafeteria Serving Lines at Ashbel Smith Elementary. The solicitation covers procurement and installation of cafeteria serving line equipment per drawings provided for the campus. Online submission is preferred; sealed hard-copy responses are also accepted.

## Relevance

Unmatched. This is a procurement for physical cafeteria/food-service serving line equipment and installation at a single elementary school campus. It does not involve IT services, staffing, consulting, program/project management, or any other approved keyword/NAICS code — it is a facilities/equipment purchase, not a services solicitation within the approved scope.
