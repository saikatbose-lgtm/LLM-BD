## Description

DHS seeks non-profit partners to operate borough-based transitional shelters for single adults without other housing options. Services include case management, referrals, and assistance securing permanent housing, implementing DHS's "Turning the Tide" plan. Contract term is five years with a four-year renewal option; funding is "as needed" through the course of the RFP, with startup funding to be negotiated.
