## Description

MontgomeryCounty (the “County”) is requesting proposals from qualified debris removalcontractors (the “Contractor”) to perform disaster-related debris removalservices throughout the San Jacinto watershed. The objective of this project isto remove eligible debris across approximately 135 miles of waterway (509river miles) , including associated tributaries and streambanks, to reduceflood risk and restore natural conveyance. The Contractor will perform debris removal, hauling,reduction, and disposal operations in compliance with all applicable Federal,State, and Local regulations. Work will include debris removal fromwaterways (within navigable waterways stream bed) .
