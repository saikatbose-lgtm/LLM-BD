## Description

The Bryan County Board of Commissioners is soliciting qualified independent certified public accounting firms to submit proposals to audit the County's financial statements for the fiscal year beginning January 1, 2026, and ending December 31, 2026. The County intends to award an initial one-year engagement with the option to renew for four additional one-year terms, subject to satisfactory performance, annual authorization, funding, and agreement on fees. The selected firm will perform the engagement in accordance with applicable professional standards and federal and State of Georgia requirements. The County seeks a firm with recent Georgia local-government audit experience, sufficient staffing, clear communication, and the capacity to complete all work within required deadlines.
