## Description

The California State University is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) to explore opportunities to collaborate with alternative (private) education loan lenders. CSU is gathering information from lenders that serve undergraduate and graduate students (including online and self-support programs) as well as parents of undergraduate students. Our objective is to better understand which lenders offer the most favorable loan terms, flexible repayment options, and high-quality borrower support'particularly in comparison to standard market offerings available to students nationwide. This RFI is issued solely for information-gathering purposes and does not constitute a solicitation, offer, or commitment to enter into a formal partnership.
