## Description

Georgia Institute of Technology requests a proposal for a Smart Parking Guidance Platform & System. The Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia by and on behalf of Georgia Institute of Technology requests proposals for a Smart Parking Guidance Platform & System. The Georgia Institute of Technology reserves the right to establish an agency contract for the needs of the campus based on the award of this solicitation. (Also cross-posted on the Georgia Procurement Registry as PE-50300-RFP-2027-000000358.)

## Relevance

Georgia Tech parking guidance platform and system; the listed NIGP codes are 55091 Vehicle Detectors, 55092 Automated Vehicle Identification Systems and 05535 GPS vehicle tracking, i.e. a parking-detection/guidance hardware system. Although issued through the IT (INTECH) team, the scope is not IT services/staffing work under the approved list.
