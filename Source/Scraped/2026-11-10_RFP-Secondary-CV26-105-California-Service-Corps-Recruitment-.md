## Description

The State seeks a qualified Contractor that can quickly develop and execute a recruitment strategy including an alumni activation plan designed to familiarize key target audiences across the state with the California Service Corps, generate interest from prospective service members and engage new recruitment contacts for high school and college-bound audiences. The Contractor must possess strong project management skills, the ability to manage subcon-tractors (if necessary), have extensive knowledge of reaching targeted grassroots audiences through proven, specific information sources. The Contractor will assist in the coordination, development and implementation of targeted outreach efforts and provide support through audience identification research and audience development, strategic planning and community engagement to support recruitment and member enrollment.
