## Description

UCPS seeks bids for the installation of a seamless resinous flooring system at Piedmont High School Flooring. The project consists of preparing existing polished concrete surfaces and installing either an epoxy flooring system (base bid) or a polyaspartic flooring system (alternate) in designated areas of the school. UCPS prefers installation during the December 19-31, 2026 window, with an alternate installation period of March 26-April 2, 2027. A mandatory site visit is required prior to bid submission.
