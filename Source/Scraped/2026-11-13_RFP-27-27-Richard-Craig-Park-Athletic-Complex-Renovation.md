## Description

Henry County is seeking proposals from qualified contractors to provide a complete turnkey renovation of the Richard Craig Park Athletic Complex for Henry County Parks and Recreation. The project includes renovations to the existing baseball and softball facilities and the conversion of four (4) tennis courts into eight (8) dedicated pickleball courts. The purpose is to modernize the complex and provide safe, accessible, and high-quality recreational facilities for public use, league play, tournaments, and community events. The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the project in accordance with the terms and conditions of this RFP RFPs will be accepted until 3:00 PM local time on Thursday, December 17, 2026. Late RFPs will not be considered nor returned. RFPs submitted by mail, common carrier or delivered in person will be received
