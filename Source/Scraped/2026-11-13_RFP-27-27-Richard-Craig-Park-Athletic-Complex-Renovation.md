## Description

Henry County is seeking proposals from qualified contractors to provide a complete turnkey renovation of the Richard Craig Park Athletic Complex for Henry County Parks and Recreation. The project includes renovations to the existing baseball and softball facilities and the conversion of four (4) tennis courts into eight (8) dedicated pickleball courts. The purpose is to modernize the complex and provide safe, accessible, and high-quality recreational facilities for public use, league play, tournaments, and community events.
