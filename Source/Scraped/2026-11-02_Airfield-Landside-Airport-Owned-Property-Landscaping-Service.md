## Description

The Columbus Airport Commission (Commission) invites qualified firms to submit fixed-price proposals to provide comprehensive mowing, vegetation management, trimming, and associated landscaping services for approximately 300 acres of airfield, landside, and airport-owned properties. The selected contractor must maintain safe operations around aircraft, movement areas, navigational aids, infrastructure, drainage systems, and public facilities. Exhibit A, attached in full, is incorporated as the geographic and technical scope of work.
