## Description

This Request for Proposals (RFP or Sourcing Event) is being issued by the Georgia Department of Natural Resources to establish an agency contract with a qualified supplier who will provide an enterprise email and SMS communications platform to support statewide public communications, marketing, outreach, education, engagement, and customer relationship activities across its divisions, programs, facilities, and locations. This RFP process will be conducted to gather and evaluate responses from suppliers for potential award.
