## Description

MassDEP is publishing this Notice of its intent to make a Best Value Grant Award to the MassCEC to implement and execute the Massachusetts Charging Hub program, in coordination with MassDEP and the Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs. The Charging Hubs Program will enable MassCEC to demonstrate successful and replicable vehicle charging business models through strategic partnerships and siting along key corridors and in high-traffic areas.
