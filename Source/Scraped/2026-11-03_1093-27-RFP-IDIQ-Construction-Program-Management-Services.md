## Description

Georgia Institute of Technology, Office of Planning, Design & Construction is soliciting Qualifications Packages from highly qualified Program Management firms (Consultant) to provide services under an Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity Contract (Solicitation/ Contract No. 1093-27) in Atlanta, Georgia.

## Relevance

Georgia Tech (Office of Planning, Design & Construction) RFQP for IDIQ Program Management firms covering planning/programming, data/analyses and pre-GMP phases of capital projects. NIGP 95877 Project Management Services and 96258 Professional Services support the keyword match, but the scope is construction/capital-program management, so it is only workable for firms with construction program management capability.
