## Description

Items: The Department of Health, Family Health Services Division seeks an entity that can purchase airline tickets and conference registrations and provide travel reimbursement for lodging, meals and incidental expenses, checked bag fees, and ground transportation fees for two oral health conferences in 2027. The goal of this project is to advance oral health education and professional development for dentists and dental hygienists in Hawaii. Vendor must have experience working with oral health stakeholders in Hawaii and the Hawaii Oral Health Coalition.
