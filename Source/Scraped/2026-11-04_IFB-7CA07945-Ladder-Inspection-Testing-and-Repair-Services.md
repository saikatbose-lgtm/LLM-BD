## Description

Contractor shall provide all materials, labor, equipment, tools, permits, taxes, transportation, and fees to ensure all fire service ladders remain compliant with applicable National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) 1932 standards and safe for operational use. Services are necessary so that the Unit is in accordance with the NFPA 1932 standards on Use, Maintenance, and Service Testing of In-Service Fire Department Ground Ladders, manufacturer recommendations, and CAL FIRE requirements.
