## Description

The Contractor/Vendor shall furnish all labor, supervision, equipment, tools, materials, transportation, safety equipment, and incidentals necessary to perform the work. Services may include but are not limited to: • Bush hog mowing and side cutting of utility rights-of-way. • Mowing open areas and easements. • Cutting and removal of tall grass, weeds, brush, vines, timber and brush growing inward and narrowing the utility right-of-way, and light woody vegetation. • Trimming around utility structures, valves, hydrants, manholes, lift stations, electrical equipment, and appurtenances. • Clearing vegetation from drainage ditches and drainage easements as directed. • Removal and disposal of debris generated by mowing operations. • Access path clearing to utility infrastructure.
