## Description

The Lee County Board of Commissioners is requesting qualifications for selection of an Engineering Firm to provide post-award services for a water infrastructure project funded by the FY 2026 Congressionally Directed Spending (CDS)/Community Project Funding (CPF) Grants Program for Water Projects. Post-award services will include, but are not limited to, planning, design, cost estimating, bidding, contracting, construction oversight, quality control, and project closeout.
