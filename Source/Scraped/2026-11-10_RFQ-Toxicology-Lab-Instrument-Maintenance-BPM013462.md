## Description

The County of San Diego is soliciting preventative maintenance and repair services for toxicology laboratory instruments. Commodities listed include laboratory/scientific equipment and professional engineering services categories.

## Relevance

Unmatched: This is hardware/instrument maintenance for lab equipment — an equipment-repair service, not IT services, consulting, or staffing. "Professional engineering services" is listed as a commodity category but is not itself an approved keyword, and the actual scope of work (physical lab instrument maintenance) is not genuinely IT, management, or business-support consulting work.
