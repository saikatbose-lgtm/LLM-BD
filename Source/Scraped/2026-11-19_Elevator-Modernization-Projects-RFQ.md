## Description

REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS
ENGINEERING DESIGN, PROCUREMENT AND CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION SERVICES For Elevator Modernization Projects
Mecklenburg County, North Carolina
Mecklenburg County requests a Statement-of-Qualifications from interested Engineering firms for professional consultant services on two upcoming projects. 
The Firm(s) selected must be licensed in NC, qualified and have experience providing these services in accordance with NC State Building Code, NC Elevator Safety Act, ASME A17.1 Safety Code, NC DOL and Mecklenburg County Code Enforcement Standards.

A copy of the complete RFQ may be obtained by sending an official Notification-of-Interest via email to LaElaina Taylor at email address: LaElaina.Taylor@mecklenburgcountync.gov

Optional Virtual Pre-Submittal meeting will be held on 12/01/26. Submittals must be received by Asset & Facility Management by Thursday, December 17, 2026, at 2:00pm.
