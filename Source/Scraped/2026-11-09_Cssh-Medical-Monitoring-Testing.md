## Description

The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (“DHS”) requires a contractor to perform medical testing for employees at Clarks Summit State Hospital (“CSSH”) located at 1451 Hillside Drive Clarks Summit, PA 18411, for the Respiratory protection program, Asbestos Clearance, Lead Blood Levels and audiometric levels with interpretation. The testing will need to be both on-site and off-site. For those staff not able to be scheduled during the annual on-site testing, the services must be provided off-site. A Respiratory Clearance will need to be supplied by a certified medical physician regarding the employees’ medical suitability to wear a respirator.
