## Description

This Request for Proposals (RFP) is to solicit for data collection, analysis, and documentation Services needed to comply with the National Environmental Policy Act and other federal laws for the Dalles Watershed Project in the Mt. Hood National Forest within Oregon on behalf of the U.S. Forest Service Barlow Ranger District. If interested in submitting a Proposal, please download and review all attachments then return all required documents before RFP Closing date and time.
