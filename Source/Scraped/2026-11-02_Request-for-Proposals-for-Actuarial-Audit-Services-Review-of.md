## Description

EMPLOYEESRETIREMENT SYSTEM OF TEXAS REQUEST FORPROPOSALS FOR ACTUARIAL AUDIT SERVICES (Review ofPension Actuary) SOLICITATIONNO. 26-15352-001 NIGP CODE: 94612and 94620 The Employees Retirement System of Texas (ERS) has issued a Requestfor Proposals (RFP) seeking a qualified firm (Respondent) to provide actuarialaudit services to review the work of ERS’ pension actuary.
