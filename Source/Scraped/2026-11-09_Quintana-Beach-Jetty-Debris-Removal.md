## Description

The Texas General Land Office (“GLO”) issues this Invitation for Bids (“IFB”) to solicit bids for the removal and disposal of debris from the Quintana Beach jetty. Debris associated with a Central Texas flooding event traveled down the Brazos River into coastal waters and subsequently accumulated on the jetty due to tides and currents. The accumulated debris poses a threat to public health and safety and must be safely removed and properly disposed of.
