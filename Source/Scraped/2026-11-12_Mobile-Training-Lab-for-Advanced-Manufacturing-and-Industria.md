## Description

Texas State Technical College (“TSTC”) is seeking proposals from qualified suppliers to provide a new custom-built 53-foot mobile training lab to support workforce training in advanced manufacturing and industrial maintenance. The mobile training lab will be used to deliver transportable, on-site training at company locations, workforce partner sites, and other locations as needed to support economic recovery, resilience, and regional workforce development. This procurement is associated with an Economic Development Administration grant award and is intended to result in a fully functional, turn-key mobile training unit.
