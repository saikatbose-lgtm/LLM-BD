## Description

The City of San Diego (City) operates approximately 163 flow monitoring sites. The major functions for the flow monitoring program are: Event Notification System (ENS), Participating Agency (PA) billing and reporting, and sewer modeling. These functions require real time and periodic data processing, analysis, and reporting of wastewater flows. The Contractor shall provide flow monitoring meter parts, sensors, battery replacements, necessary software, data analysis, reporting, and maintenance services in support of these functions.
