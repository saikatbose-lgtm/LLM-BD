## Description

The purpose of this Invitation for Bids (IFB) is to seek competitive sealed bids from qualified vendors to provide trained Emergency Response Support Personnel to augment and/or perform evaluation of existing staff. These Response Support Personnel will provide supplemental assistance/evaluation in all areas of emergency management as required, to include but not limited to the State Emergency Response Team, State Emergency Operations Center (SEOC), supporting coordination facilities, Logistics and Supply, Operations, Planning and Recovery.
