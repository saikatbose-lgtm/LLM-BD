## Description

The purpose of this solicitation is to solicit proposals from qualified Proposers to support the development of its next Enterprise Strategic Plan that produces a small number of bold, cross-divisional strategic goals. This plan is supported by measurable OKRs and KPIs, a phased implementation roadmap, strategic budgeting alignment, annual business planning tools that will enable the organization to execute on the plan while remaining flexible from year to year, and an organization-wide communications and training plan. Please note: this is substantially similar to previously posted RFP 2026-04, but there are some changes to the documents. If you previously submitted a proposal for RFP 2026-04 and are interested in submitting a proposal for this RFP, please review these documents closely to confirm eligibility.
