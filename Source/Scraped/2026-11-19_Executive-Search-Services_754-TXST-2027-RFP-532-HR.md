## Description

Texas State University seeks qualified, full-service executive search firms to conduct searches for executive positions on an as-needed basis for a five-year contract period. The selected firm(s) shall work with the requesting department, Human Resources, and other University designees to plan and conduct each search from initial consultation through final selection and hiring support.
