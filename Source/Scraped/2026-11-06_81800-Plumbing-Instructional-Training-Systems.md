## Description

This Request for Quotes (RFQ or Sourcing Event) is being issued to establish a contract with a qualified supplier who will meet the State Entity¿s needs as described in this Sourcing Event. Pursuant to the State Purchasing Act (Official Code of Georgia Annotated 50-5-50 et seq.), this electronic Request for Quotes (eRFQ) is being issued by the Technical College System of Georgia (TCSG) on behalf of Coastal Pines Technical College (CPTC) to establish a one-time purchase with one qualified supplier who will provide Instructional Plumbing Training Systems (hereinafter, the State Entity) as further described in this eRFQ. Coastal Pines Technical College is requesting the purchase of one (1) Residential Bathroom Plumbing Trainer and one (1) Water Heater Trainer.  The equipment will be used at the Jesup Campus located at 1777 West Cherry St., Jesup, GA 31545.  Please review the eRFQ instruction document and the bid specification within the attachments section of the GA@Work Marketplace Bid Event for more information about this project. For technical assistance with system registration, accessing the bid even or accessing the bid documents, contact the DOAS Customer Service Help Desk by phone at 404-657-6000 or email at ProcurementHelp@doas.ga.gov, Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, excluding State Holidays or any other day state offices are closed, such as furlough days or closings in response to inclement weather.
