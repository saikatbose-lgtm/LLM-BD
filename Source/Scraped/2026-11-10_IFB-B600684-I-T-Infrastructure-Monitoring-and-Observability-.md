## Description

The Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (MARTA) Office of Technology Infrastructure and Production is soliciting bids for professional services to deploy High Availability (HA) and configure the Server & Application Monitor (SAM) module and a three (3)-year maintenance and support renewal for MARTA's SolarWinds monitoring platform.

## Relevance

MARTA is buying professional services to deploy High Availability and configure the SolarWinds Server & Application Monitor module plus a 3-year maintenance and support renewal for its IT infrastructure monitoring platform. NIGP 92005 (hosting/cloud services) and application-software codes support the match; the work is genuine IT implementation and software support.
