## Description

The Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) seeks qualified Respondents to provide Eligibility Support Services (ESS) to support HHSC’s current eligibility system that offers individuals access to services through a statewide network of eligibility offices, Community Partners, telephone, mail, fax and the internet.
