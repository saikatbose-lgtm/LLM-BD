## Description

Standard (non-set-aside) low-bid component of the Fitzpatrick Hall Readiness Center renovation and addition project at Fort Indiantown Gap. This IFB covers the General (.1, est. $4,000,000-$5,000,000) and HVAC (.2, est. $2,000,000-$3,000,000) construction disciplines; plumbing and electrical are bid separately as a Small Business Reserve contract. Duration is 500 calendar days from the initial job conference. A mandatory pre-bid conference/site walk is scheduled for November 30, 2026. Bids are accepted electronically through e-Builder.
