## Description

The purpose of this solicitation is to solicit proposals from a qualified Proposers to provide telephonic language interpretation services in a culturally and linguistically appropriate manner. Telephonic language interpretation services will be provided for the minimum thirteen (13) Medi-Cal threshold languages, which include Arabic, Armenian, Cambodian, Cantonese, English, Farsi, Hmong, Korean, Mandarin, Russian, Spanish, Tagalog, and Vietnamese. The Contractor shall collaborate with Covered California to achieve Covered California's goal of providing first class customer service with accurate interpretation services. provide telephonic language interpretation services in a culturally and linguistically appropriate manner.
