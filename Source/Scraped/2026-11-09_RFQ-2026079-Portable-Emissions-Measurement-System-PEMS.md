## Description

This procurement is for the Portable Emissions Measurement System. The off-road portable emissions measurement section (ORPEMS) uses portable emissions measurement systems (PEMS) to measure real-world exhaust emissions from the off-road and nonroad sectors, including but not limited to, construction and agricultural machinery, locomotives, cranes, and small off-road engines (SORE), under normal operating conditions. These measurements provide critical data for CARB's compliance, surveillance, inventory, and research programs pursuant to CCR Title 13 and CFR Title 40.
