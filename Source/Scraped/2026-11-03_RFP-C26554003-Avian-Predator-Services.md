## Description

The Oceano Dunes District, State Parks located in San Luis Obispo County requests proposals from qualified firms to provide avian predator specialist services to trap and relocate problem avian predators for the management of Western Snowy Plover (WSP) and California Least Tern (CLT) at Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area (ODSVRA). All work will support the ongoing management of WSP and CLT at ODSVRA during the 2027, 2028, and 2029 breeding seasons (approximately, March 20 to September 10). PROJECT SCOPE OVERVIEW (Please reference the RFP package for detailed purpose and description of services) Contractor will monitor avian predator activity, evaluate the threat of avian predators to nesting WSP and CLT, and determine which individual avian predators pose an unacceptable threat to the nesting success of WSP and CLT at ODSVRA.
