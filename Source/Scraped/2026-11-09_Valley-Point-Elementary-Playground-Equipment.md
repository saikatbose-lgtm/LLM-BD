Whitfield County Schools is seeking proposals from qualified, licensed, and experienced playground equipment contractors to provide all designs, labor, materials, equipment, supervision, permits, inspections, and related services necessary to install playground equipment for 5-12-year-olds at Valley Point Elementary School.
