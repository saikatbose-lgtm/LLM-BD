## Description

The City of Smyrna is requesting proposals from qualified contractors to furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, tools, transportation, permits, testing, protection, cleanup, and incidentals necessary to evaluate, repair, improve drainage, and resurface the pool deck at Aline Wolfe Adult Recreation Center, located at 884 Church Street, Smyrna, GA 30080. The pool will be closed and drained during the project. The City intends to select a contractor that can provide a durable, safe, slip-resistant, and maintainable deck resurfacing system appropriate for a public aquatic facility.
