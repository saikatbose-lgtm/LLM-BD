## Description

The University of North Carolina at Wilmington (UNCW) is seeking a complete turnkey simulation video capture, audio communication, monitoring, recording, management, and playback solution for its Healthcare Clinical Simulation Labs. The awarded contractor shall furnish, install, configure, test, and provide training for an integrated audio/video system that supports simulation-based instruction, assessment, debriefing, and observation.
The solution shall include all required hardware, software licensing, system configuration, project management, installation services, training, and warranty support.
