## Description

The Town of Cortlandt is requesting information on parking enforcement scanning systems and the software used to issue, process, and manage tickets. Items of interest include handheld enforcement scanners, waterproof tickets, photo upload/storage capability, the enforcement software platform, related hardware, licensing, and ongoing maintenance costs, and whether products are available through New York State contracts. The town currently has no parking meters. This is an RFI, not a purchase commitment; a target delivery date of January 1, 2027 is noted. Full notice details are behind the BidNet Direct sign-in.
