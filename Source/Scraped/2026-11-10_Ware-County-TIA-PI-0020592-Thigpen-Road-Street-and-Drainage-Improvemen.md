## Description

Separate, sealed Bids will be received for furnishing all materials, labor, tools, equipment, and incidentals necessary for the installation of street and drainage improvements to Thigpen Road located in Ware County for the county¿s funded Transportation Investment Act (TIA) Project, PI # 0020592 until (11:30 am) (local time), Tuesday, December 15, 2026, at which time bids will be opened and read. The meeting will be held at Ware County Board of Commissioners¿ Office and will be opened to the public. Proposals can be mailed or delivered to the attention of Mr. James Shubert, County Administrator for the Ware County Board of Commissioners, 305 Oak Street Waycross, Georgia 31501. Proposals received after the designated time will not be considered. The Ware County Board of Commissioners may select a successful low bidder after evaluation of all bids. The Scope of Work Includes: Street and Drainage Improvements for a total of 1.61 miles All proposals shall be accompanied by a Bid Bond in favor of the Owner in the amount of at least five percent (5%) of the lump sum bid for the complete work: such Bid Bond representing that the Bidder, if awarded a Contract, will promptly enter into a Contract and furnish Performance Bond and Payment Bonds as provided by the law and approved by the Attorney for the Owner. Performance Bond shall be equal to 100 Percent (100%) of the contract amount and Payment Bond shall be equal to 100 percent (100%) of the Contract amount. The Bid Bond shall be forfeited to the Owner as liquidated damages if the Bidder fails to execute the Contract and provide Performance and Payment Bonds within (10) days after being notified that he has been awarded the Contract. All work on the project must be completed within 270 consecutive calendar days of the Notice to Proceed Date.
