## Description

Hephzibah Town Square is a new 40-acre development in Hephzibah; Georgia located to the south of Georgia Highway 88 and 0.25 miles east of Windsor Spring Road. The project includes the development of a 8,960 SF municipal courthouse and administration facility and 16,300 SF of historically themed commercial space centered on a 1.5-acre town green.
