## Description

The customer service counters at DDS are old and in need of replacement. There are twelve (12) customer service windows that deal with the public for the purpose of obtaining or renewing a driver's license. Each station has cash drawers, transaction counters, computers, etc. Each station will have solid surface countertops and a fixed window separating customers and employees. These workstations are a high volume area and receive a lot of everyday wear and tear. THE WORK WILL HAVE TO BE COMPLETED IN ONE WEEKNED STARTING ON SATURDAY AFTER 12:00 PM. DDS IS CLOSED ON MONDAYS SO WORK MAY CONTINUE BUT THE WHOLE AREA MUST BE 100% OPERATIONAL TUESDAY MORNING. Project includes demolition of twelve (12) existing workstations, countertops, and service windows. Replace all twelve (12) workstations with new millwork, solid surface countertops, and service windows per drawings (attached). All electrical and data are ran through the front wall of the service area and will not have to be altered in any way during this renovation. This is an informal solicitation for Cobb County. Please contact Doug Hendrix with Cobb County Facilities Management (douglas.hendrix@cobbcounty.gov) if you are interested in quoting this project. A performance and payment bond will be required. Contractor must be able to meet Cobb County's General Terms & Conditions and Insurance Requirements as well. These documents will be provided to prospective bidders by Mr. Hendrix.
