## Description

The customer service counters at DDS are old and in need of replacement. There are twelve (12) customer service windows that deal with the public for the purpose of obtaining or renewing a driver's license. Each station has cash drawers, transaction counters, computers, etc. Each station will have solid surface countertops and a fixed window separating customers and employees. These workstations are a high volume area and receive a lot of everyday wear and tear. THE WORK WILL HAVE TO BE COMPLETED IN ONE WEEKNED STARTING ON SATURDAY AFTER 12:00 PM.
