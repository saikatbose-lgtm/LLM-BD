## Description

The City of Atlanta, on behalf of the Department of Watershed Management, will shortly undergo a Professional and Consultant Services procurement process for the selection of qualified firms to provide professional services that include geotechnical engineering and subsurface investigations, environmental site assessments and testing, construction materials testing and laboratory services, topographical and land surveying, geophysical investigations, subsurface utility engineering, mapping, and related engineering and construction inspection services.
