## Description

The City of Atlanta, on behalf of the Department of Watershed Management, will shortly undergo a Professional and Consultant Services procurement process for the selection of qualified firms to provide professional services that include geotechnical engineering and subsurface investigations, environmental site assessments and testing, construction materials testing and laboratory services, topographical and land surveying, geophysical investigations, subsurface utility engineering, mapping, and related engineering and construction inspection services. Interested firms will be evaluated based on the demonstrated professional qualifications and relevant experience of the Proponent¿s proposed team, past performance on projects of similar scope and complexity, and the effectiveness of the proposed management plan and technical approach to successfully execute the required services. The City expects the selected firm to assume full responsibility for providing the Department of Watershed Management (DWM) with all qualified personnel, materials, equipment, resources, and services necessary to successfully execute assigned projects. A Pre-Solicitation Conference will be noticed and held to provide additional information and address questions from the supplier community. Hope to see you there!
