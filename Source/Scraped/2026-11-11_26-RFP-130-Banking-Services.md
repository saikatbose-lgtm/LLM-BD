## Description

Effingham County Board of Commissioners is seeking proposals from qualified financial institutions to provide comprehensive banking services for the County's operating and other designated accounts. Services include depository and cash management services, online banking, ACH and wire transfers, fraud prevention, purchasing cards, payment processing, reporting, and related banking support.
