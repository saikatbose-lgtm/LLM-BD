## Description

KIPP Texas Public Schools is looking for qualified vendors to provide lifecycle replacements and install the following items (or equivalents) across 5 different sites in Dallas Fort Worth, Houston and San Antonio. This should be a turnkey proposal to replace, install, configure, test, label and inventory the specified components. All proposed cabling costs should include wrecking out of the old cable, all terminations/enclosures/bulkheads/patch cables, and all conduit/trenching/boring as necessary.
