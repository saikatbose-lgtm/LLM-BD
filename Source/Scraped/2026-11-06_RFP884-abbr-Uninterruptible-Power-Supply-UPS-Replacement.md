## Description

**Site registration and application approval must be done prior to submitting a proposal.** **All documents must be combined and submitted as one complete proposal.** The Atlanta Regional Commission (ARC) is the regional planning and intergovernmental coordination agency for the metro Atlanta region. The ARC Office of Information Technology provides technology services to internal departments and partner agencies, with a focus on maintaining a secure, reliable, and highly available network infrastructure that protects the Agency's data assets.

Atlanta Regional Commission RFP to furnish, deliver, install and commission a 40 kVA three-phase UPS for its server room. Despite IT-category codes, it is electrical equipment supply/installation, not IT services.
