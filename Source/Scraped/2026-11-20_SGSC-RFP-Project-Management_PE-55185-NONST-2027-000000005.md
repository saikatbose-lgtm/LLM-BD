## Description

Request for proposal. The Project will be to assist the owners in determining the actual additions needed, the proposed size of the additions, the proposed budget to design and construct the additions, to apply for financing, to act as an Owner's Representative in the selection of an architect and general contractor, as well as oversee the development of a construction budget and construction of the additions. The Project Manager will also work with the school and all other site approvals such as state and local fire/safety approvals. The Project Manager will need to start immediately for a term of 18-24 months or until the project is complete. SGSC must be ready for students and staff to occupy the new additions in the fall or winter of the 2028-2029 school year.
