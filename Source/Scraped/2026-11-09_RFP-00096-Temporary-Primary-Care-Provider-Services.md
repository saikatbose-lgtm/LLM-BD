## Description

https://vendors.planetbids.com/portal/26038/bo/bo-detail/148167 California State University, San Bernardino is seeking proposals from qualified medical staffing firms to recruit, screen, credential, insure, place, and administer up to three temporary primary care physicians and/or nurse practitioners for the Student Health Centers at its San Bernardino and Palm Desert campuses. The anticipated Palm Desert assignment is for a nurse practitioner working Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., while services at the San Bernardino campus will be requested on an as-needed basis. Assigned providers must be appropriately licensed, credentialed, and prepared to perform the clinical services specified in the RFP, including medication abortion services, and must comply with all University clinical, privacy, security, and patient-care requirements.
