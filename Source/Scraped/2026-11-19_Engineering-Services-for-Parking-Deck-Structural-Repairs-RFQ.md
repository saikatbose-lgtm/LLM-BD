## Description

REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS - ENGINEERING DESIGN, PROCUREMENT AND CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION SERVICES
for the 4TH STREET PARKING DECK AND McDOWELL PARKING DECK - STRUCTURAL REPAIRS

Mecklenburg County, North Carolina

Mecklenburg County is requesting a Statement-of-Qualifications (SOQ) from qualified Engineering firms for professional services to provide design, procurement and CA services for Structural Repairs at the 4th Street Parking Deck and McDowell Parking Deck. The Firm(s) selected must hold a valid Engineering license in North Carolina, be qualified in designing structural repairs for varying conditions of existing pre-cast parking decks, and have experience providing these services in accordance with ASCE, NC State Building Code, and Mecklenburg County Code Enforcement Standards. 
A copy of the complete RFQ may be obtained by sending an official Notification-of-Interest via email to Joshua Kallam at: Joshua.Kallam@MecklenburgCountyNC.gov

Submittals must be received by Mecklenburg County Asset & Facility Management by Thursday, December 17, 2026, at 2:00pm.

Upon request, this information will be made available in an alternate format for persons with disabilities by calling 980-314-2429.
