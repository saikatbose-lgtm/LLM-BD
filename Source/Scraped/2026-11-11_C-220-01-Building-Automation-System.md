## Description

Newton County Schools is seeking information from qualified building automation system (BAS) manufacturers, controls contractors, system integrators, and technology providers regarding the establishment of a district-wide building automation controls standard. This is a non-binding request for information only. No award will be made based on this RFI. The information provided shall be used for the purpose of education on providers, their capabilities, pricing, and other items that might warrant the issuance of a formal Request for Proposals (RFP) if deemed necessary by Newton County Schools.
