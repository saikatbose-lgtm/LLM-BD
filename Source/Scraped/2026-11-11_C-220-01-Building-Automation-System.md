## Description

Newton County Schools is seeking information from qualified building automation system (BAS) manufacturers, controls contractors, system integrators, and technology providers regarding the establishment of a district-wide building automation controls standard. This is a non-binding request for information only. No award will be made based on this RFI. The information provided shall be used for the purpose of education on providers, their capabilities, pricing, and other items that might warrant the issuance of a formal Request for Proposals (RFP) if deemed necessary by Newton County Schools. There is no guarantee that an RFP will be issued. No firm will be bound by any pricing quoted in response to this Request for Information (RFI). Pricing shall be provided for information and budgetary purposes only. For the complete RFI instructions, please visit https://ncssebid.ionwave.net/.
