## Description

Sealed bids will be received for single prime construction of a new 35,380 square foot educational facility on a greenfield site located in a business and industrial park in Oxford, North Carolina. The facility will be one-story, with masonry and metal panel exterior cladding, and consist of instructional laboratories, classrooms, and related support services.
