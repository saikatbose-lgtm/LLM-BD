## Description

DHS seeks qualified vendors to operate Tier II residences for families with children who have no other housing options, plus an Overnight Facility Program providing lodging (10:00 PM–10:00 AM, 24/7) to families applying for shelter at an intake facility. Tier II residences must provide, at minimum, social services and assistance seeking permanent housing. Contract term is five years with a four-year renewal option; funding and number of awards to be determined by DHS.
