## Description

The Pennsylvania Game Commission (PGC), Northwest Region, requires the purchase of (5) Thermal, 10-point antlered White-tailed Deer decoys in the standing position. Decoy must have thermal signature comparable to a live White-tailed deer and include all necessary accessories to include remote control, battery(s) for thermal system, charger for thermal-system battery(s). Decoy must also have a moveable tail and movable front leg both of which are operated by remote control as well as removable antlers.
