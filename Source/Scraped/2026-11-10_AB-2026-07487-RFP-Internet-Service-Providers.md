## Description

This Request for Proposals ("RFP") is issued by Agriculture Financial Services Corporation (AFSC) to procure internet connectivity service for AFSC branch offices across Alberta. Estimated contract start date is February 15, 2027, with an estimated 3-year duration (through February 14, 2030). Submissions and questions are handled through AFSC's Bonfire portal; the APC posting mirrors it for public visibility.

**Matched.** The solicitation is for ongoing Internet Service Provider (ISP) / internet connectivity services for a government-affiliated organization's branch offices, directly supported by the UNSPSC codes for "Internet services" and "Internet service providers ISP." This falls squarely under the approved keyword "Networking Services" — the scope of work (procuring and maintaining network/internet connectivity for multiple office locations over a 3-year term) is a genuine IT/networking services engagement, not an incidental keyword mention.
