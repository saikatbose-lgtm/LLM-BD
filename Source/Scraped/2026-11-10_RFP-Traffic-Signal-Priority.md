## Description

The City of Laguna Beach intends to deploy a Traffic Signal Priority System, specifically an Emergency Vehicle Preemption (EVP) device solution for the City Emergency and public safety vehicles (which include Fire, Marine, and Police) within signalized intersections throughout the City. The major arterial corridors serving Laguna Beach, specifically State Route 1 (SR-1/Coast Highway), SR-73, and State Route 133 (SR-133/Laguna Canyon Road), are owned and operated by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans).
