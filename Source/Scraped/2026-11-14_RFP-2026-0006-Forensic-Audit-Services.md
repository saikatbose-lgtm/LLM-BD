## Description

The City of Hampton, Georgia (the "City") is requesting proposals from qualified independent firms to provide professional forensic audit services. The City seeks a highly experienced forensic auditing firm to conduct a comprehensive, independent forensic audit of its operational and administrative processes for the last fiscal year. The City of Hampton is a growing community of approximately 9,300 residents located in Henry County, Georgia, within the Atlanta metropolitan area.
