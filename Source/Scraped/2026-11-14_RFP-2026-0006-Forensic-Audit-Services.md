## Description

The City of Hampton, Georgia (the "City") is requesting proposals from qualified independent firms to provide professional forensic audit services. The City seeks a highly experienced forensic auditing firm to conduct a comprehensive, independent forensic audit of its operational and administrative processes for the last fiscal year. The City of Hampton is a growing community of approximately 9,300 residents located in Henry County, Georgia, within the Atlanta metropolitan area. Governed by a Mayor and six-member City Council under a City Manager form of government, the City provides essential services including public safety, public works, recreation, utilities, and administrative functions. In its commitment to transparency, accountability, and fiscal responsibility, the City is initiating this forensic audit to thoroughly review internal controls, policies, and procedures for the last completed fiscal year, 2026. The audit will identify any irregularities, inefficiencies, potential fraud, waste, or abuse, and provide actionable recommendations to strengthen operations and ensure compliance with the City's Purchasing Policies and applicable law.
