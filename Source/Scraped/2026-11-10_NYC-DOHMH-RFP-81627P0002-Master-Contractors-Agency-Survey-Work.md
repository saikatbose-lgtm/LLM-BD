## Description

NYC Health Department seeks on-call master contractors to provide survey research, data collection, and analytical services supporting public health programs, policy development, and evaluation. Selected contractors would help design, administer, and analyze surveys targeting diverse New York City populations. Contract term runs July 1, 2027 through February 28, 2034, with no renewal options. Four addenda have been issued, most recently dated November 5, 2026.
