## Description

The Borough of Garwood is requesting qualifications from firms/individuals for 2027 professional services appointments, covering: Affordable Housing Administrative Agent, Municipal Auditor, Municipal Bond Attorney, Municipal Attorney, Municipal Labor Attorney, Municipal Tax Appeal Attorney, Redevelopment Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Planner, Planning/Zoning Board Engineer, and Municipal Engineer. Contract term: January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** This is the Borough's annual omnibus professional-appointments RFQ covering legal counsel, auditing, engineering, and planning positions. None of the listed positions or scope of work are IT services, IT staffing, data processing, or other categories on the approved keyword list.
