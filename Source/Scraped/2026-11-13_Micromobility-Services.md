## Description

The University of Houston ("University") seeks proposals from qualified firms to provide shared micromobility services, mobility management technology, data analytics, and innovative safety solutions that support a comprehensive campus mobility ecosystem. The University is interested in evaluating both traditional vendor-operated shared micromobility programs and emerging technologies that may improve safety, accessibility, operational efficiency, policy compliance, and data-informed decision making. The University encourages respondents to propose current capabilities, pilot projects, developmental technologies, and future-state solutions that may enhance management of both shared and personally owned micromobility devices. When Responding: University of Houston bid openings will be reviewed andcompleted solely through the JAGGAER Sourcing Event Portal.
