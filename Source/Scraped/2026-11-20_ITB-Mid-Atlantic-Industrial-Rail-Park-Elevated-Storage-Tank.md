## Description

Pursuant to North Carolina General Statutes § 143-129 et. seq. and 2 C.F.R. § 200.320(b)(1), Brunswick County (“County” or “Owner”) is soliciting sealed bids for the construction of:  Mid-Atlantic Industrial Rail Park Elevated Storage Tank. Sealed bids will be received until 2:00 PM ET on TUESDAY, JANUARY 12, 2027, in the Brunswick County Public Utilities Operations Center located at 250 Grey Water Road NE, Supply, NC 28462, at which time they will be opened publicly and read aloud. Bids will be for the furnishing of all labor and materials, including, without limitation, all equipment required, as detailed in the technical specifications, construction drawings, and summary of work contained herein. Any changes to the specifications or summary of work or clarifications to the bidding documents will be made in the form of addendum hereto and will be supplied to all known prospective bidders and posted on the County’s website. Notwithstanding the foregoing, bidders will be responsible for ensuring that they have all addenda. 

Each sealed bid must be made on the forms provided. Brunswick County will not be responsible for the failure of any mail or delivery service to deliver a bid by the aforementioned date and time. Regardless of the manner of submission, bids received after the aforementioned date and time, incomplete bids, or bids not submitted at the correct location or in the designated manner will not be accepted. Sealed bids will be opened publicly and read aloud on TUESDAY, JANUARY 12, 2027 at 2:00 PM ET in the Brunswick County Public Utilities Operations Center located at 250 Grey Water Road NE, Supply, NC 28462. The contract shall be Single Prime.

A general description of the work includes, without limitation: Construction of 1,000,000-gallon composite elevated storage tank, 16-inch water main, and appurtenances.
