## Description

The Oceano Dunes District, California State Parks located in San Luis Obispo County requests proposals from qualified firms to provide Ornithologists or Biologists to conduct bird banding services at the Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area (ODSVRA) for the White Snowy Plover (WSP) and California Least Tern (CLT) throughout the 2027, 2028, and 2029 breeding seasons (approximately, from March 20 to September 10). The endangered avian banding program provides important breeding information and tracking of successful fledging. Please see attached RFP Packet for complete and detailed project specifications, minimum requirements, and proposal application instructions. Previous questions and answers for this RFP is also attached.
