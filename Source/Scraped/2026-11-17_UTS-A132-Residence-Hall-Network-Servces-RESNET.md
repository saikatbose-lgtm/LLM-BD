## Description

TheUniversity of Texas System Supply Chain Alliance will publish a Request for Proposal (RFP)UTS/A132 Residence Hall Network Services (RESNET) on November 17, 2026, through TheUniversity of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center’s Jaggaere-Sourcing tool. This RFP is for the selection of one or more PreferredSuppliers to provide managed residential network infrastructure and internetconnectivity services for campus residence halls and apartments, includingwired and wireless network deployment, 24/7 student technical support, andongoing equipment maintenance and lifecycle management to Alliance Members andAffiliates. We are very interested in your company’s participation and inviteyou to register as a potential supplier. The RFP is posted for download inJaggaer and the RFP documents can be found under Settings & Content /Questions.
