## Description

GTCC seeks proposals for a comprehensive, cloud-based academic operations platform. The solution should support academic scheduling, curriculum management, catalog administration, faculty workload management, enrollment forecasting, event and space scheduling, reporting, and analytics. The objective is to provide a modern, integrated solution that can replace disparate systems, reduce manual processes, improve data accuracy, enhance collaboration, and support informed decision-making. The selected solution should provide workflow automation, reporting and analytics capabilities, and integration with Ellucian Colleague and other commonly used higher education systems.
