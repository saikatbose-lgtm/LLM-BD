In general, the work consists of hazardous materials abatement and interior demolition of the northwest wing of Building F17, including the associated crawl spaces, at CSU Channel Islands, in accordance with the scoping document, exhibits, and specifications prepared by CSUCI. Work includes removal of designated interior finishes, nonstructural walls, ceilings, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing components; abatement of identified hazardous materials, including crawl-space pipe insulation; verification and repair of designated fire barriers; utility coordination; containment, debris removal, sorting, recycling, and lawful disposal.
