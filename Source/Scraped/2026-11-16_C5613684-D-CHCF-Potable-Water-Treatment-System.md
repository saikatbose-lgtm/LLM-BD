## Description

In general, the California Health Care Facility (CHCF) Potable Water Treatment System (PWTS) project will construct a new chlorination boosting system to supplement the disinfection of potable water delivered by the City of Stockton. The project will construct a PWTS consisting of an above ground chemical storage tank, chemical injection system with required electrical, mechanical, metering and control equipment. An approximately 900 square foot pre-engineered metal building, with site work and utilities, will also be constructed to house the chemicals and associated equipment. Integration of the new Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system with the existing SCADA system is also required.
