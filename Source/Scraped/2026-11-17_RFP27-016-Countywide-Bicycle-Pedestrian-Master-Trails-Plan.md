## Description

The Board of Commissioners of Glynn County, Georgia ('the Owner') seeks proposals from interested qualified firms to provide professional planning, analysis, public engagement, and technical services to develop a Countywide Bicycle and Pedestrian Master Trails Plan for the Brunswick Area Transportation Study (BATS) Metropolitan Planning Area, including all the details outlined and required in the Program Services section. This solicitation re-advertises services previously procured under RFP 26-007.
