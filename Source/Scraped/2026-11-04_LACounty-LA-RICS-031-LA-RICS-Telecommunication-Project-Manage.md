## Description

Please take notice, the Los Angeles Regional Interoperable Communications System (LA-RICS) Authority (Authority) is seeking proposals from qualified Consultants to provide comprehensive professional telecommunications project and program management, engineering, technical, construction, real estate, environmental, radio programming, outreach, and administrative support services, among other services, for the Authority's regional public-safety communications program as further described in the RFP.     A Mandatory Proposer's Conference will be held on December 8, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Please refer to Section 8.3 (Mandatory Proposer's Conference) of the RFP for detailed information.
