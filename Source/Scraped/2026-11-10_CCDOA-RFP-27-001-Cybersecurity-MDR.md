## Description

The Clark County Department of Aviation (CCDOA) invites qualified firms to submit proposals to deliver continuous, comprehensive managed security services as a strategic extension of the Department's cybersecurity program. The selected firm will provide 24x7x365 Security Operations Center (SOC) monitoring, Managed Detection and Response (MDR/EDR/XDR), proactive threat hunting, incident response, vulnerability prioritization, and specialized cybersecurity engineering across both IT and OT/ICS airport environments. Operational knowledge of critical infrastructure within the international aviation transportation industry is highly desired. This is a sealed RFP process; proposals and questions must go through the NGEM system (www.ngemnv.com).
