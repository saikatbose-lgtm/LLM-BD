## Description

Poole Road Pump Station Improvements:

1) Increase the pump station capacity by installing three new submersible
pumps within the existing wet well.
2) Other miscellaneous improvements:
i) Addition of fall protection to hatches above the wet well.
ii) Site grading to eliminate ponding.
iii) Yard piping and installation of a new electromagnetic flowmeter.
iv) Installation of a new generator and concrete generator pad.
v) SCADA improvements.
