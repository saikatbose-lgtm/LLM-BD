## Description

Remove existing, damaged roofing material down to substrate for both the roof and wall surfaces damaged by the fire on December 1st, 2026 and replace with in-kind materials as described in the bid documents. The work also includes the replacement of five damaged area drains in the upper roof gutter area. The roof, walls, and gutter area being repaired is roughly 20,000 square feet. The Contractor shall restore the structural integrity of the affected areas to meet all applicable codes, standards, and design requirements. The facility will remain operational during construction. The Contractor shall coordinate all work activities to maintain safe access and minimize impact to daily operations and scheduled events. The selected Contractor shall be responsible for coordinating, securing, and obtaining all necessary permits and approvals for any road closures, lane closures, or traffic control measures required in connection with the Project. All work shall be performed in accordance with the project drawings, specifications, and applicable City of Raleigh standards.
