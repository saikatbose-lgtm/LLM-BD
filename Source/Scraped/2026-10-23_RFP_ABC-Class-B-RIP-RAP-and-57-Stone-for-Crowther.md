## Description

ABC, Class B RIP RAP, and 57 Stone for Crowther Boating Access Area. The Purchase and Intent of this Invitation for Bid (IFB) is to establish a contract with one qualified Vendor to deliver seven hundred (700) tons of Aggregate Base Course (ABC) Stone, three hundred and fifty (350) tons of Class B RIP RAP Stone, and two hundred (200) tons of #57 Stone to 965 Hannah Ferry Rd., Salisbury, NC 28144. The stone is needed for structural support for the Crowther Boat Access Area boat ramp. (Portal: North Carolina eVP; date shown is the portal's posted/start date.)
