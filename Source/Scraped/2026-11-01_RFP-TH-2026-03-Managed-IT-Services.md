## Description

The Town of Tyrone, Georgia, is currently soliciting proposals from qualified Managed Service Providers (MSPs) to provide comprehensive information technology management, technical support, cybersecurity, and related services. The Town does not maintain an internal IT department and is seeking a qualified firm to serve as our primary technology partner. The scope includes end-user support, network and infrastructure management, Microsoft 365 administration, cybersecurity monitoring and protection, website management, and strategic IT planning.

## Relevance

Town of Tyrone seeks a Managed Service Provider to act as its entire IT department: end-user support, network/infrastructure management, Microsoft 365 administration, cybersecurity monitoring, website management and strategic IT planning. NIGP 92037 Networking Services, 92045 Software Maintenance and Support, 92066 System/Network/DBA Administration and 92064 System Implementation directly support the match; this is core managed IT services work.
