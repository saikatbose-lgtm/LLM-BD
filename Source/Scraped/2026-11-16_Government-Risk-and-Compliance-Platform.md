## Description

Central Health seeks a secure, scalable, configurable GRC platform to centralize and strengthen compliance program operations across Central Health, Sendero Health Plans, and CommUnityCare Health Centers. The solution shall replace fragmented manual processes with integrated workflows, documentation, monitoring, corrective action management, and reporting, while supporting applicable healthcare regulatory requirements including governmental healthcare, ACA Qualified Health Plan (QHP), and Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) compliance obligations, as well as Office of Inspector General (OIG) Compliance Program Guidance.
