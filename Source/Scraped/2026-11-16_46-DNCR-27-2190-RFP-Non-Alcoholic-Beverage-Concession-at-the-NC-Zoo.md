## Description

The North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources (the “Department”) seeks to engage a Vendor to provide exclusive non-alcoholic beverage vending and pouring services for the North Carolina Zoo (“NC Zoo”). The selected Vendor will supply all non-alcoholic beverages for sale and distribution across the Zoo’s restaurants, snack stands, mobile units, and vending machines operated by the Zoo’s third-party concessions provider. In addition to beverage provision, the Vendor will underwrite a Signature Sponsorship for the NC Zoo’s annual Boo at the NC Zoo event. The Vendor will also provide in-kind promotional support, including but not limited to branded wraps for recycling and trash receptacles, and promotional signage displayed on delivery vehicles serving the NC Zoo.
