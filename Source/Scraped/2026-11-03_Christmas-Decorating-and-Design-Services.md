## Description

The Little Rock Advertising and Promotion Commission dba Little Rock Convention & Visitors Bureau (LRCVB) is extending a request for proposals for the design, fabrication, and installation of a cohesive, unique set of holiday decorations for the Statehouse Convention Center, River Market, Cromwell Building, and Robinson Center. Contract Term is for two (2) years with option to renew for five (5) additional years. Initial year term will be 13 months from Jan 2027- Jan 2028 to allow time to purchase decorations for during market (January 2027), decorate spaces (November 2027), and remove decorations and plan for 2028 (January 2028). There will be an optional walkthrough on Wednesday November 18, 2026 at 10:30AM CT. We will meet at the lobby of the Statehouse Convention Center: 1 Statehouse Plaza, Little Rock, AR 72201
