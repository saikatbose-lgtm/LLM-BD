## Description

LEGAL NOTICE ADVERTISEMENT FOR PROPOSALS DISTRICT ARCHITECTURAL SERVICES Clarke County School District The Clarke County School District is requesting proposals from qualified architectural firms to provide professional architectural and engineering services for the upcoming planned projects including but not limited to; Early Learning Centers (1 New Build / 1 Renovation), 4 Career Academy Classroom/Lab Buildings (1 New Build / 3 Renovations), 8 Elementary School Additions and Renovations, 3 Gym Renovations, 3 High School Additions and Renovations, 6 District General Service Building Renovations.
