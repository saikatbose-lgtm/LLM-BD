## Description

DGS is seeking a contractor to create, develop, implement, and administer a statewide Job Order Contracting (JOC) Program and provide construction management services, including an internet-based JOC management/support system and a Unit Price Book (UPB) with technical specifications derived from commercial unit-price data and tailored to Commonwealth historical data. The goal is to let Commonwealth agencies procure design-build contractors for minor construction projects via Job Orders. Contract duration is 2 years with three optional 1-year renewals. Submission is electronic via the JAGGAER portal.

## Relevance

Unmatched: although the RFP uses the words "manage" and "management services" and calls for an internet-based support system, the actual solicited work is administration of a construction Job Order Contracting program — i.e., a construction-industry program/contract administrator role built around unit-price construction procurement, not generic IT services, staffing, or professional/business management consulting. The "internet-based system" is a minor supporting tool within a construction-management engagement, not the subject of the procurement, so it does not genuinely fall under "Project Management," "Management Services," or any IT-related keyword as intended by the approved list.
