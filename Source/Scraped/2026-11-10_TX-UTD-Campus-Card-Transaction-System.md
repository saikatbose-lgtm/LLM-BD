## Description

The University of Texas at Dallas (UTD) is soliciting proposals from qualified and experienced respondents to provide a comprehensive campus-wide card management solution to replace the University's existing Comet Card platform. The selected solution will support identification, payment, access, and transaction services for students, faculty, staff, and authorized campus users. Contract term: initial 3-year term with two 1-year renewal options (max 5 years). Bid response via https://utdallas.bonfirehub.com/projects/260299/details.
