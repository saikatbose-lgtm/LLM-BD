## Description

The City of Lawrenceville is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified contractors for the GRAYSON HWY (ROSEBUD RD TO SUGARLOAF PKWY). Sealed bids will be received by the Finance Office, at Lawrenceville City Hall ¿ Level 2, Room 238 (Business License and Taxes) - 70 S. Clayton Street, Lawrenceville, Georgia 30046 until 2:00 P.M. local time on Thursday, December 10, 2026 and then publicly opened and read aloud. Any bid received after 2:00 P.M. will not be accepted. Bid envelope should be marked on the outside with Bid Number, name of Bidder, date and time of opening and utility contractor¿s license number. Sealed envelopes must be timestamped as received by Finance to be considered as received. One unbound original and one (1) copy should be submitted. The work to be performed by the Contractor consists of but is not limited to the following: This project will consist of relocating and/or lowering approximately 2,000 feet of 8-inch Steel, approximately 1,980 feet of 6-inch PE and installing approximately 200ft of 2-inch PE. *Note: These locations and quantities are subject to change but will be of similar scope. This will be a line-item bid with a not to exceed total. A pre-bid conference is scheduled for 10:00 A.M. on Monday, November 16, 2026, at Lawrenceville City Hall located at 70 South Clayton Street, 3rd Level (Floor) Large Conference Room, Lawrenceville, GA. 30046. Questions regarding bids should be submitted in writing to Kenneth Morris, Purchasing Manager, via email at purchasing@lawrencevillega.org no later than 3:00 p.m. local time, November 27, 2026. Bids are legal and binding upon the bidder when submitted.
