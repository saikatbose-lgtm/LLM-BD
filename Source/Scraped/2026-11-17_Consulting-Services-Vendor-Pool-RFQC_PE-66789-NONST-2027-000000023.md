## Description

Invest Atlanta issues this Request for Qualifications (RFQ) to establish a pre-qualified Vendor Pool of consulting firms capable of supporting the important effort of planning for and implementing a better future across the City of Atlanta. This solicitation does not seek to provide services for a specific project or engagement. Its purpose is to identify, evaluate, and qualify a roster of firms that Invest Atlanta can engage ' individually or through coordinated teams ' as consulting needs arise across all of its portfolio, initiatives, and programs over the pool term. Qualification in the Vendor Pool does not guarantee the award of work. Task orders will be issued on an as-needed basis through direct assignment or mini-competition among pool members, consistent with Invest Atlanta's procurement policies and the task order process described in Section 8.
