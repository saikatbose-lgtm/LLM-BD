## Description

The Uni versity of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center (MD Anderson) is seeking a qualified contractor to provide janitorial services for MD Anderson owned parking facilities within the Texas Medical Center in Houston, Texas. The selected contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, tools, equipment, necessary supplies and materials necessary to perform the janitorial services and items in all included areas specified in this Scope of Work. These areas include seven (7) parking garages totaling 10,483 parking spaces, nine (9) surface parking lots totaling 2,272 parking spaces, office space, restrooms, elevator cabs and elevator lobbies.
