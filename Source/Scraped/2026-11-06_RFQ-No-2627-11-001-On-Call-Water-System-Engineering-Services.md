## Description

Sunrise Water Authority requests Statements of Qualifications from qualified professional engineering firms to provide on-call water system engineering and related services.  Services include, but are not limited to, planning and hydraulic modeling; pipelines; pump stations; water storage reservoirs; wells, including aquifer storage and recovery (ASR) wells; construction management and bid support; program management and owner's representation; and related services.
