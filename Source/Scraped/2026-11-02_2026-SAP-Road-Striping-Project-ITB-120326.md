## Description

The Lee County Board of Commissioners is accepting sealed written bids from qualified contractors for road striping services for the following 25 roads in Lee County: Smithville Road from Leesburg City Limits to S.R. 118, Pinewood Road from S.R. 32 to U.S. 19, New York Road from Lee County Line to S.R. 195, Philema Road from New York Road to S.R. 32, Old Stage Road from S.R. 32 to end of pavement, Graves Spring Road from S.R. 32 to Forrester Parkway, Garrett Road from Graves Spring Road to Philema Road, White Pond Road from Philema Road to Hattie Bell Road, Thundering Springs Road from Philema Road to White Pond Road, Miller Road from White Pond Road to Stocks Dairy Road, Flowing Well Road from White Pond Road to Stocks Dairy Road, Lovers Lane Road from S.R. 32 to Dougherty County Line, Mayhaw Road from U.S. 19 to Lovers Lane Road, Armena Road from S.R. 32 to U.S. 82, Palmyra Road from S.R. 32 to Fussell Road, James Pond Road from S.R. 32 to Palmyra Road, Oakland Road from U.S. 82 to Palmyra Road, Kinchafoonee Creek Road from Creekside Drive to U.S. 19, Haley Drive from S.R. 133 to Lovers Lane Road, Marlow Lane from West Doublegate Drive to U.S. 82, N Doublegate Drive from U.S. 82 to the Dougherty county Line, Winnifred Road from Cookville Road to U.S. 82, Hugh Road from Ledo Road to Fussell Road, Capstone Connector from Ledo Road to Fussell Road, and Century Road from Palmyra Road to U.S. 19. In general, these roads are to be restriped with either high build or thermoplastic paint as called for in the Bid Form. Striping is to match the existing striping unless otherwise noted on the bid form. Other included items are traffic control, rumble strips, stop bars, and other misc. items pertaining to the project.
