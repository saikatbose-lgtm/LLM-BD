## Description

NYSIF intends to procure NetScout nGeniusONE and InfiniStreamNG under its discretionary purchasing authority (State Finance Law 163(6)); limited to NYS-certified MWBE or SDVOB small businesses; contract value not to exceed $1,500,000. NYSIF is seeking a one-year agreement; bidders must be authorized to sell all NetScout products listed in the Appendix Z fee schedule. Responses to inquiries to be posted November 24, 2026.
