## Description

Small Business Reserve component of the Fitzpatrick Hall Readiness Center renovation and addition project at Fort Indiantown Gap. This IFB covers the Plumbing (.3, est. $700,000-$800,000) and Electrical (.4, est. $1,000,000-$2,000,000) construction disciplines, open only to DGS-certified small businesses. Duration is 500 calendar days from the initial job conference. A mandatory pre-bid conference/site walk is scheduled for November 30, 2026. Bids are accepted electronically through e-Builder.
