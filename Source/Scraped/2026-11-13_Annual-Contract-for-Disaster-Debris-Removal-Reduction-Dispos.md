## Description

Tarrant County seeks proposals from qualified service providers to monitor reduction and disposal of disaster debris activities completed by Tarrant County contracted vendors. Debris will primarily be in Tarrant County right-of-way (ROW) areas. ROW areas are defined portions of land over which Tarrant County has allowed passage to citizens. Some examples of ROW areas include, but is not limited to, facilities such as roads, railroads, and waterways. ROW areas include all land surrounding the facility itself such as road shoulders. 1. CONTRACT TERMS: Vendor(s) will be awarded a twelve (12) month contract, effective from the date of award or notice to proceed as determined by the Tarrant County Purchasing Department. At Tarrant County’s option and approval by the Vendor, the contract may be renewed for two (2) additional twelve (12) month period(s), as further explained in Renewal Options.
