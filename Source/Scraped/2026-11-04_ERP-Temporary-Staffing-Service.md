## Description

Temporary staff augmentation services to supplement the City’s workforce by providing qualified, on-site Salaried Professional personnel to support day-to-day Finance operations (Payroll, Accounts Payable, Revenue, Procurement, and Accounting) while the City prepares to replace its current Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system with a modernized ERP platform. These are on-site positions only; remote or hybrid work arrangements are not available under this engagement.
