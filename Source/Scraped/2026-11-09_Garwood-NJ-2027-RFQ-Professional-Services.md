## Description

The Borough of Garwood is soliciting Statements of Qualifications for 2027 professional services contracts, specifically: Affordable Housing Administrative Agent, Municipal Auditor, Municipal Bond Attorney, Municipal Attorney, Municipal Labor Attorney, Municipal Tax Appeal Attorney, Redevelopment Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Planner, Planning/Zoning Board Engineer, and Municipal Engineer. The RFQ is conducted under New Jersey's "fair and open process" (N.J.S.A. 19:44A-20.4 et seq.); professional services are otherwise exempt from bidding under N.J.S.A. 40A:11-5(1)(a)(i). Contract term is January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027. Responses are due no later than 11:00 AM on December 11, 2026, addressed to the Borough Clerk.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** This RFQ solicits municipal legal counsel (several attorney categories), auditing, bonding, planning/zoning board professional services, and municipal engineering — all licensed-professional positions (attorneys, auditor, engineer, planner) for a small New Jersey borough. None of these are IT services, staffing/staff augmentation, IT/program/project management, data processing, networking, clerical/administrative support, or business/management consulting as defined by the approved keyword list. While keyword #13 ("Professional, Consulting, Administrative and Management Support Services") contains the word "Professional," the actual scope here is statutory municipal legal/auditing/engineering appointments, not administrative or management support consulting work — so it is judged out of scope rather than a genuine match.
