## Description

The City of Fairburn seeks to identify a qualified consultant or consulting team to create a comprehensive safety action plan with the goal of preventing roadway fatalities and serious injuries throughout the City of Fairburn. This includes improving safety for all roadway users, including pedestrians, cyclists, motorists and public transportation. This plan will help the city reach its goal of achieving zero traffic fatalities and severe injuries.
