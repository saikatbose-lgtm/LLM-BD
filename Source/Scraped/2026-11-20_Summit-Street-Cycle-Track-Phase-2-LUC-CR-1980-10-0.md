## Description

This project will reconstruct 1 mile of pavement on Summit Street from New York Avenue to Manhattan Boulevard. This includes removing and replacing the pavement, curb, and the drive approaches, as well as minor drainage improvements. This project will also include a protected cycle track from New York Avenue to Manhattan Boulevard and the replacement of approximately 1 mile of existing water main with new 8", 12", and 16" water main.
