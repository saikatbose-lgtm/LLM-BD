## Description

The Metropolitan Water District of Southern California (Metropolitan) is requesting bids to furnish and deliver fifteen (15) new, unused Altec Air Dehydrators with Wall Mount Kits for Metropolitan's microwave network system. The dehydrators will be used to facilitate field use in remote sites in both Desert and Western Region in support of Metropolitan's microwave system. This is a sole-brand procurement.
