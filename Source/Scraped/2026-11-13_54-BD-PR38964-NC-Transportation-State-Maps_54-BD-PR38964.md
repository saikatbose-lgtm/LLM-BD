## Description

The purpose of this Invitation for Bid (IFB) is to obtain pricing and award an Agency Specific Term Contract (ASTC) for the purpose of providing 2027-2028 North Carolina Transportation Maps for the North Carolina Department of Transportation (NCDOT). These maps will be distributed to rest areas and welcome centers across the State as close to January 1, 2027, as possible.
