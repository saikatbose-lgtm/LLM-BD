## Description

The intent of this project is to furnish and install all materials, labor, and services necessary to remove and replace the existing roofing systems on Roof Sections 2, 3, 4, and 5 at Coastal Empire Montessori Charter School. The existing roofing systems have reached or are nearing the end of their expected service life and are exhibiting active leaks. Work includes, but is not limited to, tear-off of existing roofing materials, deck and insulation preparation, installation of new insulation and membrane roofing, flashing, drainage, skylights, and all related components described below.
