## Description

The City of Lawrenceville is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified suppliers for the Purchase of Gas Pipe and Materials- GRAYSON HWY (ROSEBUD RD TO SUGARLOAF PKWY) for the Lawrenceville Gas Department. Sealed Bids should be typed or submitted in ink and returned in a sealed container marked on the outside with the SB#, Company Name, date and time of opening. Bids will be received until 2:00 P.M. local time on Thursday, December 10, 2026 by the Finance Office, at Lawrenceville City Hall ¿ Level 2, Room 238 (Business License and Taxes) - 70 S. Clayton Street, Lawrenceville, Georgia 30046. Any bid received after this date and time will not be accepted. Bids will be publicly opened and read at 2:00 P.M. Sealed envelopes must be timestamped as received by Finance to be considered as received. One unbound original and one (1) copy should be submitted. Questions regarding bids should be directed to Kenneth Morris, Purchasing Director, in writing at purchasing@lawrencevillega.org, no later than 3:00 P.M. on November 27, 2026. Bids are legal and binding upon the bidder when submitted.
