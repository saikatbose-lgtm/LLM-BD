## Description

HRA seeks qualified organizations to provide tenant services for seniors in newly developed affordable senior housing, including a set-aside for formerly homeless seniors, to help residents live independently and age in place. Services include engagement opportunities for all residents and light-touch case assistance for seniors with histories of homelessness. Contract term is approximately five years with an option to renew for four years; start date of each contract depends on completion of building construction; funding is "As Needed" through the course of the RFP.
