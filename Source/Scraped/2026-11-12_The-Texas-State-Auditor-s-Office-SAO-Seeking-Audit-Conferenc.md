## Description

The Texas State Auditor's Office (SAO) is seeking speakers for the SAO 2027 Audit Conference. Statement of Need The purpose of this request for proposal/call for conference speakers is to solicit proposals from qualified training providers who can develop and deliver in-person conference sessions designed to support the skills and knowledge of government audit professionals. Intended Audience The State Auditor's Office may provide fee-based audit training courses for state agencies (Internal Auditing Act, Government Code, Sec. 2102.012).

## Relevance

Call for audit conference speakers/training providers — training delivery, not IT/staffing/consulting engagement.
