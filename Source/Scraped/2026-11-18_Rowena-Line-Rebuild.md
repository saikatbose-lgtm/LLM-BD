## Description

a. Northern Wasco County People's Utility District (hereinafter called "District") issues this ITB soliciting the Rowena Line Rebuild. Northern Wasco County Public Utility District (NWCPUD) owns and operates an electric distribution system serving from The Dalles to Rowena. Portions of the existing 12.47 kV distribution system along Highway 30 and cross county have reached the end of their service life and require replacement to maintain system reliability and safety.
