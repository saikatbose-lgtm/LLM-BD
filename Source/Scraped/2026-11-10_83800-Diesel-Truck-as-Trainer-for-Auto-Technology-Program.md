## Description

Pursuant to the State Purchasing Act (Official Code of Georgia Annotated §§50-5-50 et seq.), this electronic Request for Quotes (eRFQ) is being issued by the Technical College System of Georgia (TCSG) on behalf of North Georgia Technical College to establish a one-time purchase with one qualified supplier who will provide a diesel truck intended to be used for training in the Automotive Technology Program  (hereinafter, the State Entity) as further described in this eRFQ. North Georgia Technical College is requesting proposals for a diesel truck to be used exclusively for training in its Automotive Technology Program. The truck will provide students with hands-on experience working with real-world equipment.
