## Description

The City of Lexington, North Carolina is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from qualified Design-Build teams to provide progressive design-build services for the NC I-85 Center Pump Station and Sewer Extension. The selected DB team will provide integrated design, permitting, preconstruction, procurement, construction, startup, commissioning, and closeout services. Work includes a new submersible sewer pump station with backup power, gravity sewer, aerial sewer crossing, force main, jack-and-bore work and associated site/utility work.

## Relevance

Design-build sewer/pump station construction (NAICS 237110); not IT, staffing or management consulting.
