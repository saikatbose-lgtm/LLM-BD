## Description

Sealed bids will be received for single prime construction of a new 35,380 square foot educational facility on a greenfield site located in a business and industrial park in Oxford, North Carolina. The facility will be one-story, with masonry and metal panel exterior cladding, and consist of instructional laboratories, classrooms, and related support services. Mandatory conference/site visit: 12/2/2026. Special instructions: A mandatory pre-bid meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, December 2, 2026.
