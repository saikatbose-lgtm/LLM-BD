## Description

Rockdale County, Georgia, is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from experienced and licensed general contractors interested in performing residential rehabilitation and repair services through the County's proposed Repair & Revitalize Program. The program is intended to assist eligible owner-occupied households with necessary housing repairs and improvements addressing substandard housing conditions, health and safety concerns, structural deficiencies, accessibility needs, and applicable building code requirements. The County anticipates applying for funding through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP), with an anticipated rehabilitation program serving approximately 10 to 15 owner-occupied homes. The purpose of this RFQ is to identify and establish a pool of qualified residential rehabilitation contractors with the experience, licensing, insurance, technical capabilities, and capacity to perform the types of repairs anticipated under the program. Contractors selected through this RFQ may be invited to submit bids or proposals for individual rehabilitation projects or groups of properties, subject to applicable County procurement requirements and available funding. Rehabilitation activities funded through the program shall comply with applicable federal labor standards, including the Davis-Bacon Act, the Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards Act (CWHSSA), the Copeland Anti-Kickback Act, and applicable implementing regulations, as required by the HOME program. Prequalification through this RFQ does not constitute a contract award, guarantee a minimum number of projects, or guarantee future work.
