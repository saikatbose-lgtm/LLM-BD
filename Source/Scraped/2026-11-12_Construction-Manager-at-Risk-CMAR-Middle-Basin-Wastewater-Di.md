## Description

This is a Construction Management at Risk (CMAR) services procurement. Johnson County Wastewater (JCW) is seeking a statement of qualifications (SOQ) from firms to provide CMAR services for the Middle Basin Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF) Digester & Solids Improvements (“Project”), Contract No. IMB1-C040. This Request for Qualifications (RFQ) is the first phase of a three-phase procurement process for selection of a Construction Manager. Phase I shall be the solicitation of an SOQ and prequalifying a shortlist of firms to advance to Phase II. Phase II shall be the solicitation of a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a more in-depth response from Phase I shortlisted firms, and Phase III shall include an interview with each firm to present their proposed team members, qualifications, project plan, and answer questions.
