## Description

112T37CM The Project is located in San Diego County on Interstate 805 in the Cities of San Diego, Chula Vista, and National City from East Palomar Street to the Junction I-5/I-805 Separation and on I-5 in the Cities of San Diego, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, and Oceanside from the Junction 5/805 Separation to Harbor Drive. The Project is a set of defined work packages that will modernize existing roadway infrastructure along the I-5 and I-805 corridors to create over 40 miles of continuous Express Lanes. The work packages will construct the necessary pavement striping, signage, traffic safety elements, paving, and electrical items in coordination with SANDAG's TSI contract. A more detailed description of the Project is contained in Appendix A.
