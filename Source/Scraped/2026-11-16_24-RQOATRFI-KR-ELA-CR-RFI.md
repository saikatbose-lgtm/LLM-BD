## Description

The State of North Carolina is in the process of developing plans to score constructed response items on the End-of-Grade (EOG) English Language Arts tests in grades 6–8 and the End-of-Course (EOC) English II test as part of the North Carolina Testing Program starting in 2027–28 (G.S. § 115C-174.11). State assessments are administered using two main testing windows that according to State statute shall occur during the last five days of the semester for high school courses or last ten days for yearlong and end-of-grade courses.
