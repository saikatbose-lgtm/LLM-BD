## Description

The State of North Carolina is in the process of developing plans to score constructed response items on the End-of-Grade (EOG) English Language Arts tests in grades 6–8 and the End-of-Course (EOC) English II test as part of the North Carolina Testing Program starting in 2027–28 (G.S. § 115C-174.11). State assessments are administered using two main testing windows that according to State statute shall occur during the last five days of the semester for high school courses or last ten days for yearlong and end-of-grade courses. See Table 1 for testing windows and estimated student counts by grade level and course. The current business rule for score turnaround from these constructed response items is a maximum of 5 business days upon receipt of responses by Vendor. This aligns with the State's long-established practice of testing and reporting model to have final scores back to schools within one week of test administration. Student responses will be provided electronically to the scoring Vendor and scored on a rolling basis as files are received from local school districts based on their respective testing schedules. The peak scoring periods are usually January for the fall window and May through June for the spring window. Scoring services will support spring End-of-Grade testing and fall and spring End-of-Course testing.
