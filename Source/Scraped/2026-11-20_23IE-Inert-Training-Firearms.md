## Description

The Pennsylvania Game Commission requires the services of a supplier to provide inert training firearms for use as teaching aids in PA Basic Hunter-Trapper Education classes. Included in the Hunter-Trapper Education budget are training aids associated with the newly revised curriculum. This curriculum must meet the standards and learning objectives set by the International Hunter Education Association (IHEA) to maintain reciprocity in other states. The IHEA standards require instruction on the five most common types of firearm actions, including loading and unloading ammunition.
