## Description

The Cobb County School District (CCSD) is soliciting proposals from properly licensed/qualified vendors to provide Bus Repair Services for the district on an as-needed basis. Projects will be awarded by the issuance of individual purchase orders and may be awarded to multiple vendors.

Cobb County Board Of Education non-IT services/special project solicitation: "Bus Repair Time and Materials". After reviewing the description and codes (55705 Air Conditioning, Heating and Ventilation, 55710 Axles, Including Suspension, Springs, Shocks, Struts, etc., 55712 Bicycle Racks for Transit Buses), the scope is not IT services, IT staffing, staffing/employment, administrative/clerical support, data processing or management-consulting work under the approved keyword list.
