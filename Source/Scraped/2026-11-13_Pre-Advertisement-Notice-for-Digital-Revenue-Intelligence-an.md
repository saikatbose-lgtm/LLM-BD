## Description

The City of Atlanta on behalf of the Department of Aviation will shortly undergo a Multi-Step Sealed Bid process comprised of a prequalification phase as the initial step, followed by an Invitation for Bid (IFB) towards the procurement of an integrated, airport-wide solution that automates the real-time collection and analysis of line-item sales data from all airport concessionaires. The platform is intended to enhance revenue accuracy, transparency, and operational efficiency by supporting automated rent validation, menu compliance, audit workflows, and centralized reporting. [Status note: posted as an advance notice; the formal solicitation is to follow.]

## Relevance

City of Atlanta (Dept. of Aviation) pre-advertisement for a multi-step bid (prequalification then IFB) for an airport-wide software platform automating real-time sales-data collection, analysis, rent validation, audit workflows and reporting.
