## Description

The North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources, Division of State Parks, (hereinafter, “Department”), requires a Vendor to provide septic tank pumping and disposal services for Mount Mitchell State Park (hereinafter, “Park”). The Vendor shall provide all necessary materials, tools, and equipment.Mount Mitchell State Park is located at 2388 NC-128, Burnsville, NC 28714. The entrance to the park is off the Blue Ridge Parkway. The Blue Ridge Parkway is closed north of the park. Access to the park is from the Asheville side of the Blue Ridge Parkway only.
