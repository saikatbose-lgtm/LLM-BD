## Description

The City of Sylvania is soliciting sealed bids from qualified contractors for the replacement of approximately 3.9 miles of existing overhead copper electrical conductor with 1 AWG aluminum wire and the replacement of approximately 95 failing electrical distribution poles, together with associated electrical distribution hardware upgrades. The project is intended to improve the reliability and resilience of the City of Sylvania's electrical distribution system.
