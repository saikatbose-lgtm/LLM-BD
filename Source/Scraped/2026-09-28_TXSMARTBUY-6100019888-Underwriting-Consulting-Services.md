## Description

The Neighborhood & Housing Services Department (NHSD) is requesting proposals from qualified individuals or firms to provide on-call financial consultation and credit underwriting services for the selection and negotiation of affordable housing and economic development projects funded with CDBG, HOME, General Fund, Housing Bond, LHRD and EDI. The selected consultant will conduct financial review services for project underwriting, subsidy layering and financial gap analysis, and recommend ways to ensure compliance with federal fund regulations.
