## Description

The General Contractor shall supply all labor, equipment, materials, and supervision necessary to construct a new park in accordance with the provided drawing and specifications. The project scope encompasses site demolition, clearing, grading, construction of facilities, poured in place safety surfacing, play structure assemblies, splash pad equipment and plumbing, mountain biking trails, concrete paths, parking lots, site lighting, site furnishings, and landscaping improvements.
