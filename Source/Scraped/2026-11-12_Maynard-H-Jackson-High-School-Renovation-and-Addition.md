## Description

BID SOLICITATION Balfour Beatty (Construction Manager) is accepting emailed budget proposals from trade partners for scope associated with Division 2 through Division 32 for the Maynard H. Jackson High School Renovation & Addition (Atlanta Public Schools) project. This is the DD (Design Documents) Budget round, and awards will be via a future drawing release. Budget proposals are to be received no later than 2 PM on Thursday, December 10th, 2026, at bbcatlantabids@balfourbeattyus.com. To accommodate the project schedule, the following trades will potentially be considered as part of an early release package: Steel, Elevators, Mechanical, Electrical, Sitework, Ground Improvements, and Shoring. These trades will be considered bids, not budgets. You must make and clarify any assumptions that are necessary to provide a complete bid. Please note that APS (Atlanta Public Schools) reserves the right to revisit bid packages that may be considered for the early release package at GMP/future pricing round. Bidders may request information about this project and receive access to electronic bid package documents by sending an email to bbcatlantabids@balfourbeattyus.com or attending the Virtual Information Session. Please be advised that it is the policy of Balfour Beatty that all contracts be awarded on a strictly non-discriminatory basis without regard to race, gender, or ethnicity of the trade contractor. Balfour Beatty reserves the right to accept or reject any or all budget proposals and to waive minor irregularities. Budget proposals received after the specified time will not be considered.
