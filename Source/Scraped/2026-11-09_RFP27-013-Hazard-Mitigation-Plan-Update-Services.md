## Description

Glynn County, Georgia is seeking proposals from qualified firms to provide professional services for the update and development of the County's Hazard Mitigation Plan (HMP). The updated plan will serve as a multi-jurisdictional planning document intended to identify hazards, assess risks and vulnerabilities, and develop practical mitigation strategies that reduce long-term impacts to residents, infrastructure, critical facilities, businesses, and government operations.
