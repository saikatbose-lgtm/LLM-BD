## Description

Glynn County, Georgia is seeking proposals from qualified firms to provide professional services for the update and development of the County¿s Hazard Mitigation Plan (HMP). The updated plan will serve as a multi-jurisdictional planning document intended to identify hazards, assess risks and vulnerabilities, and develop practical mitigation strategies that reduce long-term impacts to residents, infrastructure, critical facilities, businesses, and government operations. The project must comply with all applicable FEMA and Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency (GEMA/HS) requirements to maintain eligibility for future hazard mitigation funding opportunities. The County is seeking a consultant with demonstrated experience in hazard mitigation planning, coastal resilience, GIS-based risk analysis, stakeholder coordination, and FEMA plan approval processes.
