## Description

The North East Texas Workforce Development Board (hereinafter referred to as “Board”), d.b.a. Workforce Solutions Northeast Texas, a501(c)3 not-for-profit, tax-exempt organization, in compliance with Texas lawsSB 642 and HB 1863, is the local policy making body for the Northeast TexasWorkforce Development Area (WDA).   TheBoard is comprised of a 27-member board approved by the Texas WorkforceCommission. This regional, policy-making board is made up of a majority ofbusiness persons and also has representation from labor, education and economicdevelopment concerns to plan and oversee operation of the public workforcesystem, of which child care services is a part, in the 9-county local workforcearea. The Board provides policy and program guidance and exercises independentoversight and evaluation of workforce development programs and services thataffect area employers, residents and job seekers in Bowie, Cass, Delta,Franklin, Hopkins, Lamar, Morris, Red River, and Titus counties.
