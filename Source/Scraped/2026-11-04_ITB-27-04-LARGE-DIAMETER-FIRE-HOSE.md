## Description

Butts County Board of Commissioners and Butts County Fire & Emergency Services (BCFES) are seeking sealed bids from qualified manufacturers, distributors, or authorized dealers for seventy-five (75) new sections of 5-inch Large Diameter Hose (LDH), each 100 feet in length, equipped with Storz couplings. The total hose purchase will equal 7,500 linear feet. The intent of this solicitation is to obtain durable, service-ready LDH suitable for municipal fire service operations, water supply, hydrant operations, and relay pumping.
