Separate, sealed Bids will be received for furnishing all materials, labor, tools, equipment, and incidentals necessary for the installation of Resurfacing Improvements to Five City Streets; Gaskin Avenue, Moody Lane, North College Ave, Lockwood Drive, and Kennedy Circle for the city's funded Transportation Investment Act (TIA) Project, PI # 002489 until (2:00 pm) (local time), Wednesday, December 16, 2026, at which time bids will be opened and read.
