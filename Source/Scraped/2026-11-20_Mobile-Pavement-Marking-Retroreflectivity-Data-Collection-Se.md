## Description

This purpose of this Invitation for Bids (IFB) is to solicit bids from Vendors that have been pre-qualified in accordance with “Mobile Retroreflectivity” (Discipline Code 212) for the collection of mobile pavement marking retro reflectivity data for the NC Department of Transportation Signing and Delineation Unit. Data collected will be used to assist the Department in managing its entire pavement marking asset and may be used in the Quality Control/Quality Assurance of pavement markings placed on the NCDOT highway system by contract.
