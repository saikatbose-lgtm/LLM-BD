## Description

ODOT is seeking 1 qualified Contractor to provide tow-type snow plow trailers with mounted stainless steel sanders and deicer pre-wet systems, with installation on ODOT supplied chassis. These units must be hydraulically powered by the truck hydraulics.  The tow-type plow and its attachments will be used by crews day and night, in winter weather conditions for daily highway maintenance operations.
