## Description

Texas State Technical College is seeking proposals from qualified suppliers to provide runnable aircraft engine test stands for instructional use in the Aviation Maintenance at the Waco campus. The equipment will support hands-on student training involving reciprocating and turbine engine operation, inspection, troubleshooting, and maintenance-related instructional activities.
