## Description

PFAS Water Treatment Plant Upgrades: The project consists of modifications to the existing Pond Street, Beal, and Broadway Water Treatment Plants in Hanover, MA, including pre-fabricated metal building systems; PFAS treatment systems; chemical feed system; PFAS influent wet well; and associated structural, architectural, process piping, instrumentation and controls, plumbing, fire protection, HVAC, and electrical work to provide fully functional modifications to the water treatment plants.
