## Description

PerTGC 2269 and 437.054, the Texas Military Department (TMD) is seeking competitive sealed proposals from General Contractors to modernize the Texas Army National Guard Dallas California Crossing Readiness Center (RC) facility in Dallas, Texas. The General Contractor shall provide all materials, equipment, labor, coordination, and supervisory activities necessary to complete construction of the project. This project is part of the STAR program and will renovate a 2-story approximately 59,000 SF Readiness Center. Work includes mitigation of current health, safety, and code deficiencies, as well as improve mission capabilities and force protection, as indicated in the Contract Documents except if delineated as a Proposal Option.
