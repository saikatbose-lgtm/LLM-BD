## Description

REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS: OFFICE SPACE LEASE Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board (PGCB) The Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board is seeking proposals for office real estate space in Northeastern Pennsylvania. Location Requirements: Proximity to Municipalities: Must be located in or near Dunmore, Clarks Summit, Throop, Jessup, Archbald, or Dickson City. Highway Access: Must be located within 5 miles of Interstate 81 and/or Interstate 84. Space & Facility Specifications: Size: 1,500 usable square feet (USF) or less. Occupancy: Must comfortably accommodate 10 employees simultaneously. Layout: Minimum of one (1) private locking office, one (1) small conference room (8–10 person capacity), and space for four (4) cubicles. Amenities & Security: Proposal cost must include restroom access, adequate on-site parking, security, utilities, janitorial services and the ability to install remote keypad access
