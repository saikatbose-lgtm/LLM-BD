## Description

Texas State Technical College (TSTC) is seeking proposals from qualified suppliers for a comprehensive, centralized software platform to support institutional effectiveness, assessment management, strategic and operational goal planning, accreditation management, faculty qualifications tracking, data analytics, and institutional reporting. The RFP seeks a supplier capable of providing an integrated solution that streamlines core institutional processes into a single system, eliminates redundant legacy tools, improves cross-functional collaboration, and ensures compliance with institutional and regional accreditation standards. Bid response via https://texas-state-technical-college.public-portal.us.workdayspend.com/.
