## Description

The objective of this solicitation is to establish one or more Price Agreement(s) with qualified Proposers to provide Auxiliary Aids and Services for Deaf, DeafBlind, and Hard of Hearing that support Effective Communication, including the Service Modalities, American Sign Language ("ASL"), Communication Access Real-time Translation ("CART"), Cued Speech, Oral Interpreting, Other Sign Language (other than ASL), Protactile Interpreting ("PTASL"), and Tactile ASL ("TASL").
