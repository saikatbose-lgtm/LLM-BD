## Description

The City of Thomasville is seeking a multidisciplinary consultant team to update its Comprehensive Plan in a manner that builds directly upon the vision, goals, and place-based framework established through the Thomasville: Blueprint 2028 document. The updated Plan should generally maintain that established direction unless changing conditions, subsequent planning efforts, or community input demonstrate that revisions are warranted. While the Blueprint provides a long-range vision for Thomasville, this update should establish a focused ten-year framework for implementation.
