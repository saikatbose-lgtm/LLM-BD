## Description

The City wants licensed architectural firms to provide design development, bid documents, bidding/construction support, cost estimating, schedule monitoring, and project management for renovating 11 restrooms, service desks, flooring, and accessibility upgrades at the Main Library (Olive Ave/S. Pastoria Ave).

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** This is architectural design and construction-support services for a building renovation. Although the scope mentions "project management" as one task among architectural deliverables, the engagement as a whole is an architecture/construction contract, not a standalone project-management services engagement — the PSC codes confirm Architectural Services/Design as the actual category, so this is not a genuine match to "Project Management."
