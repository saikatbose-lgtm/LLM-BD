## Description

Pursuant to Section 143-129 of the General Statutes of North Carolina, sealed proposals for furnishing Substation Equipment for the Arcadia Substation for the City of Lexington Electric Department will be received on or before 2:00 p.m. EST, Friday, December 18, 2026 in the Council Chambers at the City of Lexington City Centre, 200 North State Street, Lexington, NC 27292, at which time and place the proposals will be publicly opened and read. Bidders will be required to comply with all applicable statutes and regulations and those attached to and made a part of the proposal. Specifications may be obtained at the office of the City's Engineer, Southeastern Consulting Engineers, Inc., Jeremy Furr - jeremy@scepower.com, P. O. Box 240436, Charlotte, North Carolina 28224, Monday-Friday 9:00am-4:00pm (704) 523-6045 .
