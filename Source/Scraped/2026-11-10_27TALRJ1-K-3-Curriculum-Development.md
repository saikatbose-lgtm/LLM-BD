## Description

DESE seeks a vendor or pair of vendors to support the curriculum development and professional learning for a new K-3 curriculum, which will be offered for free to Massachusetts educators. The curriculum will include two strands: Reading Foundational Skills (Scope A) and Connected Learning: ELA, Science, and Social Studies (Scope B). Bids are welcome for one or both scopes.
