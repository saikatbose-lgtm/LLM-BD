## Description

The City of Alamo, Georgia is seeking sealed bids for construction services to replace the Alamo Event Center, located at 3 Railroad Ave, Alamo, GA 30411, which was damaged as a result of Hurricane Helene. This project will be partially funded through FEMA's Public Assistance Program with the purpose of demolishing the existing concrete slab and foundation and constructing a new Alamo Event Center.
