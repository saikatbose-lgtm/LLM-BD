## Description

Contractor shall provide temporary/relief services, on an as-needed basis, to the California Department of Veterans Affairs/Rector Reservoir (CalVet). Contractor shall provide services for the sole purpose of operating the Rector Reservoir Water Treatment Plant in accordance with the Veterans Home Policies and Procedures, the requirements of the State of California Regional Water Quality Control Board, and any applicable Federal requirements. The Water Treatment Plant is located at 7300 Silverado Trail, Napa, CA 94558. Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, staff, transportation, license, permits, and every other item of expense necessary, unless otherwise stated. Contractor shall provide services, as specified, to California Department of Veterans Affairs (CalVet).
