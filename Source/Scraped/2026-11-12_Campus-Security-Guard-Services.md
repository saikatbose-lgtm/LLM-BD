## Description

The purpose of this Request For Proposals (RFP) is to solicit responses from vendors who can provide security guard and/or patrol services for East Carolina University (hereinafter referred to as ECU, University, or State), primarily the ECU Campus Living residence hall communities. The purpose of these services is to enhance safety, provide a visible security presence, deter criminal and disruptive behavior, identify safety concerns, and support East Carolina University Police Department (ECU Police) operations. The Contractor shall provide patrol services in accordance with all applicable University policies, State regulations, and the requirements of this Contract. Consistent with the University's short-term objective, services shall focus on exterior residence hall neighborhood security and patrol operations. The intent of this solicitation is to award an Agency Contract. Special instructions: Site visit requests must be emailed to tutenb@ecu.edu no later than 5 pm on Tues, 11/17/26..
