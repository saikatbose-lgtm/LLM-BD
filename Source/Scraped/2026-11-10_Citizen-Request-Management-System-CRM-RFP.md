## Description

The Town of Wendell, North Carolina, is requesting proposals from qualified firms to provide, implement, configure, and support an organization-wide Citizen Request Management System (CRM). The selected solution will support the complete lifecycle of requests received from residents.

## Relevance

Matched: Town of Wendell seeks a firm to provide, implement, configure and support an organization-wide Citizen Request Management (CRM) SaaS system - IT implementation and software support work (commodity 811620 Cloud-based SaaS).
