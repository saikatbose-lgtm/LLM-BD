## Description

The University of Texas at Austin seeks qualified environmental consulting firms to provide environmental assessments, inspections, sampling, testing, monitoring, regulatory support, remediation protocols, project oversight, and related consulting services on an as-needed basis.The University anticipates making multiple contract awards to provide qualified options, maintain competition, increase service capacity, and support routine, planned, and emergency environmental work.Services may be authorized on a lump-sum/hard-bid basis, a time-and-materials not-to-exceed basis, or a combination of these pricing methods, as determined by the University for each assignment.
