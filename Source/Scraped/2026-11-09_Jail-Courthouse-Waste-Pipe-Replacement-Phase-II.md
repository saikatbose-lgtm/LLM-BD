## Description

Lee County is in search for bidders to replace the waste pipe in the jail/courthouse. Scope of Work includes: Provide all necessary labor, material, equipment, supervision, etc. for waste pipe improvements within designated areas of the Lee County Jail/Courthouse building. Base bid includes removal and replacement of existing cast-iron waste and vent piping in inmate cell pods “A” and “B”, removal and replacement of existing cast-iron waste and vent piping in the common utility chase serving public fixtures in inmate cell pods “A” and “B”, and addition of a floor sink and lint interceptor in laundry room 1018. Alternate 1 includes removal and replacement of approximately sixteen (16) linear feet (to be verified by Contractor) of existing cast-iron waste piping below the ground floor slab in men's locker room 1035, replacement of two (2) existing tank toilets in men's locker room 1035 and one tank toilet in women's locker room 1058 with flush valve toilets, and providing new domestic water lines to the new toilets. Alternate 2 includes removal and replacement of approximately fifty (50) linear feet (to be verified by Contractor) of existing cast-iron waste piping below the ground floor slab between offices 1038 and 1061.
