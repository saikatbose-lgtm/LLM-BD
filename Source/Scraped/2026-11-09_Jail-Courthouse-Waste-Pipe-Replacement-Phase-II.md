## Description

Lee County is in search for bidders to replace the waste pipe in the jail/courthouse. Scope of Work includes: Provide all necessary labor, material, equipment, supervision, etc. for waste pipe improvements within designated areas of the Lee County Jail/Courthouse building. Base bid includes removal and replacement of existing cast-iron waste and vent piping in inmate cell pods “A” and “B”, removal and replacement of existing cast-iron waste and vent piping in the common utility chase serving public fixtures in inmate cell pods “A” and “B”, and addition of a floor sink and lint interceptor in laundry room 1018. Alternate 1 includes removal and replacement of approximately sixteen (16) linear feet (to be verified by Contractor) of existing cast-iron waste piping below the ground floor slab in men's locker room 1035, replacement of two (2) existing tank toilets in men's locker room 1035 ... Mandatory conference/site visit: 12/10/2026. Special instructions: A Pre-Bid Meeting will be held at 10:00 AM on December 10, 2026 at 1400 S. Horner Blvd., Sanford, NC 27330, meeting in the brick courtyard between the jail and courthouse buildings..
