## Description

The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to solicit Offers from qualified Vendors to serve as the Program Broker/Administrator for the Supplemental Benefits Program. The awarded Program Broker/Administrator is expected to administer RSD-approved offerings and, at RSD’s direction, assess Retiree and other eligible benefit Recipient’s needs, conduct carrier and other provider solicitations, and negotiate favorable and customized terms.
