## Description

The Public Works Department is requesting Statements of Qualifications for on-call and as-needed staff augmentation services for the Public Works Department. Services requested include development permit inspections, encroachment permit inspections, utility permit inspections, traffic signal inspections, and landscape inspections to supplement the City's current inspection staff, in addition to construction administration services. The City intends to select several qualified firms to provide these services on an hourly on-call/as-needed basis for various projects.
