## Description

We are seeking a General Construction Contractor to be our Project Construction Manager at Risk. The Project is to be performed pursuant to the AIA A133-2019 Standard Form of Agreement with payment based on the cost of work, plus a fee and with a guaranteed maximum price. The successful proposer (the Construction Manager at Risk (CM)) shall serve as the general construction contractor and construction manager for the Project. The Project will include interior and exterior painting, at seven (7) schools, as our budget permits, and may include replacing some flooring, ceilings and lighting and other similar/related work. The services will consist of cost estimating, constructability analysis and scheduling and phasing of the renovation and painting. The CM will assist the School District and the project Architect in finalizing the phasing and scheduling of the work, and cost estimating and value engineering of the Project.
