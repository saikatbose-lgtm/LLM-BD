## Description

The Montgomery County Board of Education is requesting sealed proposals for Construction Management at Risk services for the New Montgomery County High School Baseball Field Construction project. The selected firm will enter into a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) contract. Proposals must be received by 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Wednesday, December 9, 2026, at the Montgomery County Board of Education office, 703 Dobbins Street, Mount Vernon, GA 30445. Oral, fax, or email responses will not be accepted. The proposal requirements package may be obtained by written request to James W. Buckley and Associates, Inc., Attn: Teresa Moore, 114 North Green Street, Swainsboro, GA 30401, or by email to tam@jwbuckley.com. Requests must be on company letterhead, dated and signed, and include the firm name, principal/owner, phone and fax numbers, mailing address, and project name. The procurement will follow the Georgia Local Government Public Works Construction Law, O.C.G.A. § 36-91-1 et seq. The Owner reserves the right to reject any or all proposals and to waive technicalities and informalities.
