## Description

Rockdale County, Georgia, is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from experienced and licensed general contractors interested in performing residential rehabilitation and repair services through the County's proposed Repair & Revitalize Program. The program is intended to assist eligible owner-occupied households with necessary housing repairs and improvements addressing substandard housing conditions, health and safety concerns, structural deficiencies, accessibility needs, and applicable building code requirements. The County anticipates applying for funding through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP), with an anticipated rehabilitation program serving approximately 10 to 15 owner-occupied homes.
