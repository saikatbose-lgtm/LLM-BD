## Description

Rockdale County, Georgia, is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from experienced and licensed general contractors interested in performing residential rehabilitation and repair services through the County's proposed Repair & Revitalize Program. The program is intended to assist eligible owner-occupied households with necessary housing repairs and improvements addressing substandard housing conditions, health and safety concerns, structural deficiencies, accessibility needs, and applicable building code requirements.
