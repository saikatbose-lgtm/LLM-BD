## Description

The City of Palmdale is soliciting proposals from qualified consulting Civil Engineering Design firms to provide Professional Civil Engineering Design Services for the SAVES New Building Design Modifications and Off-Site Improvements Project (Project No. 874A). The project is located along Avenue Q between 3rd Street East and Clock Tower Drive in the City of Palmdale. The project involves modifying the existing design from a tenant improvement project to a new building construction project. Services include preparing construction documents for an approximately 13,000-square-foot building and the installation of approximately 280 linear feet of 8-inch sanitary sewer main, extending northward from the proposed point of connection within Avenue Q to the future SAVES parcel.
