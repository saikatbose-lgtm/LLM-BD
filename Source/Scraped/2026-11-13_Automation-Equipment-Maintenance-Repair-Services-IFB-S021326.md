## Description

The California Correctional Training and Rehabilitation Authority is soliciting for automation equipment maintenance and repair services for the juice processing and refrigeration equipment utilized by the CALCTRA Food & Beverage Packaging Enterprise at the California Institution for Men (CIM) located in Chino, California. Services include Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC), High Temperature Short Time (HTST), and Clean In Place (CIP) automation controls/panels, refrigeration units, ice banks, chillers, glacier systems, compressors, and associated controls and components to maintain the enterprise in operable condition.
