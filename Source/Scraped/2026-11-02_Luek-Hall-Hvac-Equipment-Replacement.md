## Description

Millersville University is seeking bids for General, Plumbing, HVAC and Electrical Construction for the following scope of work. Work under this project entails General Construction selective demolition of support areas, roof repair, GWB wall and removal/reinstallation of existing ACT ceiling; HVAC Construction work includes selective demolition, new fan coils, pumps, piping, piping insulation equipment and building controls. Electrical work includes selective demolition and new circuits for HVAC equipment. Project Start May 10, 2027, Project Completion July 31, 2027. Bid documents will be available on PennBid on November 3. 2026. Mandatory Prebid Meeting – November 10, 2026, 10:00 AM Palmer Building
