## Description

THIS IS NOT A BID OPPORTUNITY.The Illinois Department of Children and Family Services is seeking an emergency contract to establish a Contract for staffing of the Diligent Search Services Center (DSSC).  The primary function of the DSSC is to assist DCFS and Purchase of Service (POS) caseworkers by providing diligent search consultation, performing extensive electronic searches, and providing detailed information on missing persons such as parents of youth in care, as required by the courts.
