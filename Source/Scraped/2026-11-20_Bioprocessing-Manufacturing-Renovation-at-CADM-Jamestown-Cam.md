## Description

Letters of Interest and Standard Form 254 (SF-254) will be received until Friday, December 11, 2026 at 3 p.m. EST in the Construction Office, Campus Operations Building, 1003 Bonner Drive, Jamestown, NC 27282, Attn: Matt Gunderson to provide architectural and engineering services for the renovation of approximately 7,500 square feet of existing unconditioned mechanical equipment mezzanine space in the Center for Advanced Manufacturing, located at 6012 W. Gate City Boulevard on the Jamestown Campus. The renovated space will support the Bioprocess Manufacturing Technology curriculum. The existing space consists of a concrete floor on steel decking, steel columns, roof joists, overhead metal decking, and partially installed drywall on metal studs. The space is currently vacant and will be converted into instructional laboratories, classrooms, offices, and restrooms. The laboratories will include simulated clean-room spaces for cell culture intended to support instruction in clean-room procedures and operations. This project does not include creation of operational clean rooms certified for manufacturing, research, or other production activities. The project will also install elevator equipment within a previously constructed elevator shaft. Listing status at retrieval: Open.
