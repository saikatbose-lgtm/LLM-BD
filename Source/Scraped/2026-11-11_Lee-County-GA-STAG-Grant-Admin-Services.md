## Description

The Lee County Board of Commissioners is requesting qualifications and proposals for selection of Grant Administrator to provide post-award services for a water infrastructure project funded by the FY 2026 Congressionally Directed Spending (CDS)/Community Project Funding (CPF) Grants Program for Water Projects.
