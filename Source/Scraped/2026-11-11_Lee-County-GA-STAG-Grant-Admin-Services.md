## Description

The Lee County Board of Commissioners is requesting qualifications and proposals for selection of Grant Administrator to provide post-award services for a water infrastructure project funded by the FY 2026 Congressionally Directed Spending (CDS)/Community Project Funding (CPF) Grants Program for Water Projects. Post-award services will include but are not limited to: preparation of final application documents, assistance with NEPA review, assistance with financial administration of grant funds and record keeping, public hearings, required acquisition following the Uniform Relocation Assistance and Real Property Acquisition Act (URA), Davis Bacon and related labor requirements; preparation of disbursement requests, reporting and close-out documents through the USEPA, and oversite to ensure program compliance. Statements of qualifications and proposals are being requested from Grant Administrators with a strong record in successfully assisting local governments in the administration of Federally Assisted Projects.
