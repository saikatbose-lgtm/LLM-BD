## Description

The Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) seeks to accept services from Dynamic Learning Maps (DLM) Alternate Assessment System as the future vendor for the MCAS Alternate Assessment (MCAS-Alt). DESE intends to use DLM's governance structure, technical platform, reporting  services, training programs, instructionally embedded essential elements, and accessibility products. Responses will determine if other vendors can deliver similar required products for DESE.
