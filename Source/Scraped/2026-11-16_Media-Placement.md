## Description

The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to solicit proposals from qualified Service Providers to provide comprehensive digital and traditional media strategy, planning, buying, placement, and analytics services for cohesive, cost-effective, and data-driven advertising campaigns. The selected Service Provider will partner with Asheville-Buncombe (“A-B”) Tech’s Marketing & Communications Department to design and execute campaigns that strengthen brand awareness, drive enrollment growth, support special program initiatives, and deliver measurable return on investment through targeted media placements and real-time performance analytics. This procurement is being led by A-B Tech’s Marketing and Communications Department, which is responsible for developing, implementing, and overseeing the College’s strategic communications, branding, and advertising initiatives.
