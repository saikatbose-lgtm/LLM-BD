## Description

The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to solicit proposals from qualified Service Providers to provide comprehensive digital and traditional media strategy, planning, buying, placement, and analytics services for cohesive, cost-effective, and data-driven advertising campaigns. The selected Service Provider will partner with Asheville-Buncombe (“A-B”) Tech’s Marketing & Communications Department to design and execute campaigns that strengthen brand awareness, drive enrollment growth, support special program initiatives, and deliver measurable return on investment through targeted media placements and real-time performance analytics. This procurement is being led by A-B Tech’s Marketing and Communications Department, which is responsible for developing, implementing, and overseeing the College’s strategic communications, branding, and advertising initiatives. The division works to engage prospective students, strengthen community awareness, and support enrollment and retention efforts in alignment with A-B Tech’s mission and strategic plan. A-B Tech is Western North Carolina’s largest community college, serving more than 23,000 students annually through academic programs (degrees, diploma and certificates), continuing education and workforce training programs. A-B Tech is the 8th largest of 58 colleges that comprise the North Carolina Community College System. The College’s service area is in Buncombe and Madison counties, but it enrolls students from throughout Western NC and beyond. The intent of this solicitation is to award an Agency Specific Contract. Objective: The primary objective of this contract is to generate a robust pipeline of prospective students to meet admission and enrollment targets while supporting special interest campaigns. The Service Provider shall act as a strategic partner to the Marketing and Communications department to secure high-quality media inventory and negotiate added-value opportunities.
