## Description

The City of Sandy Springs is seeking a qualified contractor to conduct a Pavement Condition Index (PCI) assessment of approximately 302 miles of City-maintained roadways, as outlined in the Scope of Services below. The City has conducted six roadway condition evaluations since its inception, with the most recent evaluation completed in 2023.
