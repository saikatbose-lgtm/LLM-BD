## Description

This solicitation is authorized pursuant to Government Code Section 14838.5, which provides for the award of contracts for the acquisition of goods, services, or information technology that has an estimated value of greater than $5,000.00, but less than $249,999.99, to a certified SB, including a Micro Business. Participation is restricted to California Certified Small Business (SB) or Microbusiness (MB) vendors. Quotes submitted by non SB/MB vendors will not be considered for award. A bid of $250,000.00 and above will result in rejection of your bid due to non-responsiveness. Bids shall only be accepted for this solicitation from Department of General Services (DGS) registered small businesses/micro businesses. Please find more information about becoming a registered small business/micro business at the Office of Small Business and Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise Services.
