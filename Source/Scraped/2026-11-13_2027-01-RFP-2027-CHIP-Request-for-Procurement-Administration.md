## Description

CITY OF PERRY, GEORGIA REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS AND PROPOSALS FOR PLANNING, ADMINISTRATION AND RELATED SERVICES The City of Perry is requesting statements of qualifications and proposals from consultants with a strong record in successfully assisting local governments with the implementation of Federally Assisted Projects. Responding firms should be qualified to provide grant writing, grant administration, and related services for projects utilizing federal funds.

## Relevance

City of Perry RFQ/RFP for planning, administration and related services for CHIP (housing) federally assisted projects - specialized grant administration, not IT/staffing/admin support work.
