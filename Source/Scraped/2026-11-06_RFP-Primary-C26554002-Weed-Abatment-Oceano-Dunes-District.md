## Description

The Department of Parks and Recreation (Parks), Oceano Dunes District located in San Luis Obispo County, CA is issuing this RFP-Primary to receive Proposals from qualified firms (Proposers) to provide Weed Abatement Services. The objective of this contract is to restore the ecological integrity and function of the native habitat through the eradication and/or control of several exotic invasive plant/weed species that have established within the Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area ODSVRA) and Pismo State Beach (PSB) located in San Luis Obispo County with the Oceano Dunes District (ODD). The Agreement period will start upon DGS approval of the Agreement and will extend 36 months (3 years).
