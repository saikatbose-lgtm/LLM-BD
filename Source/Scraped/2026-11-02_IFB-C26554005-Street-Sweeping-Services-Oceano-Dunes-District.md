## Description

The Oceano Dunes District of the CA Department of Parks and Recreation, located in San Luis Obispo County invites bids from qualified firms to provide PM-10 efficient street sweeping services three times per week, with a tentative start date of 01/01/2026 through 12/31/2029, or upon contract approval. Services are to be provided along Pier Avenue, located within Oceano, CA, with additional services locations upon the State Representative's Request.
