## Description

The purpose of this solicitation is to create/expand an established pool of Ohio Licensed Lead Abatement Contractors-Offerors to assist the City of Toledo, Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD) in meeting its established goals for its Lead-Based Paint Hazard Control Grant. State of Ohio licensed Lead Abatement Contractors will perform lead-based paint hazard control work of varying degrees primarily on residential 1-4 single-family housing units, using State of Ohio licensed Lead Workers.
