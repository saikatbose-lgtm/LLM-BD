## Description

NYSIF intends to procure Dun and Bradstreet Hoovers under its discretionary purchasing authority; limited to NYS-certified MWBE or SDVOB small businesses; value not to exceed $1,500,000. Bidders must be able to resell D&B Hoovers Audience Builder including 249,000 company records, 70,000 contact records, Visitor Intelligence with monthly loading of audience data, 25 custom fields, and hands-on implementation and ongoing support.

## Relevance

Business data/analytics SaaS subscription with implementation and ongoing support; matches Data Research and Analytics and Software Maintenance/Support. Borderline: it is a reseller set-aside for a named product rather than a services engagement.
