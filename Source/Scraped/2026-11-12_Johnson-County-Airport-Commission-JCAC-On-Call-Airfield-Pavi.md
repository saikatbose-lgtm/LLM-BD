## Description

The Johnson County Airport Commission (JCAC) is requesting competitive sealed bids for select airfield paving improvements to include asphalt, concrete, and ancillary items as further described herein (Bid Request No. 2026-057). The Contract shall be structured as an indefinite quantity type contract with no guaranteed minimum, where dispatch orders for the required services will be issued as the need arises. It is anticipated that up to two (2) Contractors will be awarded as a result of this Invitation for Bid. Tasks assigned under these contracts will generally be limited to $75,000 and will not exceed $275,000 aggregate per year. Sealed bids for Bid Request No. 2026-057 shall be submitted electrically via IonWave at https://jocogov.ionwave.net until the close date reflected above.
