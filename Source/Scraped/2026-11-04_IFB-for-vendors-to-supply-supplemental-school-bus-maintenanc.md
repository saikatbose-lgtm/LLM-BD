## Description

Weston Public Schools is issuing this Invitation for Bids (IFB) pursuant to M.G.L. c. 30B, section 5 to solicit firm fixed pricing from qualified vendors to provide supplemental preventive maintenance, diagnostic, warranty, and corrective repair services for the District's student transportation fleet (school buses, MFSABs, and vans across gasoline, diesel, and electric powertrains). Services will be requested on an as-needed basis to supplement internal capacity during peak volume period
