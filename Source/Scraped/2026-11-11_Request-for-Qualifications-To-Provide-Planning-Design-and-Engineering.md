## Description

The City of Chickamauga is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from qualified firms to provide planning, design, and engineering services for a HUD Community Project Funding (CPF) grant supporting blight remediation and revitalization in downtown Chickamauga.Anticipated improvements include underground relocation of existing overhead electrical infrastructure, sewer line installation, rehabilitation and façade improvements to selected downtown storefronts, and site preparation (clearing, grading, and related work) on non-contaminated portions of the former Crystal Springs Print Works property. HUD-funded site preparation work is separate from environmental remediation activities on contaminated portions of the property being performed under the City¿s EPA Brownfields Cleanup Grant.Services include project planning and development, preliminary and final design, preparation of plans, specifications, and bid documents, bidding assistance, construction administration, and other related professional services. The selected firm must comply with all applicable federal requirements, including the Build America, Buy America Act (BABA), Davis-Bacon and Related Acts, 2 CFR Part 200, and other HUD cross-cutting requirements.Statements of Qualifications are due December 12, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. ET. See the attached Request for Qualifications for the complete scope, evaluation criteria, submission requirements, and contact information.
