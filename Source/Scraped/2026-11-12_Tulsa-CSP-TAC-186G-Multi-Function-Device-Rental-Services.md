## Description

The City of Tulsa Information Technology Department is seeking proposals to provide the monthly rental of multi-function digital print/fax/scan/copy devices (MFDs), including ongoing maintenance and support services, to equip the majority of City facilities at multiple sites throughout Tulsa and Tulsa County. Pre-proposal conference (virtual) 11/18/2026 at 9:00 AM.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** Although this solicitation is owned by the IT Department and includes "ongoing maintenance and support," the underlying work is hardware equipment rental (copier/printer/MFD leasing) with device maintenance — not software maintenance/support, IT services, programming, data processing, networking, or staffing. The NIGP codes (600-72, 985-58) are office-equipment rental/lease codes, not IT-service codes. The scope of work (device configuration, OEM spec sheets, print/copy capabilities) confirms this is an equipment procurement rather than genuine IT services/consulting work covered by the approved keyword list.
