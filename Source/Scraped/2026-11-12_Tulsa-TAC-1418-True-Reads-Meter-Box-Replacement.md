## Description

The City of Tulsa Water and Sewer Department is soliciting sealed bids from qualified sellers to perform service line inspection and meter box replacement in support of the "True Reads" city-wide meter change-out project, which involves replacing approximately 147,000 water meters with automated meter reading (AMR) technology, replacing associated meter cans, and inspecting water service lines. Pre-bid conference 12/1/2026 at 9:00 AM CT.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** This is a field construction/physical-infrastructure services contract (water meter and meter-box replacement, service line inspection) for a municipal water utility. Although it involves "automated meter reading" hardware, the scope is physical utility field work, not IT services, software, data processing, or staffing covered by the approved keyword list.
