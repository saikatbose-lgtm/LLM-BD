## Description

The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, Department of Labor and Industry (DLI), on behalf of the Commonwealth Technical Institute (CTI) at the Hiram G Andrews Center (HGAC) located at 727 Goucher Street, Johnstown, PA is issuing this Invitation for Bid to award a purchase order to a qualified responsible, authorized supplier to furnish, install and maintain leased Speed Queen washers and dryers suitable in a student housing setting with an average student census of 350. The supplier shall provide all labor, tools, hardware, accessory parts and materials necessary to furnish, deliver, install and maintain leased washers and dryers to include hook up, preventive maintenance, and non-routine repairs to ensure continuous and safe operation. Appliances must be ready to operate immediately upon completion of delivery and installation. BID SUBMISSION AND PRICING Only the pricing entered into the SRM
