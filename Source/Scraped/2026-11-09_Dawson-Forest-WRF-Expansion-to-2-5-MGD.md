## Description

Invitation for Bidder's Pre-Qualification for Dawson Forest WRF Expansion to 2.5 MGD, Etowah Water & Sewer Authority, Dawsonville, Georgia. Beginning on November 11, 2026, a copy of the application and qualification requirements can be obtained from the Etowah Water & Sewer Authority Engineering & Construction Department located at 1162 Highway 53 East, Dawsonville, Georgia 30534 or via email electronically from Tim Collins at tcollins@etowahwaterga.gov. Completed pre-qualification applications for bidding will be received electronically in PDF format until 5:00 p.m., local time, December 18, 2026. All applicants submitting pre-qualification documentation will be notified of their status by January 8, 2027. THE PROJECT consists of furnishing all materials and equipment and performing all labor necessary for the expansion of the existing Dawson Forest WRF to 2.5 MGD with new equipment and appurtenances as detailed in the specifications and as shown on the contract drawings. The project generally consists of the following major components of work and appurtenances: 1. Installation of Mechanical Bar Screen No. 2 and Appurtenances at the Influent Pump Station; 2. Miscellaneous Upgrades to Existing SBR Tanks No. 1 & No. 2; 3. Construction of SBR Tanks No. 3 & No. 4 and Appurtenances; 4. Installation of Post EQ Pump No. 3; 5. Installation of additional Disc Filter Equipment; 6. Installation of additional UV Equipment; 7. Miscellaneous Upgrades to Existing Aerobic Digesters No. 1 & No. 2; 8. Conversion of Two Abandoned SRB Tanks to Aerobic Digesters No. 3 & No. 4; 9. Miscellaneous Upgrades to Existing Aerobic Digester Blower Building; and 10. Miscellaneous Civil, Electrical and Instrumentation Improvements.
