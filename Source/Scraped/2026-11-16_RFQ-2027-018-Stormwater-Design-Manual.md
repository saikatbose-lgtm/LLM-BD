## Description

Union County, North Carolina, through the Union County Planning Department, is seeking Statements of Qualifications (SOQs) from qualified professional engineering and consulting firms to develop the Union County Stormwater Design Manual. The selected firm will provide stormwater technical standards, design criteria, stormwater control measure guidance, administrative procedures, public outreach, and final manual deliverables. This is a Request for Qualifications (RFQ). Responding firms shall not include project-specific work product or fee proposals in their SOQ. Selection will be conducted in accordance with N.C.G.S. § 143-64.31.
