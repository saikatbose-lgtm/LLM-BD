## Description

Sealed Bids will be received by the City of Albany, Procurement Division, 222 Pine Avenue, Suite 260, Albany, Georgia 31701, until 2:30 p.m. on December 17, 2026, this project consists of pavement reconstruction of an existing taxiway/roadway, construction of new asphalt taxilanes, and three new T-hangars. Other items include grading and drainage, marking, fencing, and grassing. The Base Bid consists of all pavement reconstruction, new pavement construction, grading and drainage, marking, fencing, and grassing. There are four additive bids which include one (1) T-hangar each.
