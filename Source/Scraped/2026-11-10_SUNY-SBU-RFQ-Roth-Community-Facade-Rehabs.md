## Description

Stony Brook University is soliciting qualifications from consultants to repair and rehabilitate concrete, masonry, and spandrel beam facades at Roth Community on the Stony Brook campus. This is a qualifications-only RFQ (no cost proposals at this stage); cost proposals will be requested only after a consultant is selected from the qualifications submitted. One firm will be selected based on this solicitation.
