## Description

The Savannah-Chatham County Public School System ("SCCPSS" or the "District") is soliciting proposals from qualified firms and individuals to provide legislative lobbying, government relations and strategic consulting services related to legislative matters occurring during the 2027 legislative session. The selected Offeror(s) will represent and advocate on behalf of the District before the Georgia General Assembly, state executive agencies, and other governmental entities whose actions may impact the District.
