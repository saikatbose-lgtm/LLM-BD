## Description

Request for Information (RFI) is to solicit input from interested vendors relating to Scope of Work (SOW) development for LWP updates in the Tar-Pamlico 03020103 NCDMS Service Area. NCDMS is seeking information and feedback on innovative and data driven methods to evaluate current watershed conditions and identify potential functional uplift opportunities, building upon past NCDMS LWPs and other relevant watershed planning efforts within the Tar-Pamlico River Basin, the Tar-Pamlico 03020103 HUC8 and/or within the Green Mill Run and Cow Swamp LWP planning areas.
