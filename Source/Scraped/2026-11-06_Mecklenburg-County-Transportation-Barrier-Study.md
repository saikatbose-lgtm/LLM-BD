## Description

Mecklenburg County, located in the State of North Carolina, is seeking Quotes from qualified vendors to perform a Transportation Barrier Study for the Mecklenburg County area. Mecklenburg County Department of Child, Family, and Adult Services (CFAS) has been awarded a Section 5303 planning grant to fund a study on transportation barriers for older adults 55+ who are unhoused or at risk of being unhoused. Special instructions: Email responses to Meckbids@mecklenburgcountync.gov or demetrius.littlejohn2@mecknc.gov. ****DO NOT SUBMIT QUOTES TO NCEVP.****.
