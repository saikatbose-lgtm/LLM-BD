## Description

Butts County Board of Commissioners is requesting proposals for the furnishing, delivery, assembly, installation, testing, and commissioning of five (5) commercial-grade dual-lift, sit/stand dispatch consoles for the new Butts County 911 Emergency Communications facility in Jackson, Georgia. The primary dispatch room is approximately 32' x 40'. The system shall be designed for continuous 24-hour public-safety operations.
