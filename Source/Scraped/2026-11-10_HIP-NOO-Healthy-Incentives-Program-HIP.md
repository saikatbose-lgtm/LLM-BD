## Description

Healthy Incentives Program (HIP) is a Massachusetts program that helps SNAP clients pay for fruits and vegetables purchased from participating HIP farm vendors, up to a monthly limit. DTA seeks to add HIP access points to its network of vendors so that more SNAP clients can use HIP to purchase fruits and vegetables. Through this NOO, DTA is accepting proposals from eligible local produce vendors that want to participate in HIP. This opportunity is open to both new and existing HIP vendors.
