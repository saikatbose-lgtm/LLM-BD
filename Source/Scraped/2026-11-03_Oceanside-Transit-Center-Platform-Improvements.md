## Description

North County Transit District, operating as North County Transit – San Diego Railroad (NCTD) is seeking proposals for improvements at the Oceanside Transit Center (OTC), including repainting, restriping, concrete pad replacement, concrete joint seal repair, and electrical appurtenance replacement. The Project will procure the necessary materials, equipment, and labor needed to perform these tasks as indicated in the scope of work. The Work shall be performed within an active transit center with ongoing public train and bus operations and passenger activity.
