## Description

The Massachusetts Executive Office of Health and Human Services (EOHHS) is issuing this Request for Responses (RFR) pursuant to 801 CMR 21.00, which governs procurement of services by state agencies and requires a competitive process, including the issuance of an RFR, for acquisitions of all commodities and services. In this RFR, EOHHS is soliciting proposals from qualified Bidders for the processing, printing, and mailing of Personal Care Attendant (PCA) identification cards.
