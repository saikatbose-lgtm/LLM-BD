## Description

The purpose of this solicitation is to establish a qualified contractor to provide scheduled inspection, testing, certification, preventive maintenance, filter replacement, annual performance verification, semiannual room decontamination, deficiency reporting, corrective-action support, and re-testing for CDPH BSL-3 laboratory systems. The work is intended to maintain continuous containment capability, support biosafety requirements, reduce unplanned outages, and provide auditable documentation of system performance.
