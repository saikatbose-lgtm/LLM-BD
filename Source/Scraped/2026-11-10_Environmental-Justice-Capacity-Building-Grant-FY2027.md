## Description

EOEEA is soliciting community-based organizations who work at the intersection of environmental justice and equity issues, and serve residents most affected by environmental and energy burdens and climate change. The goal is to support organizations in amplifying the voices of marginalized communities, supporting community-driven solutions, addressing environmental and climate justice, and building capacity .
