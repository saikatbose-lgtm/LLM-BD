## Description

Georgia Institute of Technology, Office of Planning, Design & Construction is soliciting Qualifications Packages from highly qualified Program Management firms (Consultant) to provide services under an Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity Contract (Solicitation/ Contract No. 1093-27) in Atlanta, Georgia. Program Management Services include planning and programming activities, including the development of data, analyses, concepts, and recommendations for design, and pre-GMP construction phases of projects to ensure that all elements of the work meet the required quality, design standards, budget and construction schedule on one or more categories of projects noted under Section II - Category of Work of the RFQP. Respondents who are determined especially qualified may be shortlisted and invited to interview. Interested firms must attend the virtual MANDATORY Pre-Qualification Meeting. Please read the RFQP carefully for additional information. Bid Documents for response are available for download in the Supporting Documents tab. The Project Manager is the main contact for this solicitation. No Phone Calls Please.
