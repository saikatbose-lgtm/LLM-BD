## Description

The University of Iowa solicits proposals from qualified suppliers to establish a pool of preferred suppliers for the provision of promotional products for university-wide use. The bid process is essential for vetting suppliers to ensure they are properly licensed and capable of offering and delivering a wide range of high-quality, brand compliant, and cost-effective products that meet the needs of various campus departments. Please be aware that this is an extensive bid. All Suppliers are cautioned to allow ample time for the submittal of RFP responses well before the due date time requirement. Bids must be submitted via the eBid system.
