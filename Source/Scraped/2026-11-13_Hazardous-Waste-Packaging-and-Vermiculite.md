## Description

Battelle requests proposals for the supply of various UN-specification packaging meeting DOT requirements, plus vermiculite, to support hazardous waste packaging activities. Battelle anticipates a single IDIQ contract with fixed unit pricing for a three-year ordering period. Responses must include the Appendix A pricing form and completed Representations and Certifications.
