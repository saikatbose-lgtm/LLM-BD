## Description

The Board of Commissioners of Glynn County, Georgia (¿the Owner¿) seeks proposals from interested qualified firms to provide a technical assistance and overdose prevention among the justice involved population, building on the framework for community response to the Opioid/Fentanyl issue, including all the details outlined and required in the Program Services section. Glynn County Board of Commissioners, Glynn County is seeking qualified applicants to submit proposals for the development and implementation of data collection instruments, creation of release kits for inmates being released into the community, distribution of first responder safety kits, and a minimum of three trainings for Glynn County first responders, with a foucs on safety of detention officers. The purpose of this RFP is to strengthen community response to the Opioid/Fentanyl issue, reduce the incidents of drug overdose in the community. Glynn County, as a sub-grantee (N51-8-009), received funding from the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council of Georgia to support the development of these activities. Glynn County was identified as one of the top ten priority counties for funding due to the number of overdose incidents and the number of referrals to residential treatment facilities.
