## Description

The City of Brookhaven seeks proposals for an enterprise asset management system (EAMS) supporting Public Works, Parks, and Facilities operations. The solution must be GIS-centric, cloud deployed, mobile enabled, and support lifecycle management of public assets. The system must support comprehensive reporting and analysis and integrates with the City's existing ArcGIS Enterprise environment with minimal disruption to current systems.

## Relevance

City of Brookhaven seeks a GIS-centric, cloud-deployed, mobile-enabled Enterprise Asset Management System for Public Works, Parks and Facilities with reporting/analytics and integrations. NIGP 20868 Project Management Software, 96258 Professional Services and 95882 Records Management Services support the match; this is an IT system selection/implementation.
