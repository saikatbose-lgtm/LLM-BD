## Description

The Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia by and on behalf of the Georgia Institute of Technology is seeking proposals from qualified Contractors to provide composting services for the Georgia Tech campus in Atlanta, Georgia.  This Request for Proposals (RFP) is for the collection, transportation, and processing of compostable material from Georgia Tech¿s campus. Collection of materials will be limited to bins located on loading docks and other waste collection locations on campus. All internal collections will be managed by Georgia Tech. Compostable materials will include food and food residuals, including bone, meat, fish, poultry, and fats; paper, wood and plant fiber products; landscape materials; and all BPI certified plastics. Our intent is to award a contract to the single most responsive responsible bidder.   Use the following link to access the sourcing event: https://bids.sciquest.com/apps/Router/PublicEvent?CustomerOrg=GIT The scope of work is posted in the sourcing event.
