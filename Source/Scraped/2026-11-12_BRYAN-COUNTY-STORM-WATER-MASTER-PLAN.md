## Description

The Bryan County Board of Commissioners is soliciting competitive, sealed Qualifications-Based Selection (QBS) services from highly qualified engineering and environmental consulting firms (A/E professional-services procurement) to develop an unincorporated county, comprehensive Storm Water Master Plan. This project is funded through a Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) award administered by the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency (GEMA/HS). In compliance with federal procurement standards (2 CFR 200.320), Bryan County is utilizing the Qualifications-Based Selection (QBS) and Scope Maximization procurement process. The total project budget is fixed at an approved cap of $2,781,910.00. Because the financial threshold is set by the grant award amount, price is excluded as a competitive metric.
