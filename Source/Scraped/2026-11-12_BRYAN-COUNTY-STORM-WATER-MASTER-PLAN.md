## Description

The Bryan County Board of Commissioners is soliciting competitive, sealed Qualifications-Based Selection (QBS) services from highly qualified engineering and environmental consulting firms (A/E professional-services procurement) to develop an unincorporated county, comprehensive Storm Water Master Plan. This project is funded through a Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) award administered by the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency (GEMA/HS).
