## Description

The Bryan County Board of Commissioners is soliciting competitive, sealed Qualifications-Based Selection (QBS) services from highly qualified engineering and environmental consulting firms (A/E professional-services procurement) to develop an unincorporated county, comprehensive Storm Water Master Plan. This project is funded through a Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) award administered by the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency (GEMA/HS). In compliance with federal procurement standards (2 CFR 200.320), Bryan County is utilizing the Qualifications-Based Selection (QBS) and Scope Maximization procurement process. The total project budget is fixed at an approved cap of $2,781,910.00. Because the financial threshold is set by the grant award amount, price is excluded as a competitive metric. Statement of qualifications will be evaluated, and the contract will be awarded to the firm demonstrating the highest technical qualifications that proposes to accomplish the greatest level, density, and volume of physical work within the fixed grant amount. Interested parties must request a copy of the REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS (RFQ) by emailing Charlene Bunch, cbunch@bryancountyga.gov, prior to preparing and submitting their proposal.
