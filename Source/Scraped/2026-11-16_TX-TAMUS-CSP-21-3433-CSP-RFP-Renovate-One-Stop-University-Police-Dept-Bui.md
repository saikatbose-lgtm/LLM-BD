## Description

The Texas A&M University System Office of Facilities Planning &Construction is soliciting proposals through the Competitive Sealed Proposal (CSP) process on behalf of the Board of Regents of The Texas A&M UniversitySystem for East Texas A&M University in Commerce, Texas. This projectconsists of a renovation that encompasses approximately 27,900 gross squarefeet. Work includes roof replacement, mechanical system upgrades, new interiorfinishes, lighting, acoustical ceilings, flooring, and code-compliantrestrooms. Existing storefronts, doors, frames, glazing, and louvers will bereplaced as necessary, including UL 752 Level 3-rated assemblies at designatedsecure locations. Interior improvements will provide secure, acousticallyprivate spaces for UPD operations. Select walls in public-facing areas willreceive ballistic protection, while additional security enhancements will beincorporated to protect areas requiring CJIS (Criminal Justice InformationServices) compliance, including the Property and Evidence Vault.
