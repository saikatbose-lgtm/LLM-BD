## Description

Spalding County seeks a qualified consultant or multidisciplinary team experienced in planning, zoning, development regulation, public engagement, historic preservation, and related technical disciplines to evaluate, update, and assist with adoption and implementation of the County's Unified Development Ordinance (UDO). The consultant shall provide the professional personnel, analysis, drafting, mapping, coordination, and training required to fulfill the terms and conditions of this RFP.
