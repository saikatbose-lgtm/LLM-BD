## Description

McIntosh County is soliciting proposals to provide all labor, equipment, materials, and appurtenances necessary to complete patching, leveling, resurfacing, and striping for approximately ± 4.27 (LM) of county-maintained roadways. The work includes patching, leveling and resurfacing Belle Hammock Road from Pine Harbor Road to Anchor Road; patching, OGI leveling, and resurfacing Pine Harbor Road from the Shellman Bluff Road intersection to Belle Hammock Road; and base preparation and paving of Country Woods Road from SR 99 extending approximately 1,000 feet.
