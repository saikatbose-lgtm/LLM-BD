## Description

Southwestern Community College (SCC) is requesting quotes from qualified planning and consulting firms to produce a comprehensive report and prioritized Facilities Capital Project List aligned with institutional capacity and regional workforce demand. 1. Target Program Sectors The analysis and resulting capital project recommendations must focus specifically on three priority areas: • Healthcare • Manufacturing • Skilled Trades 2. Required Data Sources The selected firm must synthesize data from the following sources to justify all recommendations: • Regional Labor Market Data: Western North Carolina Labor Market Intelligence portal ([https://wnc.juliuslmi.com/](https://wnc.juliuslmi.com/)) • Regional Assessment: Hanover Research – Regional Needs Assessment (dated May 2026), attached to this RFQ • Institutional Needs & Baseline: Existing program offerings, enrollment trends, and current space/facility constraints provided by SCC leadership during a virtual kickoff session 3. Core Deliverables • Workforce-to-Facilities Gap Analysis Report: A concise narrative and data synthesis translating regional labor projections in Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Skilled Trades into specific instructional space, lab, and infrastructure requirements for SCC. • Prioritized Facilities Capital Project List: An actionable, itemized list of recommended capital projects (such as new construction, building renovations, or specialized lab/bay buildouts). Each item must include a clear scope description, strategic rationale tied to the required data sources, and priority tiering (short-, mid-, or long-term). • Preliminary Budget Estimates: Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM) cost estimates for each proposed capital project to assist SCC with capital planning and funding requests. (Note: Remove this bullet if you only want project scopes without cost projections.) 4. Quote Submission Requirements To ensure comparable proposals, vendors must submit: • A lump-sum, all-inclusive price covering all research, analysis, meetings, and final deliverables. • A proposed project timeline with key milestones. • A brief summary of relevant experience in community college or higher education facilities master planning. • Confirmation of whether the quote is based on remote analysis or includes on-site campus walkthroughs.
