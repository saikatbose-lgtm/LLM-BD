## Description

The Department of General Services (DGS) is seeking proposals from qualified Offerors to create, develop, implement, and administer a Job Order Contracting (JOC) Program, and to provide construction management services. The selected Offeror shall provide an internet-based management information and support system (the “JOC System”), along with a Unit Price Book (UPB) product and technical specifications. This system must enable Commonwealth Agencies to efficiently procure and manage multi-disciplinary contracts for the design, repair, alteration and execution of minor construction projects through the issuance of Job Orders. The JOC System must support the development and issuance of Job Orders based on the pre-priced tasks contained within an online UPB.
