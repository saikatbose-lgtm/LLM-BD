## Description

The City of Lexington, North Carolina is soliciting Statements of Qualifications from qualified Design-Build teams to provide progressive design-build services for the NC I-85 Center Pump Station and Sewer Extension (Project). The selected DB team will provide integrated design, permitting, preconstruction, procurement, construction, startup, commissioning, and closeout services under a single design-build relationship, subject to CITY authorization of each phase. The Project is located in the NC I-85 Center / Seven Oaks Drive area near Lexington and Linwood, Davidson County, North Carolina. The work generally includes a new submersible sewer pump station with backup power generation; gravity sewer and associated manholes; an aerial sewer crossing; force main; jack-and-bore work; erosion and sedimentation control; grading and restoration; utility coordination; roadway and right-of-way ...
