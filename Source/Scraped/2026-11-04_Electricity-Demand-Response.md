## Description

The purpose of this Demand Response Services ITQ is to qualify responsible and responsive Curtailment Service Providers (CSP) to facilitate the participation of Commonwealth facilities in Demand Response Programs administered by PJM Interconnection, LLC. In addition, Local Public Procurement Units, as defined by the Commonwealth Procurement Code, may use this contract in accordance with the COSTARS Provision contained within this document. Please click here to view a list of ITQ Contracts and related documents, and search for # 4400034766. If you are already registered as a qualified Supplier on the existing Electricity Demand Response ITQ 4400016352, you can use your current PA JAGGAER Portal registration and update it by adding the new 83100000-ITQ-628 Commodity Code and retaining the existing 83100000-ITQ-191 Commodity Code. It is recommended not to create an additional JAGGAER Portal account. First, suppliers must register as a Procurement Vendor with the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and receive a six-digit vendor number if never having done business with the Commonwealth previously.
