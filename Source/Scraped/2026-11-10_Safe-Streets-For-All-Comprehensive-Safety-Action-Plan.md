## Description

Concho Valley Council of Governments (“CVCOG”) is soliciting Proposals from qualified and experienced Respondents to provide a SAFE STREETS FO ALL COMPREHENSIVE SAFETY ACTION PLAN in accordance with the requirements set forth in this Solicitation (the submitting parties “Respondents” and the submissions “Proposals”). The objective of this Solicitation is to identify and contract with a Respondent that demonstrates the capability, expertise, and resources necessary to deliver high-quality services/products that meet the operational and performance needs of CVCOG. The successful Respondent will be selected based on their demonstrated ability to satisfy the evaluation criteria described in this document. All Proposals must be submitted in accordance with the instructions contained herein.
