## Description

Eligible entities are invited to submit a proposal to launch and administer the Athens-Clarke County Affordable Housing Fund (ACCAHF). The goal of the ACCAHF is to help address critical affordable housing needs in Athens-Clarke County, through revolving loan programs that result in the creation and preservation of affordable homes. The selected entity will provide services per the scope of services as indicated in Section III of this RFP. ACCGov will consider selecting a single entity with proven relevant experience in both areas, or a joint application from multiple entities whose combined proven experience meets experience requirements.
