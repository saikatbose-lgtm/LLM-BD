## Description

The Douglas County Board of Commissioners (DCBOC) is seeking sealed proposals to provide Indirect Cost Allocation Services for Douglas County Board of Commissioners. The Douglas County Board of Commissioners (DCBOC or 'County') is seeking sealed proposals from qualified firms to provide Indirect Cost Allocation Plan Services. The selected firm will assist the County in reviewing, developing, preparing, and maintaining an accurate and compliant indirect cost allocation plan in accordance with applicable federal and state regulations, accounting standards, and cost allocation guidelines.
