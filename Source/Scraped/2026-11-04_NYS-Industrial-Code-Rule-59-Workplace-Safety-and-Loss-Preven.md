## Description

NYSIF seeks qualified bidders to provide NYS Industrial Code Rule No. 59 workplace safety and loss prevention consultation and evaluation services. Bidders must be certified as a Workplace Safety and Loss Prevention Consultant by NYS DOL. Selected firms will serve NYSIF policyholders notified by NYCIRB of required participation in the CR 59 Mandatory Safety and Loss Prevention Program, with referrals coinciding with quarterly NYCIRB citation lists. Responses to inquiries posted 12/2/2026.

## Relevance

Occupational safety/loss-prevention consulting requiring NYS DOL certification; specialized safety consulting rather than management, IT or scientific/technical consulting under the approved list. Unmatched (could be revisited if 'Consulting Services' is read broadly).
