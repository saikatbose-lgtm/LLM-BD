## Description

The City of Hampton ("City") invites qualified consulting firms to submit proposals to conduct a comprehensive Classification and Compensation Study. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the City's current compensation structure, position classifications, internal equity, market competitiveness, and compensation administration practices. The selected consultant will provide recommendations that support the City's goals of attracting, retaining, and motivating a qualified workforce while ensuring fiscal responsibility and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
