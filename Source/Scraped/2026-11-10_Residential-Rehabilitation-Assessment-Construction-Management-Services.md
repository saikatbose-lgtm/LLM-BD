## Description

Rockdale County seeks proposals from qualified residential rehabilitation consultants to provide property assessments, repair specifications, cost estimates, construction procurement support, construction monitoring, and project closeout services for the County's Repair & Revitalize Program. The selected consultant will assist the County in delivering safe, decent, code-compliant housing repairs to eligible owner-occupied, single-family homes in accordance with the approved CHIP grant, applicable Georgia DCA requirements, federal HOME regulations, and County policies.
