## Description

The North East Texas Workforce Development Board (hereinafter referred to as “Board”), d.b.a. Workforce Solutions Northeast Texas, a501(c)3 not-for-profit, tax-exempt organization, in compliance with Texas lawsSB 642 and HB 1863, is the local policy making body for the Northeast TexasWorkforce Development Area (WDA). TheBoard is comprised of a 27-member board approved by the Texas WorkforceCommission. This regional, policy-making board is made up of a majority ofbusiness persons and also has representation from labor, education and economicdevelopment concerns to plan and oversee operation of the public workforcesystem, of which child care services is a part, in the 9-county local workforcearea.

## Relevance

Fiscal monitoring of workforce board subrecipients (NIGP 91804 accounting/auditing, 91887 procurement consulting) — financial audit/monitoring work, not IT/staffing/management consulting under the approved list. (ESBD status: Addendum Posted — still an open solicitation with an addendum.)
