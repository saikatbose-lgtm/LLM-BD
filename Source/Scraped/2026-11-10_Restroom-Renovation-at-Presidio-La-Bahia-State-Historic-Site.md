## Description

Restroom Renovation at Presidio La Bahia State Historic Site(Solicitation No. IFB 808-27-270293). The Texas Historical Commission(THC) is seeking competitive, sealed bids from qualified vendors with a minimumof 5 years of experience to provide comprehensive restroom renovation andflooring restoration services at the Presidio La Bahia State Historic Site in Goliad, Texas. Theselected contractor will provide all labor, equipment, and materials necessaryto perform demolition, plumbing drain line scoping, sub-floor corrections tomeet Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS), restroom Saltillo re-tiling, and abaseline 25-square-foot tile replacement in the hallway.

 

Bidders must physically attend one of the two mandatorysite walkthrough options at 217 US-183, Goliad, TX 77963, scheduled foreither Tuesday, November 17, 2026 or Wednesday, November 18, 2026from 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM CST, and must submit a signed Exhibit X Site VisitForm to remain eligible for an award.

 

All technical or administrative inquiries must be submittedin writing to Purchaser Christi Duve at christi.duve@thc.texas.gov  by November 19, 2026.
