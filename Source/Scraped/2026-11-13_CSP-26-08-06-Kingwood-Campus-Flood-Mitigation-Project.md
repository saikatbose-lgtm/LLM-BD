## Description

NOTICE TO OFFERORS The Lone Star College System issues this Competitive Sealed Proposal (CSP) for the procurement of construction services necessary for the construction and implementation of a new continuous structural flood wall and passive flood gates. The project is intended to provide flood protection for ten (10) campus buildings previously damaged by Hurricane Harvey. All prices and/or discounts are to be effective from the time of submission until completion of the project and acceptance by the System. Prices for items requiring delivery are to include delivery charges FOB Lone Star College.
