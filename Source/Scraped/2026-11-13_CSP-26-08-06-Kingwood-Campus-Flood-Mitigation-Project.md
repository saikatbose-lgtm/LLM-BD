## Description

NOTICE TO OFFERORS

The Lone Star College System issues this Competitive Sealed Proposal (CSP) for the procurement of construction services necessary for the construction and implementation of a new continuous structural flood wall and passive flood gates.

The project is intended to provide flood protection for ten (10) campus buildings previously damaged by Hurricane Harvey.

All prices and/or discounts are to be effective from the time of submission until completion of the project and acceptance by the System. Prices for items requiring delivery are to include delivery charges FOB Lone Star College.

This agreement is subject to the appropriation of funds by the System in its budget adopted for any fiscal year for the specific purpose of making payments pursuant to the Agreement. The obligations of the System in any fiscal year for which this agreement is in effect shall constitute a current expense of the System for that fiscal year only and shall not constitute an indebtedness of the System beyond that fiscal year. In the event of non-appropriation of funds in any fiscal year to make payments pursuant to this Agreement, this Agreement may be terminated. The quantities listed are estimates and should not be construed as a guarantee of either minimum or maximum, as purchases are dependent on actual need and available funds. Official LSCS purchase orders will be issued on an as-needed basis.
