## Description

The City is seeking submissions from highly qualified contractors to provide Stormwater Repair, Maintenance, and Inspection Services throughout the City, as directed. The successful Respondent ("Contractor") will provide scheduled maintenance, upkeep, and operation of City owned facilities. The Contractor shall employ and appropriately train a sufficient number of employees to provide efficient and timely services on behalf of the City. The Contractor shall assume responsibility for the cost of employing the workforce to maintain the City's facilities. The Contractor shall have current registration with GDOT as a Qualified Routine Maintenance Contractor in the following work class: Drainage Rehabilitation, Repair, Replacement, and Miscellaneous Maintenance.
