## Description

The Unified Government of Athens-Clarke County desires to solicit qualifications and competitive proposals from responsible and qualified contractors to provide comprehensive Preconstruction and Construction Management services for the proposed Judicial Center and, as an additive alternate, the proposed Parking Deck, both to be constructed in Athens-Clarke County. The proposing firms will provide services per the scope of services as indicated in Section IV of the attachment this Request for Qualifications / Request for Proposals.
