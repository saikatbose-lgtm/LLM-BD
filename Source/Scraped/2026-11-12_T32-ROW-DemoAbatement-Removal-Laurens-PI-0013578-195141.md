## Description

This solicitation is open to potential bidders who currently hold prequalification status for Right of Way Property Management Services issued by GDOT and who hold a current prequalification certificate. Potential bidders who have not been prequalified by GDOT should visit www.dot.ga.gov. Once on the website select "Doing Business with GDOT" and then  "Prequalification" in the Business Opportunities tab. On the Prequalification page select "Right-of-Way Services" to obtain the application, information and instructions. A bid response received by any firm not currently listed on GDOTs prequalification listing will not be considered for award for this solicitation. The Georgia Department of Transportation (GDOT) is seeking a PRE-QUALIFIED firm interested in providing Demolition and Removal of Improvements, Structures or Other Encumbrances in the Right of Way or Easement Areas. GDOT will select the lowest, responsive and responsible bidder based on analysis of bid prices received. Bidders are responsible for checking this solicitation for any/all updates. It is the bidders responsibility to ensure that its bid(s) arrive on or before the specified time. No bids will be accepted after the time set for receipt. CAUTION: A RESTRICTION OF COMMUNICATION IS IN EFFECT FOR THIS PROJECT. From the advertisement date of this solicitation until a successful respondent is selected and the selection is announced, respondents are not allowed to communicate about this solicitation or scope with any staff of GDOT, including the Commissioner, and Board Members, except for submission of questions as instructed in the ITB, or as provided by any existing work agreement(s). For violation of this provision, GDOT reserves the right to reject the submittal of the offending Respondent.
