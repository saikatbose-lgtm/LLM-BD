## Description

Beaufort County has been awarded a grant under the North Carolina Department of Commerce, Rural Economic Development Division (REDD), Community Development Block Grant – Neighborhood Revitalization (CDBG-NR) program, Grant No. 24-C-4217. The project will reconstruct eligible owner-occupied housing units for low- and moderate-income households in Beaufort County. The County is soliciting competitive proposals from qualified firms to provide grant administration and construction oversight services for the full grant period.
