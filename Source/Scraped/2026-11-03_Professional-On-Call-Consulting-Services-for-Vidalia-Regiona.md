## Description

The City of Vidalia Regional Airport is seeking qualified aviation-consulting firms to provide services supporting the Airport Capital Improvement plan at the Vidalia Regional Airport. Two qualification packages are requested: 1) Planning and environmental services; and, 2) Professional on-call engineering, architectural, and construction inspection/administration services.
