## Description

Nevada State University is seeking experienced, qualified professional consulting firms to develop a comprehensive Infrastructure Inventory and Atlas for its approximately 512-acre campus in Henderson, Nevada. The intent is to select a firm to inventory and map existing campus infrastructure, evaluate utility capacities and constraints, perform surveying and base mapping, and prepare coordinated CAD/GIS files and an Infrastructure Atlas to support future campus development, capital projects, operations, and deferred maintenance planning. No pre-submittal meeting or site walk.

## Relevance

Unmatched: Although the solicitation references CAD/GIS deliverables, the underlying scope of work is civil/surveying engineering consulting — physical utility mapping, capacity analysis, and land surveying for campus infrastructure planning. CAD/GIS here are tools used within a civil engineering and surveying engagement, not a genuine "Computer Network Consulting" or "Programming Services, Computer" IT engagement as intended by the approved keyword list. This is an engineering/surveying professional services contract, outside the approved scope.
