MassTech is issuing this NOFO for the Rural Health Cybersecurity Grant Program to solicit responses from rural health facilities interested in receiving grants to fund cybersecurity services, which include assessments, Managed Detection and Response, and other related managed cybersecurity services. This NOFO is funded by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services under the Rural Health Transformation Program.
