## Description

The purpose of this Invitation for Bids (IFB) is to seek competitive sealed bids from qualified vendors to provide trained Emergency Response Support Personnel to augment and/or perform evaluation of existing staff. These Response Support Personnel will provide supplemental assistance/evaluation in all areas of emergency management as required, to include but not limited to the State Emergency Response Team, State Emergency Operations Center (SEOC), supporting coordination facilities, Logistics and Supply, Operations, Planning and Recovery. The Response Support Personnel may be required to perform their assignments at the State Emergency Operations Center (SEOC), alternate support locations, or in the actual impacted areas of the state. NCEM is a government organization that administers state and federal emergency management programs enabled by the Federal Stafford Act and Chapter 166A of the North Carolina General Statutes. Proposals shall be submitted in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Request for Proposal (RFP) and any addenda issued hereto.
