## Description

Harnett County Schools invites single prime bids on the Project known as the “Flatwoods Middle School Roadway Improvements”. Sealed bids will be received at the office of the Harnett County School Central Offices, 601 South Main Street, Lillington, NC 27546, until 3:00 PM on Thursday, December 10, 2026, for the construction of the indicated Project. All bids will be opened and read aloud starting at 3:05 PM in the Board Room. This Project will be bid and awarded in accordance with G.S143-128 and G.S. 143-129. The Work of the Project includes, but is not limited to the construction of a roundabout intersection with associated stormwater and waterline improvements. A Pre-Bid Conference will be held on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 3:30 PM at the Flatwoods Middle School facility located at 3600 US-401, Lillington, NC 27546. The meeting will convene at the Metcon construction trailer. A tour of the Project site will commence immediately after the meeting. All requests for information (RFIs) or questions regarding this solicitation must be submitted in writing no later than Thursday, November 24, 2026. Responses and clarifications, as applicable, will be issued through official addenda. The final addendum will be issued no later than Monday, November 30, 2026. All addenda will be posted on the Harnett County Schools website under Bid Opportunities and shall become part of the official bid documents. All questions after that time shall be submitted via email to the Design Consultant at SfL+a Architects, 421 Fayetteville Street, Suite 1450, Raleigh, NC 27601 via email to: mkick@sfla.biz . Contract Documents, including plans and specifications, may be obtained from the Harnett County Schools website at: https://www.harnett.k12.nc.us/about-us/building-projects-and-bid-opportunities, or complete plans and specifications may be examined at the offices of SfL+a Architects, 421 Fayetteville Street, Suite 1450, Raleigh, NC 27601 during normal office hours beginning Wednesday, November 4, 2026. Complete digital plans and specification are available on request by email to: mkick@sfla.biz .
