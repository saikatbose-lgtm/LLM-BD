## Description

The Savannah-Chatham County Public School System (SCCPSS) seeks sealed proposals from qualified vendors to provide a comprehensive, turnkey video surveillance solution utilizing Motorola Solutions security camera and video management technologies as the district's preferred manufacturer and platform standard. The intent of this solicitation is to replace, upgrade, and expand the district's security camera infrastructure with a modern, scalable, centrally managed surveillance system that enhances safety, security, situational awareness, and operational effectiveness across District facilities.

## Relevance

Turnkey Motorola video surveillance (security camera) solution for SCCPSS - physical security systems, not IT services/staffing.
