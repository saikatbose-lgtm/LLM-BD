## Description

Henry County is seeking bids from contractors to provide a fully operational incinerator suitable for the disposal of animal carcasses, destruction of condemned evidence, and destruction of controlled substances. The selected Contractor shall be responsible for the removal and proper disposal of the existing unit and the furnishing, delivery, installation, configuration, testing, and commissioning of the new incinerator in accordance with all applicable requirements and specifications of this solicitation. The Contractor shall furnish all materials, labor, tools, equipment, and services necessary in the performance of this Bid. Bids will be accepted until 3:00 PM local time on December 1, 2026.
