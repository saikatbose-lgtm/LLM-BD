## Description

The City of Sacramento is proposing to repair the Tangerine Avenue Bridge over Elder Creek (Bridge No.24C0293) by installing concrete slope paving to mitigate future scour and protect the bridge foundations as well as repair of roughly five hundred feet of channel lining east of the bridge. The City of Sacramento is proposing to repair the Watt Avenue Bridge over Arcade Creek (Bridge No.24C0177) by reconstructing the slope paving, extending the end diaphragms, installing structure approach slabs and drainage systems.
