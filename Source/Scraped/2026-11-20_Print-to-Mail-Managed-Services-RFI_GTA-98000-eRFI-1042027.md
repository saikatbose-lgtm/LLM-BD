## Description

The primary objective of this RFI is to solicit information from Print-to-Mail Service providers who possess the expertise, resources, and technologies necessary to deliver a comprehensive solution tailored to the state's needs. We seek to gain insight to assess market interest and supplier capabilities for providing Print-to-Mail Services within a managed services environment similar to the Georgia Enterprise Technology Services (GETS) program. The anticipated scope includes centralized print production, document processing, mail preparation, postage management, mailing and distribution services, and a limited volume of courier services to support specific agency operational requirements. The primary objective of this RFI is to determine supplier interest and market readiness to deliver these services under a managed services model that emphasizes operational efficiency, service-level accountability, security, cost transparency, and consistent service delivery across multiple governmental customers. This RFI is for market research and planning purposes only. It is not a solicitation for proposals or an offer to contract. Responses will assist GTA in evaluating available market capabilities and determining the most appropriate approach for a potential future competitive procurement.
