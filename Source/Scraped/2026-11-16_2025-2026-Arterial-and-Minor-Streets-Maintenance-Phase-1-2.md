## Description

The project entails street maintenance improvements within various locations of the City of Riverside. The project generally includes but is not limited to cold milling of existing deteriorated asphalt pavement, the application of ARAM; CSPB; resurfacing asphalt pavement with DGAC; slurry sealing streets, repairing damaged concrete sidewalks, driveways, pedestrian ramps, curb/gutters, and cross gutters; landscaping; irrigation; the installation of gravel trenches; with the replacement of traffic striping; and signage enhancements.
