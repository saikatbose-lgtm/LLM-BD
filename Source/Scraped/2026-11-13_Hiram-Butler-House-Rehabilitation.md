## Description

Project site is 2382 Pine Mountain Road, Kennesaw, GA 30152. Principal items include exterior repair work of the brick envelope, painting, reroofing, structural repairs, and mechanical and electrical upgrades to the Hiram Butler House at Smith Gilbert Gardens. Total stated cost limitation for the project is $550,000, inclusive of pre-construction, construction, contingency, and other owner associated costs. Within the total stated cost limitation, a maximum of $350,000 is available for all construction work excluding HVAC and HVAC-related work.
