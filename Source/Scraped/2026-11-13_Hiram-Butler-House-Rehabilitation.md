## Description

Project site is 2382 Pine Mountain Road, Kennesaw, GA 30152. Principal items include exterior repair work of the brick envelope, painting, reroofing, structural repairs, and mechanical and electrical upgrades to the Hiram Butler House at Smith Gilbert Gardens. Total stated cost limitation for the project is $550,000, inclusive of pre-construction, construction, contingency, and other owner associated costs. Within the total stated cost limitation, a maximum of $350,000 is available for all construction work excluding HVAC and HVAC-related work. This $350,000 limitation is fixed and shall not be increased by any savings or unused funds associated with the HVAC and HVAC-related scope. Funds allocated to HVAC and HVAC-related work shall remain separate from the $350,000 limitation applicable to all other construction work. The construction manager at risk will be evaluated and selected through a qualification-based process. A full RFQ Package stating the full Response requirements can be obtained from the Kennesaw City Clerk¿s Office, upon request to Lea Alvarez, City Clerk, via email at lalvarez@kennesaw-ga.gov . Only a Response submitted by a Firm on record with the City Clerk¿s Office as having received the RFQ Package from the Issuing Office will be opened. Responses must be submitted in a sealed envelope bearing on the outside the name of the Firm, the Firm¿s address, and the name of the Project for which the Response is submitted. If forwarded by mail, the sealed envelope containing the Response must be enclosed in another envelope addressed as specified above. Responses will be received by the City of Kennesaw, Georgia at the office of the City Clerk located in Kennesaw City Hall at 2529 J.O. Stephenson Avenue, Kennesaw, Georgia, 30144 until: Friday, December 11, 2026, at 1:00 p.m. Local Time. Responses will be opened and the names read aloud. The Owner reserves the right to reject any or all Responses, to waive formalities, and re-advertise.
