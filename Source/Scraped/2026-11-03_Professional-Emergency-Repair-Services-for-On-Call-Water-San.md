## Description

The City of Canton ('City') is requesting proposals for qualified construction contractors to submit proposals to provide on-call emergency and repair services for the City's water distribution, sanitary sewer, and stormwater drainage systems. The general scope includes the following but is not limited to: Availability: Emergency response services must be available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year (including weekends and holidays) and contractors must be capable of mobilizing and arriving on-site within four (4) hours of initial notification.
