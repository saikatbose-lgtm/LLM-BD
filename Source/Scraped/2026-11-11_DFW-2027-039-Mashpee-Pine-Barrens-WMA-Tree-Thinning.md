## Description

The Massachusetts Division of Fisheries and Wildlife (MassWildlife) is soliciting bids from Contractors to conduct woodland thinning during Fiscal Year 2027 (FY27) on up to approximately 35 acres at the Mashpee Pine Barrens Wildlife Management Area (WMA) and adjacent Town of Mashpee lands located in Mashpee, Massachusetts. This work will provide important woody fuel reduction for future facilitation of prescribed fire within the pine barrens to promote restoration of this rare natural community.
