## Description

The Cobb County School District (CCSD) is seeking proposals from qualified vendors to provide removal and disposal of hazardous waste services that shall include the segregation, packaging, transport, and disposal of surplus and waste chemicals, contaminated debris, paints, biological specimens, old custodial liquid supplies, and other types of regulated and non-regulated waste.
