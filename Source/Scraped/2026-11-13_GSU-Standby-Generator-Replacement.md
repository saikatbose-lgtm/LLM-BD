## Description

Georgia State University is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified contractors for the removal of the existing standby generator and installation of a new standby generator system at the WRAS Panthersville Radio Tower facility. The project includes providing all labor, supervision, engineering, equipment, materials, rigging, transportation, permits, and other services necessary to deliver a complete, fully operational replacement standby power system. Work includes existing generator removal, new generator installation, structural evaluation of the elevated steel support platform, electrical integration, fuel system verification, startup, testing, commissioning, training, and project closeout. The successful contractor shall provide a complete turnkey installation and shall be responsible for verifying all existing site conditions prior to equipment procurement. Bidders shall submit a total turnkey project price. MANDATORY SITE VISIT Date: November 24, 2026Time: 10:00 AMLocation: GSU WRAS Panthersville Radio Tower - 2401 Wildcat Road, Decatur, GA 30034 Attendance at the site visit is mandatory. Suppliers must attend to remain eligible to submit a bid. BID SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS All bids and all required supporting documentation, must be submitted electronically through Jaggaer Sourcing Director by the solicitation closing date and time. Bids submitted by email, in person, or through any other method will not be accepted.
