## Description

Georgia State University is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified contractors for the removal of the existing standby generator and installation of a new standby generator system at the WRAS Panthersville Radio Tower facility. The project includes providing all labor, supervision, engineering, equipment, materials, rigging, transportation, permits, and other services necessary to deliver a complete, fully operational replacement standby power system.
