## Description

Georgia State University is soliciting competitive sealed bids from qualified contractors for the removal of the existing standby generator and installation of a new standby generator system at the WRAS Panthersville Radio Tower facility. The project includes providing all labor, supervision, engineering, equipment, materials, rigging, transportation, permits, and other services necessary to deliver a complete, fully operational replacement standby power system. Work includes existing generator removal, new generator installation, structural evaluation of the elevated steel support platform, electrical integration, fuel system verification, startup, testing, commissioning, training, and project closeout.
