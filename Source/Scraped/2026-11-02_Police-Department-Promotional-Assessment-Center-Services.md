## Description

The City of Lubbock is soliciting proposals for Police Department Promotional Assessment Center Services.Through this RFP, the City seeks a qualified independent consultant capable of providing a consistent, secure, objective, job-related, valid, reliable, and professionally defensible Assessment Center process that complements the City’s Police promotional examination process and provides qualified candidates a fair opportunity to demonstrate their readiness for promotion.
