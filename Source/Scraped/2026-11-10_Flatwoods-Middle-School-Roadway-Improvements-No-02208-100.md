## Description

Harnett County Schools invites single prime bids on the Project known as the “Flatwoods Middle School Roadway Improvements”. Sealed bids will be received at the office of the Harnett County School Central Offices, 601 South Main Street, Lillington, NC 27546, until 3:00 PM on Thursday, December 10, 2026, for the construction of the indicated Project.
