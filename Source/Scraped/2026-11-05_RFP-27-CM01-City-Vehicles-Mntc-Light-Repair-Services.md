## Description

The City of Milton is requesting sealed proposals from qualified Offerors with demonstrated professional competence and experience to provide for the fleet vehicle maintenance and light repair services as specified in the Scope of Work (Section 3). The awarded contractor(s) must be located within a five (5)-mile radius of Milton city limits and have a location of suitable size and personnel to handle the repair and maintenance of the fleet vehicles without undue delay. The City of Milton may and prefers to award multiple non-exclusive contracts. However, the City reserves the right to award the RFP to one Contractor if needed. The City desires a three (3) year agreement which is renewed annually. All qualified proposals will receive consideration without regard to age, handicap, religion, creed or belief, political affiliation, race, color, sex, or national origin. The City of Milton reserves the right to reject any or all bid/proposals and to waive technicalities and informalities, and to make award in the best interest of the City of Milton.
