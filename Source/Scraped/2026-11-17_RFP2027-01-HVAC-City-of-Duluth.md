## Description

The City of Duluth will receive proposals from contractors for the following scope of work: Task I) Remove existing basement AHU and replace with new 15-ton AHU including necessary VAV Controls, dehumidification, economizer with powered exhaust, connect existing electrical and low voltage controls, integrate into the control system outlined in Task II. Task II) Replacement of HVAC Controls. The existing Carrier DDC controls (Carrier Control Network) for the Duluth City Hall have been found to be obsolete and failing from a mechanical standpoint.
