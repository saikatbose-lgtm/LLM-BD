## Description

The City of Duluth will receive proposals from contractors for the following scope of work: Task I) Remove existing basement AHU and replace with new 15-ton AHU including necessary VAV Controls, dehumidification, economizer with powered exhaust, connect existing electrical and low voltage controls, integrate into the control system outlined in Task II. Task II) Replacement of HVAC Controls. The existing Carrier DDC controls (Carrier Control Network) for the Duluth City Hall have been found to be obsolete and failing from a mechanical standpoint. A new open-source BAS (Building Automation System) will be required to replace the existing Carrier CNN system. The project involves a full upgrade of the controls system of the Duluth City Hall HVAC system. This scope includes the following: (5) air handling/rooftop units, (57) associated VAV units, and (1) exhaust fan. Any additional units are to be considered existing to remain and controlled locally. Specified units are to be provided with updated controls and tied into the new open-source DDC system. The new front end must contain the same information and functionality as the existing equipment including, but not limited to, equipment status, mode of operation, setpoints, airflow, temperatures, occupancy, and alarms.
