## Description

The Georgia World Congress Center Authority ('Authority') will receive responses from firms specializing in the removal and replacement of existing sheet vinyl flooring with commercia-grade, no-slip safety flooring in designated corridors of the Thomas Murphy Ballroom area. The Authority retains the right to reject any or all submittals, and to waive technicalities, irregularities, and informalities, or cancel this procurement at any time. IMPORTANT- A RESTRICTION OF COMMUNICATION IS IN EFFECT FOR THIS PROJECT.
