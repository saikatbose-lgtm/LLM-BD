## Description

The White County Board of Commissioners is issuing this Request for Proposal to solicit proposals from qualified vendors for the purchase, installation, implementation, configuration, training, support and ongoing maintenance of a Next Generation 911 (NG911) compliant call handling and telephone system for White County 911 Communications. The proposed system must provide a modern, reliable, scalable and user-friendly platform that supports emergency and administrative call handling, text-to-911, caller location information and continuity of operations.

## Relevance

Purchase, installation and support of an NG911 call-handling telephone system - public-safety telephony equipment, not IT services/staffing.
