## Description

HRA is soliciting proposals to provide funding solely for the congregate (single-site) supportive housing model under the NYC 15/15 initiative. The RFP covers support services only, not rental assistance. Proposed contract term is five years with one option to renew for up to four years. A "Scope of Work - Congregate Housing" document is attached; 13 addenda plus a Community Hiring Rider have been posted. Vendors need a PASSPort account and an approved HHS Prequalification application to respond.
