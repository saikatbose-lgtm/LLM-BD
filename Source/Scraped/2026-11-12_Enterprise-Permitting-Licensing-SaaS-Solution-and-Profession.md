## Description

The City of San Diego’s Development Services Department is seeking proposals to procure, implement, and provide ongoing support for a fully integrated, cloud based permitting solution. The system must enable end to end processing of permits, code enforcement, and licensing across building, engineering, and land development operations. The selected solution must comply with all applicable California laws and regulations and adhere to the City’s technical standards. (Issue/start date taken from the source field 'Posting Date'.)
