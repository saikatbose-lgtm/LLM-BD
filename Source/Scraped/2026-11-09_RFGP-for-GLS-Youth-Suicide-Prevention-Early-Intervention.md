## Description

This funding opportunity is designed to build capacity for local public health authorities (LPHAs), Local Mental Health Authorities (LMHAs) and/or Community Mental Health Programs (CMHPs) serving jurisdictions with a burden of youth suicide deaths (10-24-year-olds) higher than the national average (between 2020-2022, 10.5/100,000, CDC, 2024).
