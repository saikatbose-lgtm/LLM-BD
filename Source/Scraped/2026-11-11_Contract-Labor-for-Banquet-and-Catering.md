## Description

1.0 Scope of Solicitation – It is the intention of the Cypress-Fairbanks Independent School District (CFISD, the District and/or Owner) to establish an annual contract, on an as needed basis, for: Contract Labor for Banquet and Catering - The purpose of the this proposal is to establish primary and secondary vendors for wait staff, bartenders, banquet servers, prep cooks, dish washers, host/hostess for the Food and Beverage department for the Berry Center and Pridgeon Stadium at CFISD. ** It shall be the contractor's responsibility to ensure the contract laborers arrive on the date specified and on time.** ​ (Issue/start date taken from the source field 'Solicitation Posting Date'.)
