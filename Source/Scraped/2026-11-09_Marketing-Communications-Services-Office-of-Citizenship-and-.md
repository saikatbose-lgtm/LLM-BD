## Description

The Office of Citizenship and Service requires a marketing and communications “teammate” to execute and support the Citizens for Good program, Texas A&M University's aggregated effort to prepare Aggies to lead, serve, and contribute through civic leadership, Civic Friendship, and service. This scope of work outlines the services required to build, manage, maintain, and grow the program's communications presence across campus and beyond. Services are primarily focused on the ongoing maintenance, enhancement, and continuous improvement of the program's website, social media channels, newsletters, and student engagement initiatives that encourage participation and progression through the Citizens for Good credentialing pathway.
