## Description

The travel training contractor will provide staff to teach people with disabilities how to use SamTrans and Caltrain services. The contractor who teaches people with visual disabilities will provide staff who are orientation and mobility specialist. The contractor who teaches people with physical, cognitive and other disabilities will provide staff who have experience working with people with disabilities. The District, subject to Board of Directors' approval, intends to award a seven (5)-year base term contract, for Services to one (1) Proposer.
