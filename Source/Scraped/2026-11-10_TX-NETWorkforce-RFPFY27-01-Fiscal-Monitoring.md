## Description

The North East Texas Workforce Development Board, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit policy-making body for the Northeast Texas Workforce Development Area, is soliciting proposals via RFP FY27-01 for fiscal monitoring services. The Board oversees workforce development programs (including a child-care-services component) across a 9-county area and requires a contractor to perform fiscal monitoring/oversight of its programs.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** The NIGP codes (Accounting/Auditing/Budget Consulting, Procurement Consulting, Finance/Economics Consulting) and the title ("Fiscal Monitoring") show this is an accounting/financial-audit consulting engagement — reviewing the Board's fiscal/grant compliance — not IT services, staffing, data processing, or general business/management consulting as contemplated by the approved keyword list. It is a specialized financial-audit consulting scope, which falls outside the approved filters.
