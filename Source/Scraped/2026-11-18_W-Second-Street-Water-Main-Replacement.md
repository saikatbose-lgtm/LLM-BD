## Description

The Town desires to avail itself of the services of the Contractor in connection with the replacement & upsizing of 320 LF 4” ACP water main to 6” PVC. Remove & replace (2) 4” Gate Valves to (2) 6” Gate Valves. Removal & replacement of (6) existing water services. Trenches to be patched per TOC MSSD. (4) existing sewer service cuts to be patched, along with 255 LF of existing open cut from water main installation. 0-1.5” milling along W Second St, from S Moore St to Atkinson St.
