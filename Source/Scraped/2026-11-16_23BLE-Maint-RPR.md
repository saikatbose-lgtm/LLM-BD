## Description

The PA Game Commission (PGC), Bureau of Law Enforcement (BLE) is issuing an Invitation for Bid (IFB) to procure the services of a vendor to provide material vehicle repair parts procurement to in conjunction with comprehensive repair and maintenance services for the PGC’s Regional and Statewide Law Enforcement SUV and Truck fleet (State Game Warden and State Game Warden Supervisor patrol vehicles). Interested bidders must acknowledge their facility capacity to securely store, repair and maintain the components outlined within the agency-provided installation and service manual and deliver a finalized product which strictly conforms with current PGC build specs.
