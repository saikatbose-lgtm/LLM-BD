## Description

Truckee Meadows Water Authority (TMWA) is accepting sealed bids for all labor, materials, equipment, and incidentals required for the main replacement project on Marsh Ave between Plumb Ln and St. Lawrence Ave in Reno, NV. Project will consist of installing approximately 617 LF of 6" RJ DI and 3,009 LF of 8" RJ DI, completing approximately 47 service tie-overs, grout filling approximately 2,940 LF of 6" and 8" cast iron main, and removing and disposal of approximately 250 LF of 6" Transite (ACP) main.
