## Description

he City of Atlanta, on behalf of the Department of Watershed Management (DWM), will shortly undergo a Multi-Step Competitive Sealed Bidding process comprised of a qualification phase as the initial step, followed by an Invitation for Bid (IFB), for the procurement of qualified contractors to provide green infrastructure maintenance, installation, and streambank stabilization and restoration services throughout the City of Atlanta and applicable service areas. Interested and qualified vendors must demonstrate relevant experience performing green infrastructure, stormwater infrastructure, environmental restoration, landscape maintenance, installation, and/or streambank stabilization work of similar size, scope, and complexity. Vendors must demonstrate appropriate technical expertise, applicable licenses and certifications, qualified personnel, staffing capability, equipment, and field experience necessary to perform work in regulated and environmentally sensitive environments. Vendors must also demonstrate the operational capacity to perform on-call work across multiple locations while complying with applicable federal, state, and local requirements. The City reserves the right to make multiple awards to vendors determined to be responsible and responsive. A Pre-Solicitation Conference will be noticed and held to provide more details and address comments, questions, and concerns. Hope to see you there.
