## Description

Per TGC 2269 and 437.054, the Texas Military Department (TMD) is seeking competitive sealed proposals from General Contractors to modernize the Texas Army National Guard Camp Mabry facility in Austin, Texas.  The General Contractor shall provide all materials, equipment, labor, coordination, and supervisory activities necessary to complete construction of the project. This project is part of the STAR program and will renovate an approximate 144,458 SF. Work includes general facility sustainment such as refinishing of interior surfaces, exterior doors, and resurfacing of pavement. Restoration of MEP, roof systems, and similar types of work. Modernization to include compliance with ADA standards and current building codes, required ATFP, and requirements of dual gender restrooms. Energy will provide restoration of electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and insulation which is beyond useful life and/or lacked maintenance. The estimated construction budget for the services described in the Request for Proposal (RFP) is estimated Proposal Base is Twenty-Nine Million Dollars ($29,000,000.00) with the estimated Proposal Options Nine Hundred Seventy Thousand ($970,000.00). General Contractors must request via email: FY27Building8RCMM@cfmo.mil.texas.gov the Drawings/Plans and Specifications electronically in digital format.
