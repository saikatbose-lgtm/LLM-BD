## Description

Per TGC 2269 and 437.054, the Texas Military Department (TMD) is seeking competitive sealed proposals from General Contractors to modernize the Texas Army National Guard Camp Mabry facility in Austin, Texas. The General Contractor shall provide all materials, equipment, labor, coordination, and supervisory activities necessary to complete construction of the project. This project is part of the STAR program and will renovate an approximate 144,458 SF. Work includes general facility sustainment such as refinishing of interior surfaces, exterior doors, and resurfacing of pavement.
