## Description

The Borough of Garwood, NJ issued a Request for Qualifications for 2027 Professional Services, soliciting qualification statements from firms/individuals for the following positions/roles: Affordable Housing Administrative Agent, Municipal Auditor, Municipal Bond Attorney, Municipal Attorney, Municipal Labor Attorney, Municipal Tax Appeal Attorney, Redevelopment Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Attorney, Planning/Zoning Board Planner, Planning/Zoning Board Engineer, and Municipal Engineer. The RFQ satisfies the "fair and open process" requirements of N.J.S.A. 19:44A-20.4 et seq.; these professional services are statutorily exempt from standard competitive bidding under N.J.S.A. 40A:11-5(1)(a)(i). Contract term: January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027. Responses (an original plus one copy, plus a flash drive copy) must be submitted in a sealed envelope to the Borough Clerk no later than 11:00 AM on December 11, 2026.

## Relevance

**Unmatched.** After reading the full RFQ document (not just the title), the actual scope of work solicited is a slate of specialized municipal legal, financial-audit, planning, and engineering professional retainers (municipal attorney/bond attorney/labor attorney/tax appeal attorney, redevelopment attorney, municipal auditor, planning/zoning board attorney/planner/engineer, municipal engineer, and an affordable housing administrative agent). None of these roles are IT services, IT staff augmentation, staffing/clerical/administrative support, data/records management, HR services, or any other approved keyword category — they are discrete professional licensure-based services (law, accounting, engineering, planning) carved out under New Jersey's "fair and open" professional-services exemption, not general business/management/administrative consulting support work. No NAICS/NIGP/UNSPSC codes were published on the listing or in the RFQ document to cross-check. The keyword "Professional, Consulting, Administrative and Management Support Services" does not genuinely apply because the underlying work is specialized legal/audit/engineering retainers, not administrative or management-support consulting.
