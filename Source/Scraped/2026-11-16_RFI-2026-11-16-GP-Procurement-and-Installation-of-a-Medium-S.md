## Description

Battelle (M&O contractor for PNNL/DOE) issues this Request for Information to gather industry input on the procurement, installation and operation of a medium-scale superconducting quantum computing system on the PNNL campus. The anticipated contract is a 3-5 year lease agreement with supplier support, award targeted in FY2027. The system will be operated as a scientific instrument for feasibility studies, benchmarking, hybrid quantum-classical workflows and co-design.

## Relevance

Unmatched: this is an RFI for leasing/installing specialized quantum computing hardware (NAICS 532420 equipment leasing) with vendor support - a hardware/scientific-instrument acquisition rather than IT services, staff augmentation or consulting under the approved list.
