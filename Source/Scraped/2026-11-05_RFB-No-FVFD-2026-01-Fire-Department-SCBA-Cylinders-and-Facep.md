## Description

The City of Fort Valley, Georgia, through Danny Lamonte of Municipal Central LLC acting on the City's behalf, invites sealed bids submitted by email for Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA), spare cylinders with valves, and facepieces for the Fort Valley Fire Department. Quantities: 11 SCBA packs, 11 spare carbon-fiber 4,500 psig 45-minute cylinders with valves, and 15 facepieces (size Medium), plus an optional second set of 11 cylinders.
