## Description

The County of Kenosha Wisconsin, through its Purchasing Division, requests bids to establish a contract for shoreline maintenance at the Kemper Center, 6501 3rd Ave, Kenosha, WI 53143. Maintenance improvements for the Kemper and Anderson Center revetment include reconstruction of the existing revetment and an adjacent stone splash apron, raising the revetment crest elevation and rebuilding the slope face.
