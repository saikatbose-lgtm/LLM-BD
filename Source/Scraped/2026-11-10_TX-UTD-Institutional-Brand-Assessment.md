## Description

UT Dallas is seeking a strategic branding partner to assess how key audiences perceive the University and develop a distinctive brand position and messaging framework reflecting its STEM-focused research identity. The selected firm will evaluate the university's existing "The Future Demands Different" campaign, recommend improvements to creative strategy and visual identity, and provide a proven approach to earning brand adoption across the decentralized campus.
