## Description

RFP #Brooks11-12026 Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) Systems The Brooks County Board of Commissioners is soliciting sealed proposals from qualified vendors for the furnishing, delivery, training, and support of forty (40) complete Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) systems for use by Brooks County Fire Rescue personnel. The successful proposer shall provide a complete SCBA solution meeting or exceeding all minimum specifications established in the Request for Proposals.
