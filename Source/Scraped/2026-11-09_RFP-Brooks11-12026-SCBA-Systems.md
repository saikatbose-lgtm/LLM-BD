## Description

RFP #Brooks11-12026 Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) Systems The Brooks County Board of Commissioners is soliciting sealed proposals from qualified vendors for the furnishing, delivery, training, and support of forty (40) complete Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) systems for use by Brooks County Fire Rescue personnel. The successful proposer shall provide a complete SCBA solution meeting or exceeding all minimum specifications established in the Request for Proposals. SCBA units must comply with current NFPA 1981 and NFPA 1982 standards and include full-face positive pressure facepieces, integrated PASS devices, electronic monitoring displays, emergency rescue air-sharing systems, integrated voice communication capability, and two 4,500 PSI composite cylinders per unit with a minimum 45-minute rating. Training, warranty documentation, maintenance recommendations, batteries, and charging equipment are also required. Vendors must be properly licensed, in good standing with the Georgia Secretary of State, and demonstrate experience supplying NFPA-compliant SCBA equipment to public safety agencies. Proposals will be evaluated based on cost, product quality, technical specifications, warranty coverage, service availability, and delivery capability. Equipment must be available for delivery within ninety (90) days of award. This procurement is anticipated to be funded through the FEMA Assistance to Firefighters Grant (AFG) Program and is subject to all applicable federal procurement requirements, including 2 CFR Part 200 and FEMA grant regulations. Proposed equipment must meet current FEMA eligibility requirements and all applicable OSHA and NFPA standards. New equipment only will be accepted. Sealed proposals must be received by the Brooks County Board of Commissioners at 610 South Highland Road, Quitman, Georgia 31643, no later than 11:00 a.m. on December 11, 2026.
