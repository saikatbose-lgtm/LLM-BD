## Description

The purpose of this solicitation is to procure and establish a contract for Garbage and Trash Disposal ' Augusta State Medical Prison (ASMP) for a one-year period with an option of up to four (4) yearly renewals. Services must be performed as scheduled, according to the attached specifications. No services will be performed on weekends or State of Georgia holidays.
