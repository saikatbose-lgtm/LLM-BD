## Description

The Habersham County Board of Education is accepting proposals for Category 2 E-Rate equipment and services, that include a firewall and other networking components to upgrade its internal network infrastructure. This project is contingent upon E-Rate funds and approval of local funds by the Habersham County Board of Education. All quoted prices should be valid until Sept. 30, 2028
