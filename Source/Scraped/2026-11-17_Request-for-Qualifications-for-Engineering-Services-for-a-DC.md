## Description

Public Notice Mecklenburg County (County) is soliciting Statements of Qualifications (SOQs) from firms/teams to provide Professional Engineering Services (Services) for Valerie C. Woodard Center Electric Vehicle DC Fast Chargers in Mecklenburg County. The County is seeking firms whose combination of experience and expertise will provide timely, professional services to the County. The County reserves the right to enter one or more contracts with any firm selected under this RFQ process. Mecklenburg County reserves the right to reject any or all submittals and to waive informalities or technicalities, as it may deem to be in its best interest. The contract resulting from this RFQ will be partially reimbursed with Federal-aid funding through the North Carolina NCDOT of Transportation (NCDOT). The solicitation, selection, and negotiation of a contract shall be conducted in accordance with all NCDOT and federal requirements and guidelines. The contract length is for the duration of the project. The contract aims to provide all engineering and design services for the Valerie C. Woodard Center Electric Vehicle DC Fast Chargers. After the project described in 1.3 is complete, any services will be on an as-needed basis. A firm will not be considered unless the following minimum requirements are met: 1. A firm must be registered with the North Carolina Secretary of State. 2. The engineers performing the work and responsible for the work must be registered Professional Engineers in the State of North Carolina and have good ethical and professional standing. 3. Before the RFQ deadline, the primary and any sub-consultant firm(s) shall be prequalified by the North Carolina Department of Transportation (NCDOT) to perform the required Services. The firm (s) should be prequalified with NCDOT under one or more of the following NCDOT Work Codes, as identified below. Discipline Code Description Discipline Code Number Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Support 222 Electrical Engineering Design 351 General Engineering Services 572 Work Codes for each primary and/or subconsultant firm (s) shall be listed on the respective RS-2 FORMS. All questions about this RFQ must be directed to the County's designated contact person. Attempts to discuss this project with staff other than the contact person noted below may lead to disqualification of your firm.
