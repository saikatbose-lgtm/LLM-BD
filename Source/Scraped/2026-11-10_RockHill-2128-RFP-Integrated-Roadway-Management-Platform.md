## Description

The City of Rock Hill is seeking proposals for a single integrated software platform covering roadway imagery, pavement condition assessment, roadway-asset assessment, maintenance and capital planning, asset management, and work orders. A non-mandatory pre-proposal conference is scheduled for 2026-11-19 at 10:00 AM EST at the City of Rock Hill Operations Center. Responses are submitted electronically via the City's OpenGov procurement portal, which requires a free vendor account.

## Relevance

Matched: the solicited work is the implementation and ongoing operation of an enterprise software platform for asset management, maintenance planning, and work-order management — directly workable under the approved "Asset Management" and "Software Maintenance/Support" keywords, since the scope is a software/IT system procurement with implementation and long-term support rather than a construction/paving contract (the BusyBids trade tag "Paving & Asphalt" does not reflect the actual software scope).
