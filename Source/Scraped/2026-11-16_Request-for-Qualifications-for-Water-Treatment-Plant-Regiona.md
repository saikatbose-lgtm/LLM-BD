## Description

The Cities of Lexington and Thomasville are seeking statements of qualifications (SOQs) from qualified professional engineering firms to conduct a drinking water Merger/Regionalization Feasibility (MRF) Study. Both Cities operate aging water treatment facilities that require substantial rehabilitation, replacement, or modernization. The Cities desire an objective evaluation of independent-system and regional alternatives to determine the long-term technical, financial, operational, managerial, governance, regulatory, resilience, and ratepayer implications of each approach.
