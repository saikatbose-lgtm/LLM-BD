## Description

PURPOSE In order to be in compliance with federal, state and local purchasing laws, the Birdville Independent School District (BISD) is soliciting competitive sealed proposals for Purell Products and Supplies for the district. Based on the 2025-2026 expenditure, the total estimated contract on an annual basis is $253,109.33, the catalog portion of this contract includes services and items that are subject to the maximum purchasing limits established by CH Legal and BISD Board Policy. SCOPE OF WORK BISD is seeking a supplier to provide Purell products and supplies for district-wide use. The District intends to continue using its existing Purell dispensing system and is not seeking an alternative product line or replacement dispensing system.
