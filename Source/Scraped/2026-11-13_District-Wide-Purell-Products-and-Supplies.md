## Description

PURPOSE
In order to be in compliance with federal, state and local purchasing laws, the Birdville Independent School District (BISD) is soliciting competitive sealed proposals for Purell Products and Supplies for the district.  Based on the 2025-2026 expenditure, the total estimated contract on an annual basis is $253,109.33, the catalog portion of this contract includes services and items that are subject to the maximum purchasing limits established by CH Legal and BISD Board Policy.
 
SCOPE OF WORK
BISD is seeking a supplier to provide Purell products and supplies for district-wide use. The District intends to continue using its existing Purell dispensing system and is not seeking an alternative product line or replacement dispensing system. Products offered must be compatible with the District's current Purell dispensers.

Soap and hand sanitizer supplies will be replenished regularly to maintain adequate inventory levels. Annual quantities listed in the bid line items are based on historical usage and are provided for estimation purposes only. BISD does not guarantee minimum or maximum purchase quantities. The District reserves the right to purchase only the products and quantities needed and is not obligated to purchase all items listed in this proposal. Actual purchases may vary based on District needs, available funding, and operational requirements.
