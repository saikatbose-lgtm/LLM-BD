## Description

Pursuant to Arkansas Code §19-65-201, the City of Hot Springs is issuing its annual, statutorily-required call for Statements of Qualifications and Performance Data for "professional services" generally, to be kept on file and evaluated whenever a specific project requiring such services arises during the year. The notice does not name a specific project, scope of work, department, or discipline — it is a general, recurring administrative invitation for any qualified professional-services firm (the kind of filing architects, engineers, surveyors, and similar design/professional disciplines commonly make under this statute) to submit credentials. No specific IT, staffing, or consulting engagement is described.
