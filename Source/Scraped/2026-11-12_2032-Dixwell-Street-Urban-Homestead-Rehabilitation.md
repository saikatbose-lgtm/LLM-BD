## Description

The City of Davenport is accepting proposals for the rehabilitation of one single family home located at 2032 Dixwell Street. This property will be rehabbed with federal funding and once complete will be sold to a qualifying home owner. The City is requesting General Contractors to submit qualifications, pricing, references, schedule and scope of work summary for the rehabilitation.
