## Description

TxDOT is issuing this Invitation for Bid (“Solicitation”) to establish a contract for the purchase of the goods (“Goods”) and/or services (“Services”) described in the Statement of Work. TxDOT invites any qualified party to submit a Response. If the Statement of Work describes only Goods, references to Services will be disregarded, and vice versa. The provision of Goods and Services in accordance with the Statement of Work and performance of all other obligations of Vendor described in the Solicitation, including without limitation in the Form of Contract and the Contract Terms and Conditions, is collectively referred to as the “Work.” The SOW and the Contract Terms and Conditions will be exhibits to the Contract. For purposes of the Solicitation, terms used but not defined in the Statement of Work will have the meaning assigned to them in the Form of Contract and the Contract Terms and Conditions. NOTE: All information or changes related to this solicitation will be posted on this website. Respondents should monitor this website for any addendums, additional documents, questions and answers, or changes. The Response Due Date specified on this posting is the due date for submitting responses. Response submittals will not be accepted after the Response Due Date and Time.
