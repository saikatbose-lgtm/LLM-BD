## Description

The Work to be completed for the 2026 St. Mary's Dr. Storm Improvements & Paving is described briefly as follows: The project consists of removing approx.. 30 LF of existing 24' RCP across a road and replacing it with 30 LF of 30' RCP, removing & replacing 2 double wing catch basins, remove & replace approx.. 100 LF of curb & gutter, cut 2 ex. 15' HDPE storm pipes from the inside that are protruding into ex. 30' CMP and seal the connections. Project includes approx.. 2700 LF of resurfacing/milling & overlay improvements on St Mary's Dr..
