CLARK COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF AVIATION PROCUREMENT & CONTRACTS REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL NUMBER 27-001 CYBERSECURITY MANAGED DETECTION & RESPONSE (MDR) INVITATION TO PROPOSERS The Clark County Department of Aviation (CCDOA) invites qualified firms to submit proposals to deliver continuous, comprehensive managed security services to function as a strategic extension of the Department’s cybersecurity program.
