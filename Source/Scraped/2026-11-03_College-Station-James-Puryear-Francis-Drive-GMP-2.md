## Description

The project includes a multi-component public infrastructure and park improvement project. Phase 2 (GMP#2) of this project includes: the redevelopment of James Parkway, Puryear Drive, and Francis Drive. - Scope includes (but is not limited to): demolition, milling of existing asphalt, earthwork, subgrade stabilization, SWPPP, traffic control, tree protection, Water/Sanitary/Storm utilities & infrastructure upgrades, concrete paving, concrete curbs, concrete sidewalks, concrete driveways, stamped colored concrete, gravel driveways, asphalt transitions, paving sealants, pavement markings, signage, mailboxes, street lighting & power modifications, landscape/hardscape/irrigation repairs.
