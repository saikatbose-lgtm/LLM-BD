## Description

To provide for the facilitation and preparation of a comprehensive 3-year strategic plan for Invest Athens-ACCGov Economic Development Department. The strategic plan will help the department establish organizational priorities, implementation strategies and provide measurable goals and performance metrics to help guide the department's work from 2027 ' 2030.

## Relevance

Athens-Clarke County seeks facilitation and preparation of a 3-year strategic plan for its Invest Athens Economic Development Department, setting organizational priorities, implementation strategies and performance metrics. NIGP 91890 Strategic Planning and Consulting, 91883 Organizational Development Consulting, 96110 Business Plan Development and 96258 Professional Services support a management-consulting match.
