## Description

DISA is seeking quotes for a 1GB commercial telecommunications circuit within Europe. Bidders must be telecommunications providers holding a current DITCO Basic Agreement signed by both the Government and the Contractor before submitting quotes; evaluation is lowest-price, technically acceptable. Not a small-business set-aside.
