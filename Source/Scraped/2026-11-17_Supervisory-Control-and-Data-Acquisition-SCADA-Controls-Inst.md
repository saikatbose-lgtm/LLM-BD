## Description

Under this contract, the Contractor will perform work at the County's water and wastewater treatment collection and distribution facilities as well as any remote monitoring or control stations. The Wastewater facilities include two treatment plants; and approximately sixty-seven lift stations and associated remote facilities. The Drinking Water facilities include one water treatment plant, one raw water intake facility, seven ground storage tanks, five elevated tanks, five water booster stations, one stand pipe and multiple pressure monitoring locations.

## Relevance

DeKalb County contract for SCADA controls and instrumentation work at water/wastewater plants and lift stations. Although NIGP 92064/92045 appear, the real scope is industrial controls/instrumentation field work, not IT services.
