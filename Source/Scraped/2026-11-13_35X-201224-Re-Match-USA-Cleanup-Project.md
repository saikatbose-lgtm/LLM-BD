## Description

The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (“Commonwealth”), Department of Environmental Protection (“DEP”), Bureau of Waste Management is seeking professional services to conduct a removal/clean-up action of solid waste and other materials at the Len’s Concrete Services facility (“Len’s Concrete”). A portion of the property was contracted to Re-Match USA, Inc. as a storage site. Len’s Concrete is located at 1956 US 22, Jonestown, Pennsylvania in Swatara Township, Lebanon County (“Jonestown Facility”). Solid waste and other materials abandoned at the Jonestown Facility include, but are not limited to, approximately 4,000 tons of rolled waste artificial turf, crumb rubber, and sediment controls. Mandatory Pre-Bid Meeting: There will be a mandatory pre-bid site inspection held at the Jonestown Facility, 1956 US 22, Jonestown, PA, 17038 in Swatara Township, Lebanon County at 10:00 AM on Tuesday December 1, 2026. Please RSVP to Brian Franklin via email (brfranklin@pa.gov) to request attendance.  Requests to attend the pre-bid site inspection meeting must be received no later than (Monday November 30th at 12:00 PM). Questions regarding the technical information should be addressed to Jordan Wilson at jordwilson@pa.gov or (610) 916-0145. Questions regarding the bidding or contracting procedures should be directed to Brian Franklin at brfranklin@pa.gov or (717) 787-2471.
