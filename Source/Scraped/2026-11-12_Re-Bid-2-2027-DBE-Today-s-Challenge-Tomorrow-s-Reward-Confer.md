## Description

The Illinois Department of Transportation's Office of Business and Workforce Diversity (OBWD) seeks a venue for the 2027 Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) Conference: Today's Challenge, Tomorrow's Reward. The event provides workshops and networking to help DBE firms pursue federally funded highway contracts and support Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) goals. It will be held on 2 consecutive days between Feb. 24 and Apr. 8, 2027, in Sangamon, McLean, Peoria, or Champaign County.
