## Description

Frederick County (issued by its Division of Interagency Information Technologies) is seeking a configurable enterprise Land Management software system to replace its current system, Infor Public Sector, which is moving to a cloud platform that cannot carry over the county's custom modifications. The new system must support planning, permitting, inspections, and code enforcement, integrate with existing county systems, and come with long-term vendor support. A non-mandatory pre-proposal conference is scheduled 2026-11-17 via Teams. Submission is electronic through the county's OpenGov portal (free vendor account required).
