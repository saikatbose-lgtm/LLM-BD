## Description

Tarleton State University (herein referred to as Tarleton) is soliciting proposals and intends to enter into an agreement with a Vendor who can provide the best value to Tarleton by developing and maintaining a comprehensive graduation program. It is the intention of Tarleton to contract with a single company to provide these services for the Tarleton campus and its sites.
Vendor must have demonstrated competence in providing these services and must have the qualifications necessary to perform the services outlined in this RFP. The services will be provided in accordance with the terms, conditions, requirements, and specifications set forth in this RFP. This RFP provides sufficient information for interested parties to prepare and submit proposals for consideration by the University.
