## Description

The City of Tulsa Water and Sewer Department is soliciting bids to purchase an emergency generator for the North Surge Tank facility. Mandatory pre-bid conference is scheduled for 11/17/2026 at 2:00 PM CT at 3100 S 161st East Ave, Tulsa, OK 74108.

**Unmatched.** This is a straightforward equipment-purchase solicitation (an emergency backup generator) for a water utility facility. It is construction/equipment procurement, not IT services, staffing, consulting, or any other category on the approved keyword list.
