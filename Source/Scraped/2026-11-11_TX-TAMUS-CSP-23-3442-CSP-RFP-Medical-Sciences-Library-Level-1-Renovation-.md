## Description

The Texas A&M University System Office of Facilities Planning & Construction is soliciting proposals through the Competitive Sealed Proposal (CSP) process on behalf of the Board of Regents of The Texas A&M University System for Texas A&M Health in College Station, Texas. This project consists of renovating the 18,429 SF available on Level 1 to repurpose the space previously occupied by the Medical Sciences Library. The updated layout will provide 66 offices to accommodate the faculty and staff of the School of Public Health, along with several conference rooms and a large flexible meeting room to support a variety of functions. The program also addresses the department's ancillary needs and includes designated student work areas. The renovation scope includes demolition of existing interior walls and removal of the raised flooring. The HVAC will be demolished as outlined in the Program of Requirements, and plumbing will be limited to the addition of the break room.
