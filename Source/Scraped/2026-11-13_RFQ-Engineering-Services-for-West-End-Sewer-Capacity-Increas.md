## Description

Morehead City, North Carolina (the “City”), is seeking Statements of Qualifications (“SOQs”) from qualified firms to provide Engineering Services for West End Sewer Capacity Increase – Phase II. Phase I planning is underway, which increases capacity at four existing lift stations and related infrastructure with the upgrades of existing pumps and motors and additional force main construction. Phase II is for the planning, design, and construction of an additional lift station, with the intention of further mitigating head pressure on existing facilities. Morehead City has acquired property for the construction of this lift station at 4800 Country Club Rd Morehead City, NC. The intended result of the West End Sewer Capacity Increase – Phase II Project is the addition of a lift station to the current City wastewater system, located at 4800 Country Club Rd, Morehead City, NC.
