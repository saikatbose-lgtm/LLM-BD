## Description

Gwinnett County Public Schools (GCPS) Board of Education is soliciting competitive sealed proposals from qualified suppliers for Comprehensive Asset Identification, Tracking & Intelligence Solutions. The purpose of this solicitation is to establish one or more competitively awarded, national cooperative purchasing contracts for the procurement of technologies, software, and services for the identification, tracking, and intelligent management of physical assets, including identification and sensing hardware, tracking and visibility platforms, optional artificial intelligence (AI) and analytics capabilities, and related professional and managed services. Solicitation documents can be viewed through the Euna Procurement (formerly Bonfire) Interactive online portal located at www.gwinnett.bonfirehub.com or by visiting the GCPS Purchasing website at https:publish.gwinnett.k12.ga.us/gcps/home
