## Description

Gwinnett County Public Schools (GCPS) Board of Education is soliciting competitive sealed proposals from qualified suppliers for Comprehensive Asset Identification, Tracking & Intelligence Solutions. The purpose of this solicitation is to establish one or more competitively awarded, national cooperative purchasing contracts for the procurement of technologies, software, and services for the identification, tracking, and intelligent management of physical assets, including identification and sensing hardware, tracking and visibility platforms, optional artificial intelligence (AI) and analytics...

## Relevance

Gwinnett County Public Schools seeks cooperative contracts for asset identification, tracking and intelligent management technologies, software, tracking/visibility platforms, optional AI/analytics, and related professional and managed services. The asset-management software/managed-services component makes it workable under Asset Management/Information Technology, though hardware (RFID/GPS tags) is a large part of the scope.
