## Description

The Texas Education Agency (TEA) is seeking responses from qualified respondents to design, develop, implement, and operate a statewide Bond Transparency Database. This database will collect, validate, and publicly display information on school district and open-enrollment charter school bonds, maintenance taxes, capital projects, and related elections, as required by Texas Education Code §45.114 (SB 843, 89th Legislature). The solution must support secure data submission, robust reporting, and public transparency, and must be interoperable with other state financial reporting systems.
