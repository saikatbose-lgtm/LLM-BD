## Description

This project consists of finishing out an existing metal building to create a new multipurpose facility for Baconton Community Charter School. The completed building will include a gymnasium, classrooms, offices, and specialized instructional spaces for art, agriculture mechanics, and welding. The project will include the necessary interior and exterior construction, finishes, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and other building systems required to make the facility fully functional and ready for use by students and staff.
