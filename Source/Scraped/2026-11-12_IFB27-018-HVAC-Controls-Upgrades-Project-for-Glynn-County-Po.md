## Description

The Board of Commissioners of Glynn County, Georgia ('the Owner') seeks proposals from interested qualified firms to provide upgrades to the HVAC system controls located at 157 Carl Alexander Way, Glynn County Police Department. Project includes upgrades to the current systems to include implementation of the Carrier i-Vu system and all details outlined and required in the Program Services section.
