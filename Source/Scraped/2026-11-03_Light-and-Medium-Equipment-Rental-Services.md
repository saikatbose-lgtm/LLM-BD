## Description

The Contractor will provide all labor, equipment, attachments, accessories, transportation, licenses, and permits necessary to perform all services listed herein. DWR will provide necessary supervision over usage of the rented equipment. The Contractor must have an operating rental facility located within 65 road-miles of the locations in San Luis Field Division's jurisdiction.
