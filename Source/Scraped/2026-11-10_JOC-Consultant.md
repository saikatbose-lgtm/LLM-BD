## Description

The Department of General Services (DGS) is seeking proposals from qualified Offerors to create, develop, implement, and administer a Job Order Contracting (JOC) Program, and to provide construction management services. The selected Offeror shall provide an internet-based management information and support system (the “JOC System”), along with a Unit Price Book (UPB) product and technical specifications. This system must enable Commonwealth Agencies to efficiently procure and manage multi-disciplinary contracts for the design, repair, alteration and execution of minor construction projects through the issuance of Job Orders. The JOC System must support the development and issuance of Job Orders based on the pre-priced tasks contained within an online UPB. The UPB and associated technical specifications must be derived from a commercially available unit price data set and tailored to reflect commonly utilized construction activities based on Commonwealth historical data. The UPB and specifications must be maintained in an electronic format and include functionality that allows for automated search, selection, and application of unit pricing and task descriptions. The main objective of the JOC Program is to enable Commonwealth Agencies to efficiently and expeditiously procure design-build construction contractors to perform design, construction and construction-related services. There will be a Pre-Proposal Conference, Friday, November 20, 2026, at 1:00pm ET. Offerors who wish to attend shall submit their RSVP to Jamie Zarr, jzarr@pa.gov with the subject line RFP 6100066691 Pre-Proposal RSVP, no later than Friday, November 20, 2026, at 11:00am ET.
