## Description

The Town of Chatham is seeking proposals from qualified developers for the development of approximately 4-6 units of year-round permanently deed restricted affordable and attainable ownership housing on a 1-acre parcel of land owned by the Town of Chatham located at 127 Old Harbor Road. The Town intends to convey the property to an entity that will develop the property as affordable and attainable housing. All housing must be ownership housing and deed restricted for year-round residency.
