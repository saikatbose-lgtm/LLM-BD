## Description

The City of Toledo Mayor's Office of Neighborhood Safety and Engagement (MONSE) announces a funding opportunity through its Bureau of Justice Assistance grant (No. 15PBJA-24-GG-06062-CVIP) to help build Toledo’s community violence intervention pipeline. Funded organizations will partner with MONSE, Roca Inc., and frontline CVI workers to provide foundational training in CVI, primarily through the launch of a CVI Training Academy. Trainees will include microgrant awardees, young adults, credible messengers, the faith-based community, community members, and other organizations (Issue/start date taken from the source field 'Posting Date'.)
