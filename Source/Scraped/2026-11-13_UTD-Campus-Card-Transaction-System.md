## Description

UT Dallas is soliciting proposals for a campus-wide card management solution to replace its existing Comet Card platform, supporting identification, payment, access, and transaction services for students, faculty, staff, and authorized campus users. Initial term of three years with two one-year renewal options (up to five years total).
