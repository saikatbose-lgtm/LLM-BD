## Description

This is a Progressive Design-Build services procurement. JCW is seeking a statement of qualifications (SOQ) from teams/Design-Builders to provide preconstruction and design with the option of construction services for the Brush Creek Pump Station (BCPS) Improvements (“Project”), Contract No. MTM1-C080. This Request for Qualifications (RFQ) is the first phase of a three-phase procurement process for selection of a Design-Builder. Phase I shall be the Request for Qualifications (RFQ) and pre-qualifying a short list of firms to advance to Phase II. Phase II shall be the solicitation of a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a more in-depth response from Phase I shortlisted firms, and Phase III shall include an interview with each firm to present their proposed team members, qualifications, project plan, and answer questions.
