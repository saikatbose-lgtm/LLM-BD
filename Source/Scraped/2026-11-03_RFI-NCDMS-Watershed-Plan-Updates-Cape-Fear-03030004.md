## Description

RFI to solicit input from interested vendors relating to Scope of Work development for Local Watershed Plan updates in the Cape Fear 03030004 NCDMS Service Area. NCDMS is seeking information and feedback on innovative and data driven methods to evaluate current watershed conditions and identify potential functional uplift opportunities, building upon past NCDMS LWPs and other relevant watershed planning efforts within the Cape Fear River Basin, the Cape Fear 03030004 HUC8 and/or within the Middle Cape Fear/Kenneth and Parker Creek LWP planning area.
