## Description

The Texas State Auditor's Office (SAO) is seeking speakers for the SAO 2027 Audit Conference. 




Statement of Need

The purpose of this request for proposal/call for conference speakers is to solicit proposals from qualified training providers who can develop and deliver in-person conference sessions designed to support the skills and knowledge of government audit professionals. 




Intended Audience

The State Auditor's Office may provide fee-based audit training courses for state agencies (Internal Auditing Act, Government Code, Sec. 2102.012). Therefore, most of the participants are from state agencies in Texas; however, the conference is open enrollment, so the audience may be varied.




Deliverables

Interested speakers should propose 60-minute sessions. Responses should be audit-related or information technology audit-related and designed to enhance the skills and abilities of government audit professionals at all levels. Topics may include, but are not limited to: 

•	Government Operations and Accountability

•	Emerging Risks

•	Improving Audit Effectiveness and Impact

•	Audit Practices and Professional Skills

•	Fraud and Ethics

•	Cybersecurity and Resilience

•	Artificial Intelligence

•	Overcoming challenges in Audit
