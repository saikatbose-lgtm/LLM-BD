## Description

The University at Buffalo intends to issue a purchase order to one (1) energy company for the purchase of Renewable Energy Credits ("RECs"), with final ownership transferring to the University, to satisfy eligibility requirements for the 2026 reporting year. The purchase order will not include the purchase of energy, ancillary services, or transmission of electricity. Bidders must provide a firm, all-inclusive Price Per REC in $/MWh, based on an estimated quantity of 222,000 RECs. Award goes to the responsive/responsible bidder with the lowest total price under NYS Finance Law §163.
