## Description

EMPLOYEESRETIREMENT SYSTEM OF TEXAS REQUEST FORPROPOSALS FOR ACTUARIAL AUDIT SERVICES (Review ofPension Actuary) SOLICITATIONNO. 26-15352-001 NIGP CODE: 94612and 94620 The Employees Retirement System of Texas (ERS) has issued a Requestfor Proposals (RFP) seeking a qualified firm (Respondent) to provide actuarialaudit services to review the work of ERS’ pension actuary. Respondent shallprovide the level of services required in the RFP and meet other requirementsthat are in the best interests of ERS, its members, and the state of Texas(State). Respondent shall be required to execute a contract (Contract) providedby, and satisfactory to, ERS for a period that begins upon execution of theContract by ERS and the Services are anticipated to extend through May 31, 2028,subject to the terms of the Contract. The Contract Term, however, is throughJune 30, 2029, to account for the potential service of testifying before theTexas Legislature. It is anticipated the Contract will be executed in July2027. Questions will be accepted via Euna Procurement (formerly,“Bonfire”) until November 16, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. Central Time (CT) .Answers to questions will be posted to Euna Procurement and ESBD on the datespecified in RFP Section A.4.
