## Description

Walker County, Georgia is requesting proposals from consultants to conduct an intensive historic resources survey of approximately 523 parcels in the southeastern portion of unincorporated Walker County. The project will be financed in part with funds from the National Park Service, through a Historic Preservation Fund Grant, administered by the Georgia Historic Preservation Division. Proposals will be received at the office of Joe Legge, Walker County Government, 101 S Duke St., LaFayette, GA 30728 until 2:00 p.m.
