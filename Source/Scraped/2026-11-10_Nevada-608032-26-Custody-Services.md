## Description

Clark County is requesting responses from qualified and experienced financial institutions to provide comprehensive custody, safekeeping, and settlement ("CUSTODY") services for the County's investment portfolio, including asset safekeeping, trade settlement, income collection, corporate action processing, performance measurement and reporting, account administration and reconciliation, compliance support, and secure online access to account information and reporting. Responses are submitted via the Clark County Bonfire Portal. A Pre-Response Conference is scheduled for December 1, 2026.

## Relevance

Unmatched: This solicitation is for financial custodian/investment safekeeping and settlement services provided by a banking/financial institution for the County's investment portfolio. It is a financial services engagement, not an IT, staffing, consulting, or administrative/management support service. "Compliance support" and "secure online access" appear only incidentally within a financial custody context and do not constitute a genuine match to any approved IT/HR/staffing keyword or supporting NAICS code.
