## Description

The Bulloch County Board of Commissioners is seeking written proposals for a healthcare brokerage firm to provide Employee Benefit Brokerage services related to Bulloch County Employee Benefits Program. The health plan renews on 7/1/2027 and selected broker shall analyze all aspects of current plan design, spend, vendors, and make recommendations to Bulloch County for the new 2027/2028 plan year that begins on 7/1/2027. The selected broker/firm will be named as Agent of Record for the appropriate policies. The deadline for receipt of proposals is 3:00 PM, December 10, 2026.

## Relevance

Bulloch County employee-benefits insurance brokerage. HR/benefits consulting codes are listed, but the work is insurance brokerage, not HR Services/staffing.
