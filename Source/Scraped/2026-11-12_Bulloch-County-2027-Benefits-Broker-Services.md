## Description

The Bulloch County Board of Commissioners is seeking written proposals for a healthcare brokerage firm to provide Employee Benefit Brokerage services related to Bulloch County Employee Benefits Program. The health plan renews on 7/1/2027 and selected broker shall analyze all aspects of current plan design, spend, vendors, and make recommendations to Bulloch County for the new 2027/2028 plan year that begins on 7/1/2027. The selected broker/firm will be named as Agent of Record for the appropriate policies. The deadline for receipt of proposals is 3:00 PM, December 10, 2026. Proposal submissions shall include all information necessary to respond to the questions in this Request for Proposals, as well as all required documents as shown in the last three pages of this RFP.
