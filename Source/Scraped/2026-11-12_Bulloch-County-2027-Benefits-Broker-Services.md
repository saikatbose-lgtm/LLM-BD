## Description

The Bulloch County Board of Commissioners is seeking written proposals for a healthcare brokerage firm to provide Employee Benefit Brokerage services related to Bulloch County Employee Benefits Program. The health plan renews on 7/1/2027 and selected broker shall analyze all aspects of current plan design, spend, vendors, and make recommendations to Bulloch County for the new 2027/2028 plan year that begins on 7/1/2027. The selected broker/firm will be named as Agent of Record for the appropriate policies. The deadline for receipt of proposals is 3:00 PM, December 10, 2026. Proposal submissions shall include all information necessary to respond to the questions in this Request for Proposals, as well as all required documents as shown in the last three pages of this RFP. FAXED OR EMAILED PROPOSALS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED. Bidders are responsible for the actual delivery of proposals during normal business hours to Bulloch County Board of Commissioners, Attention: Peyton Fuller, Procurement Director, 115 North Main Street, Statesboro, Georgia 30458. The ORIGINAL PROPOSAL along with five (5) copies and supporting documents along with one (1) digital copy (on a USB flash drive or similar) must be submitted in a sealed clearly marked envelope. Submissions will not be publicly opened, but their receipt will be recorded as received as of the due date and time. The content of all proposals will be kept confidential until after the selection is made. A request for proposals may be requested by emailing the Bulloch County Procurement Director, at pfuller@bullochcounty.net, going to the Bulloch County website: https://bullochcounty.net/bid-opportunities/ or by going to the Georgia Procurement Registry website: https://doas.ga.gov/state-purchasing/georgia-procurement-registry-local-governments/gpr-overview.
