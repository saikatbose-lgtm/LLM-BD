## Description

Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT) is soliciting written proposals for a professional Offeror to provide a full range of solution development services for the Infrastructure and Economic Development Delivery Center (IED DC)’s Enterprise Application Security Solution (ESEC), Identity and Access Management (IAM) solution, and Privilege Access Management (PAM) solution, including: functional and technical enhancements, maintenance and support, integration with new IED DC’s applications, and migration of existing applications to new IAM platform. ESEC is PennDOT’s enterprise standard IAM solution which provides IAM functionality for all IED DC’s new web applications used by IED DC and Commonwealth employees, consultants, business partners, and the general public. There is no DBE goal established for this procurement, but Offerors are encouraged to subcontract to DBE firms. THIS SOLICITATION MUST BE VIEWED ON JAGGAER.
