## Description

The City of Laguna Beach (City) seeks a qualified website development firm (Consultant) to design, develop, and implement a modern, visually engaging, accessible, secure, and user-friendly municipal website to replace its existing website at www.lagunabeachcity.net. The existing website presents operational challenges, including a complex and outdated content management system (CMS), limited employee training resources, obsolete webpages, broken navigation links, and accessibility deficiencies.
