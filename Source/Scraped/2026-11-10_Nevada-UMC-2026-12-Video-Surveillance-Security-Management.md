## Description

University Medical Center (UMC) is soliciting proposals for a Video Surveillance and Security Management System, including hospital garage and trauma garage security plans (physical camera/security plans for Garage L1, L4, and Trauma Garage L1). A Non-Mandatory Virtual Pre-Proposal Conference and a Non-Mandatory Site Walk at UMC Hospital are scheduled. Attachments include an "UMC IT Requirements" exhibit alongside physical security plans for hospital parking garages.

## Relevance

Unmatched: The core scope of work is a physical/electronic security system — video surveillance cameras and a security management platform for hospital garages — which is a facilities/physical security systems procurement, not a professional IT services, staffing, or cybersecurity-managed-services engagement as intended by the approved keyword list. The presence of an "IT Requirements" exhibit (governing network/IT integration standards for the installed system) is incidental to the underlying hardware/physical-security scope and does not make this a genuine IT Security Management Services or Networking Services engagement under the approved filters.
