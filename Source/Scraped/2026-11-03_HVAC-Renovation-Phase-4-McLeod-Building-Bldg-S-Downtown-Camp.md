## Description

HVAC Renovations Phase 4 - Building S - Design Build Services for selective HVAC renovation of one floor and controls upgrades for another floor at CFCC's Building S - Downtown Campus, Wilmington, NC. Cape Fear Community College is seeking proposals from qualified Design Build HVAC teams to design and construct major HVAC Renovations for this building, located at CFCC’s Wilmington NC Campus. The Existing building is 7 floors, 114,000sf. Desired scope is replacement of VAV’s, hot water coils and control dampers on existing dual deck or multizone HVAC system on one floor (Floor #2) of the building. Also, replace DDC controls on existing AHU’s on the ground floor. Work to include DDC controls, hot water piping from building riser to VAV’s, grilles, outside air dampers and actuators, fire dampers and other required accessories.
