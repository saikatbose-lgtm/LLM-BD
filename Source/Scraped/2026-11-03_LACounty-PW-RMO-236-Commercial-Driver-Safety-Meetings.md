## Description

Hello -     Los Angeles County Public Works is seeking proposals for Commercial Driver Safety Meetings. After reviewing the information contained in the attachment, please submit all items requested within the Request for Proposal (RFP) by email to RJara-Pinedo@dpw.lacounty.gov or by fax to (626) 282-8312 no later than Tuesday, November 17, 2026. All bid proposals must be on company letterhead. Late and/or incomplete proposals may not be accepted. Vendors must be a registered County Vendor. You can register at http://camisvr.co.la.ca.us/LACOBids/    IMPORTANT NOTE: If you are unable to participate in this solicitation, please submit a 'No Bid' response to RJara-Pinedo@dpw.lacounty.gov.    Proposals will be examined in accordance with the goals and objectives outlined in this RFP and will be evaluated to determine if training curriculum, manuals, approach, experience, and qualifications demonstrate that the Proposer is likely to meet or exceed expectations. Vendor must clearly  demonstrate compliance with requirements:    Quality/depth of training content  Experience of trainers  Cost  Length of time in business of training and employee education  Responsiveness to each proposal requirements in this Bid Solicitation    Vendor will be disqualified if proposal is missing any of the items requested.
