## Description

Texas State Technical College (TSTC) is seeking proposals from qualified suppliers for a comprehensive, centralized software platform to support institutional effectiveness, assessment management, strategic and operational goal planning, accreditation management, faculty qualifications tracking, data analytics, and institutional reporting. The purpose of this Request for Proposals (RFP) is to select a supplier capable of providing an integrated solution that streamlines core institutional processes into a single system, eliminates redundant legacy tools, improves cross-functional collaboration, and ensures compliance with institutional and regional accreditation standards.
