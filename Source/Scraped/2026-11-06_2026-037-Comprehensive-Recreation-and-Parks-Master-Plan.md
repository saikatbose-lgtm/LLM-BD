## Description

Cherokee County, Georgia, in cooperation with the selected consultant and through research and public input will complete a Comprehensive Recreation and Parks Master Plan that assesses the current level of service (LOS) and future needs of the County over the course of the next six (6) years. The purpose of the project is to develop a community-supported plan that directly relates to the mission of the County and provides guidance to better prioritize, fund, develop and manage parks, recreation facilities, and recreation programs.
