## Description

Rockdale County seeks proposals from qualified environmental consulting firms to provide environmental review support, lead-based paint inspection and risk assessment services, clearance testing, and related environmental documentation for the County's Repair & Revitalize Program. The program will provide rehabilitation assistance to eligible owner-occupied homes using funding anticipated through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP).
