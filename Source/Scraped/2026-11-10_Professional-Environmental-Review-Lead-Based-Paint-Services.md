## Description

Rockdale County seeks proposals from qualified environmental consulting firms to provide environmental review support, lead-based paint inspection and risk assessment services, clearance testing, and related environmental documentation for the County's Repair & Revitalize Program. The program will provide rehabilitation assistance to eligible owner-occupied homes using funding anticipated through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP). The selected consultant shall provide services in accordance with the applicable CHIP award conditions, federal HOME requirements, environmental regulations, Georgia requirements governing lead-based paint activities, and other applicable federal, state, and local requirements.
