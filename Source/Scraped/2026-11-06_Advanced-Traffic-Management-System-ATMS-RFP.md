## Description

IDOT seeks to procure a system which will standardize processes and systems commonly used by IDOT District Traffic Management Centers. This system will be used to interact with traffic surveillance cameras, dynamic message boards, signs, and includes all ITS requirements for IDOT. The system will also be used to collect and distribute data on traffic incidents, lane closures, and many other traffic related issues for over 16,000 state highway miles.
