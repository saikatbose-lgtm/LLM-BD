## Description

Rockdale County is soliciting proposals from qualified architectural and engineering firms to provide professional services on an as-needed basis for the County's Repair & Revitalize Program. The program is intended to assist eligible owner-occupied households with housing rehabilitation activities that address substandard housing conditions, health and safety concerns, structural deficiencies, accessibility needs, and applicable building code requirements.
