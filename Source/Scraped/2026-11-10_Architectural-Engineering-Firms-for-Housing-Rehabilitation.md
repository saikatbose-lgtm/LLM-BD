## Description

Rockdale County is soliciting proposals from qualified architectural and engineering firms to provide professional services on an as-needed basis for the County¿s Repair & Revitalize Program. The program is intended to assist eligible owner-occupied households with housing rehabilitation activities that address substandard housing conditions, health and safety concerns, structural deficiencies, accessibility needs, and applicable building code requirements. The County anticipates applying for funding through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP). Services provided under this solicitation will be subject to available funding, applicable CHIP requirements, and written authorization by the County. The selected firm will provide professional evaluations, repair drawings, technical specifications, engineering calculations, and other architectural or engineering services when necessary to support eligible housing rehabilitation projects.
