## Description

Rockdale County is soliciting proposals from qualified architectural and engineering firms to provide professional services on an as-needed basis for the County¿s Repair & Revitalize Program. The County anticipates applying for funding through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs (DCA) Community HOME Investment Program (CHIP).
