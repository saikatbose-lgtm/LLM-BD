## Description

The City of Toledo Mayor's Office of Neighborhood Safety and Engagement (MONSE) announces a funding opportunity through its Bureau of Justice Assistance grant (No. 15PBJA-24-GG-06062-CVIP) to serve as an Umbrella Organization for Toledo's community violence intervention (CVI) ecosystem. This role will provide the administrative, fiscal, and coordination functions that small CVI organizations cannot carry alone so that frontline workers can focus on outreach, mediation, and relationship-building.
